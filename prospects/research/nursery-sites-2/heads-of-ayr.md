## At a glance
- **Site:** https://www.headsofayrnursery.co.uk/
- **Who runs it:**
  - Heads of Ayr Farm Park, a family-run, award-winning rural attraction on Dunure Road, Alloway by Ayr KA7 4LD.
  - The nursery opened in March 2021 in a purpose-built timber building (designed 2019). It has 57 places for ages 2 to 5 and is open 8am to 6pm Monday to Friday.
  - There are two playrooms: the **Sunshine Room** (2–3, 1:5) and the **Rainbow Room** (3–5, 1:8). The garden has treehouses, tunnels, a mini stream and farm animals next door.
  - It holds UNICEF Rights Respecting Schools and daynurseries "Top 20" badges for 2023 to 2026, links its Care Inspectorate registration, and charges £73 a full day.
  - Built on **Wix**. Fonts: Chelsea Market (display), Futura and Avenir (body).
- **Overall feel:**
  - **The softest and most "storybook" of my sites.** A **watercolour illustration header** (children running with goats through a meadow), **torn-paper edges** between sections, a **paint-speckle texture**, blob and oval photo masks, pastel room colours and tiny hand-drawn doodles (sun, rainbow, welly boots, butterfly, bee).
  - Colourful in a gentle, earthy way rather than loud.
  - Very little text: **165 words on the homepage**.

## Why it feels colourful and fun
- **Palette (computed):**
  - Cream `#EFEBC4` (main ground), olive `#BFBA83` (bands, tabs), sage `#7F975F`, deep green `#59744B` (buttons, headings).
  - Mint `#C5E8B3` / `#D7F4C9` (cards, Rainbow Room), pale lemon (Sunshine Room).
  - Headings in a muted grey-green, text `#403E2C`.
  - Accents come from the **illustration and photos**: the red tractor, the pink and blue puddle suits, the blue sky.
  - It is a **low-saturation, outdoorsy palette** where colour arrives through pictures and doodles rather than flat bands.
- **Shapes:**
  - **Torn-paper edges** on every section join (image masks).
  - **Organic photo masks**: an oval, a blob, a rounded "egg".
  - Square-cornered tab buttons in olive.
  - Small pill buttons with a 50px radius.
  - A 24px radius on cards.
  - **A cloud-shaped logo.**
- **Illustration:**
  - The **watercolour hero** (children and goats in a meadow, a tree).
  - **Doodle icons** per room: a smiling sun for the Sunshine Room, a rainbow for the Rainbow Room, yellow wellies for the Garden.
  - A butterfly and a bee beside the headings, a grass tuft in the footer.
  - On Education, **line icons in olive**: a leaf, a paw print, holding hands.
  - The children in the watercolour are illustrated, not real, so it gives a "children" feeling without photos of real children.
- **Texture:** a fine red and grey **paint-speckle pattern** over the white sections.
- **Photography:**
  - Natural light, rural.
  - Farm animals (alpacas, goats, ponies, a stag statue), the red tractor, timber buildings, empty wooden rooms with birch-trunk features and a sky-painted wall, food plated by the chef.
  - Children mostly in bright puddle suits, often **from behind or at a distance** (four children at a fence looking at alpacas, on the Education hero).
  - Some close-up faces too.
- **Type:**
  - **Chelsea Market** (a chunky, playful hand-lettered display face) for headings at 40 to 56px, in sage or grey-green.
  - Futura Book 16px/28.8px for body; Avenir Light in places.
  - Light, airy and a bit low in contrast.
- **Motion:**
  - A background **video** in the homepage rectangle (desktop only).
  - Wix fade-ins on scroll.
  - Slide transitions between pages.
  - The site CSS includes a `prefers-reduced-motion` rule.
- **Spacing:** generous, with centred text in narrow measures and lots of cream space.

## How it keeps text light
- **The homepage is 165 words:** a welcome paragraph, a "who are we" card and a quick-links paragraph. Everything else is pushed to inner pages through **four big photo tiles** with one-word labels.
- **About ends with "Quick Links"**, six labelled buttons (Meet Our Staff, Education, Fees, Virtual Tour, Documents, Book a Visit).
- **Rooms page:** each room is one colour block, one photo and one paragraph, with the age and ratio in the paragraph and a doodle at the bottom.
- **Education:** a hero with one sentence, then three pastel columns (Outdoor Adventures, Animal Magic, Indoor Play) with an icon each, then a row of 5 photos, then two more link panels (Our Rooms: "2-3 year olds are in the Sunshine Room / 3-5 year olds are in the Rainbow Room"; Mission Statement and Nursery News).
- **Fees:** one card over a room photo: "£73 / Full Day: 8am – 6pm", half days £42, booking fee terms. Then a cream strip, "We now have 1140 funded hours available as part of our partnership with South Ayrshire Council", and a bulleted "What is included?" list.
- **Weak points:** testimonials are 674 words on one page, and the room paragraphs are centred, 60 to 100 words each.

## Gallery

- **Layout:** Wix Pro Gallery in a **collage / mosaic** (rows of mixed sizes: one large tile beside two or three small ones), full width, 44 photos on one long page (5,560px desktop, 5,900px phone). No pagination, no filters, no captions. On a phone it stays a mosaic in 2 to 3 columns.

## Phone experience
- **Works:**
  - The watercolour fills the first screen with a mood.
  - A phone button and a menu button.
  - Big Chelsea Market headings.
  - The video is replaced with a still.
  - Pages are short.
  - Photo tiles with big labels.
- **Doesn't:**
  - Low-contrast sage-on-cream headings and the light Futura body text.
  - The first screen is all illustration, so the welcome only starts below it.
  - The menu table scrolls sideways.
  - The menu is hidden behind "+", with no visible "Book a tour" until you scroll.
  - The Wix scripts weigh more than the content (174 requests for a 165-word page).

## Borrow this
1. **A painted or drawn header scene.** A watercolour or flat illustration of the nursery's own setting (its garden, its hills, its animals or toys) across the top of every page. It brings children and play into the design **without photographing any child**. Commission it, or build a simple layered SVG landscape (hills, sun, trees) in the brand palette. Export a compressed WebP/AVIF with a fixed height, or SVG.
2. **Torn-paper or hand-cut section edges** as a small SVG mask, to make the page feel like a scrapbook.
3. **A scrapbook collage block:** mixed shapes (oval, blob, rectangle) of building, garden and animal photos around one short "who we are" card.
4. **A doodle icon per room** (sun, rainbow, wellies) in that room's pastel colour, used on the room block, the timetable and the fees row.
5. **Photo tiles with one-word labels as the main navigation** on the homepage (Rooms · Food · Fees · Visit).
6. **Price on a card over a room photo**, plus a strip for funded hours (only with confirmed facts; "phone to check" otherwise).
7. **Team cards with first name, role and qualifications**, in pastel blocks, with real staff photos taken with consent.
8. **Gallery, our way:** the Heads of Ayr mosaic, but only the **no-child and from-behind photos** (rooms, garden, animals, food, artwork, hands, children from behind at a distance).
   - A CSS grid mosaic with `grid-auto-flow: dense` and a few `span 2` tiles.
   - Descriptive alt text on every photo.
   - `loading="lazy"`, `srcset`, WebP.
   - Optional category chips (Rooms · Garden · Food · Art) as real buttons.
   - Either no lightbox, or a native `<dialog>` with focus management and Escape.
9. **Video, our way: the "living photo".** A small inline window (not a full hero) in the collage:
   - 6 to 10 seconds, self-hosted, 720p, under 1.5 MB, of the garden stream, a tractor, an empty room or hands planting seeds.
   - `muted playsinline loop` with a real `poster`.
   - Only autoplay when `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; a visible pause button.
   - **Phones get the poster only**, as Wix does.
   - Never 80 seconds or 12 MB for decoration.
10. **A promised virtual tour, done:** a 60 to 90 second self-hosted, captioned walk through the empty rooms and garden, poster-first, click to play, sound only on request.

## Avoid this
- Zero alt text across 44 gallery photos.
- A "virtual tour" page that says "coming soon".
- An 80-second, 12 MB decorative loop on desktop with no poster.
- Low-contrast sage headings and light grey body text on cream.
- A sideways-scrolling menu table on phones. Use a day-by-day list instead.
- A first phone screen with no visible action. Put "Book a visit" and "Call" on the first screen.
- Wix's script weight and third-party map.
