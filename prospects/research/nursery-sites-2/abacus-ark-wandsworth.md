## How it keeps text light
- The first screen has **14 words**, all headline.
- The **fact strip** gives phone, email, manager, hours and reviews in one navy bar with no sentences.
- **Each feature block is one heading plus 40 to 70 words** next to a big circle photo, and blocks alternate sides, so the eye zigzags. The 22px body text means few words per line (about 10 to 12).
- **One-line claims in pill banners** break up the run.
- **The age cards** are pictures plus labels only, and they navigate to the programme pages.
- The weak spot: the three **SEO blocks near the end** (430 words of "nursery schools in Wandsworth" keyword text), which no parent needs.

## Borrow this
1. **One theme carried through everything:** the logo shape becomes a repeating motif (boats on the wave), the copy echoes it ("Setting sail"), and the room names follow it. For a Scottish nursery the theme could be hills, a river, birds or a local landmark, drawn for the client.
2. **Long, soft wave dividers with a thin crest line** between flat colour bands (two SVG paths, one for the fill and one 3px lighter line).
3. **Circle photos with thick coloured rings** that rotate through the palette, for room, garden and food photos.
4. **The empty-room location page:** every feature block illustrated with a wide, bright photo of an empty room or outdoor space. It fully respects our no-children rule and still looks lively, because the rooms are colourful.
5. **A fact strip** under the hero: phone, email, manager's name, hours, rating link, each with an icon. It replaces a paragraph.
6. **Playful room names on age cards** ("Home From Home", "Little Explorers"), only if they are the nursery's real room names. The portrait slot would show a **room photo or an illustration**, not a child.
7. **A large, readable body size** (20 to 22px desktop, 18px phone, line height 1.5) with a chunky rounded display face for headings. Both self-hosted, as Abacus does.
8. **A multicolour headline:** 2 to 3 key words in different palette colours, but only at 40px or more and with each colour passing 3:1 for large text on its background.
9. **A one-time hand-drawn underline** under a key phrase, animated once on load and static under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
10. **Gallery and video under our rules:** a "Have a look around" strip of 6 ringed circle photos (rooms, garden, lunch table) that opens an accessible lightbox or links to a gallery page grouped by room. If a video is added, use a 30-second self-hosted walk through the empty rooms, with a poster, captions and `preload="none"`.

## Avoid this
- **Keyword SEO blocks** and keyword-stuffed alt text.
- **No fees at all**, only "Get Pricing Details".
- **The third-party review widget showing an AI-written review verbatim** ("Certainly! Here's the updated version including Marufat: …"). Unvetted widgets publish whatever comes in.
- GTM, Microsoft Clarity, three Facebook pixels, Elfsight and Google Maps. None of these are allowed for us.
- Low-contrast colour text: peach `#fab078` links on white (about 2:1), and pale aqua `#d0eced` on aqua `#69c8c4`.
- Studio portraits of children (they look like stock) on the age cards.
- A top bar and header that take a third of the first phone screen.
