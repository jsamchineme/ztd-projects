# Engaging nursery websites: selling patterns and five new template directions

For template designers and AI build workers. Written 13 October 2026.

This is the companion to `nursery-websites.md` (the first brief). That brief made our first three nursery templates calm and information-first: Front Desk Nursery (`uk-nursery`), Key Worker (`uk-people-nursery`) and Garden Gate (`uk-inside-nursery`). The owner now wants templates that are **more fun and more engaging**, and that **sell the nursery to parents emotionally**, while still giving every fact a parent needs.

**What doesn't change.** Every rule in the first brief still holds:
- never invent facts (fees, places, grades, reviews, awards, staff, menus, ratios, apps);
- no identifiable children, and no children's names, in our builds;
- no stock or AI-generated people;
- no carousels, pop-ups, chat bubbles, third-party widgets, embeds or trackers;
- self-hosted fonts and no CDNs;
- WCAG 2.2 AA, with `audit.mjs` at 0 blockers, 0 major and 0 minor, and clean `viewports.mjs` layouts;
- plain English at a reading age of 9 to 11.

So the fun has to come from **colour, type, shape, illustration, texture, motion, copy voice, children's own artwork, and well-chosen photos** of rooms, gardens, hands, food, kit, staff and children from behind.

**Sources.** Four researchers studied 20 UK nursery websites on 13 October 2026, with whole-site crawls and phone and desktop screenshots. Each point below cites the study file in `prospects/research/nursery-sites/` by name, for example `young-friends-kindergarten.md`. "First brief §6.2" means `nursery-websites.md` section 6.2. I also looked at the screenshots myself: the homepages of all 20 sites and the shots the summaries call distinctive. Those screenshots live in the session scratchpad and aren't kept; the four `SUMMARY.md` files list their paths.

**The sites, in four groups:**
- Premium design-led groups: `n-family-club.md`, `kido.md`, `littleforestfolk.md`, `storal.md`, `bright-horizons-uk.md`.
- Large playful chains: `busy-bees.md`, `kids-planet.md`, `monkey-puzzle.md`, `tops-day-nurseries.md`, `childbase-partnership.md`.
- Scottish nurseries and groups: `kirktonholme.md`, `thrive.md`, `links-city-nurseries.md`, `willowden.md`, `banbury-cross.md`.
- Award winners and distinctive sites: `young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `bright-beginnings-leeds.md`, `kido-schools.md`, `fennies.md`, `partou-uk.md`.

**The best-designed site is Young Friends Kindergarten** (Hove, NDNA Nursery of the Year 2026). It is a single independent nursery, the same shape as our prospects, and it is fun and premium at once. Several directions below borrow from it.

## 1. What the best sites do to sell

### 1.1 They sell a feeling first, then prove it with facts

The best sites open with how the parent will feel, not with what the nursery is. Then they put a hard fact straight underneath.

- **Feeling, then fact.** Busy Bees' hero reads "Smoother mornings. Easier goodbyes. They're exactly where they should be." Then a location search [`busy-bees.md`]. It talks about the drop-off, which is the moment parents worry about most.
- **Start with the parent's worry.** "We know choosing a nursery is one of the biggest decisions you'll make as a parent" comes before Busy Bees' numbers [`busy-bees.md`]. Thrive's settling-in page does the same: "We know it's a big step" [`thrive.md`].
- **One conviction, shown everywhere.** Young Friends turns one real belief (plant-based, outdoors, child-led) into the H1, the colour cards, the food page and the fees page. The values are the product. "Nothing here is tokenistic or staged" [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`]. Tops does the same with climate and food [`tops-day-nurseries.md`]. Little Forest Folk does it with the outdoors [`littleforestfolk.md`].
- **The childhood parents remember.** Little Forest Folk sells "the wonderful outdoors childhood that many of us were fortunate enough to have experienced" [`littleforestfolk.md`]. Bright Horizons: "This is more than childcare. This is childhood" [`bright-horizons-uk.md`].
- **Home from home** is the commonest promise in Scotland (Banbury Cross, Links, Kirktonholme, Thrive) [`banbury-cross.md`, `links-city-nurseries.md`, `kirktonholme.md`, `thrive.md`]. It's warm but generic. It only works with specific details next to it: "plenty of cuddles throughout the day", "treasure baskets", "walks in the buggies", "a decked area in the garden" [`kirktonholme.md`, `banbury-cross.md`].
- **A small piece of magic.** Willow Den's "hidden garden through our 'secret gate'" is the most charming line in the Scottish sample [`willowden.md`]. Every nursery has one thing like this: a gate, a den, a tree, a window seat.
- **A story or growth metaphor that runs through the site.** Storal's roots and chapters ("Where wholesome roots grow remarkable futures", "Begin their story") [`storal.md`]. Kids Planet: "Where curiosity takes root" [`kids-planet.md`].
- **Concrete beats abstract.** A named cook and real dishes ("Our fantastic cook Agata... Thai green curry, fish pie and chicken fajitas") does more than "nutritious meals" [`kirktonholme.md`]. Parents' own words show this too: "she got to ring the bell", "Beautiful red stone buildings!" [`links-city-nurseries.md`].

**For us:** each template needs one emotional promise of 8 words or fewer, written about the parent's day or the child's day. A plain fact sentence goes straight under it (ages, hours, weeks, area). The conviction must be one the nursery really holds and can show.

### 1.2 How they structure the homepage

The groups lead with a nursery finder. That is irrelevant to a single nursery [`partou-uk.md`, `kido.md`]. The single nurseries and the best brand pages share this order:

1. **Promise and one action** in the hero. "Book a visit" sits in the header too [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `busy-bees.md`, `links-city-nurseries.md`].
2. **A short "why us" block of 3 to 6 cards**, each with one sentence and one link: Young Friends' six colour cards [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`], Kids Planet's three paper icons [`kids-planet.md`], Storal's three pillars [`storal.md`], Links' four icon tiles [`links-city-nurseries.md`], Tops' five sticker cards [`tops-day-nurseries.md`].
3. **Proof**: awards, the inspection, reviews [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `kids-planet.md`].
4. **An invitation to visit**, written as a promise of what you'll see: "You'll see children who are engaged, calm and deeply involved in meaningful play" [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`].
5. **Parents' words**, then the footer with the visit prompt again.

Banbury Cross shows that a very short site (684 words) can sell well with an award, three parent quotes and colour-coded room chapters. It also shows the cost: no fees, no funding and no grades [`banbury-cross.md`]. **The gap in the Scottish sample is a site that sells like Young Friends and informs like our Front Desk.** That is what the new templates must be.

### 1.4 Proof, and which proof our nurseries can honestly use

Almost every big site leans on a daynurseries.co.uk widget and a group-wide score [`n-family-club.md`, `kids-planet.md`, `monkey-puzzle.md`, `storal.md`, `childbase-partnership.md`]. Our small Scottish nurseries can't, and shouldn't. Here is what they can use, strongest first.

**1. Care Inspectorate grades, shown whole.**
- Every grade from the latest report, with its exact headings, the report date and a link to the service record (first brief §3.2, §4).
- Bright Horizons shows the Care Inspectorate grade with a "Download report" link on its Edinburgh nursery page. It's the only group that does [`bright-horizons-uk.md`].
- Childbase is honest about the change in Ofsted's wording [`childbase-partnership.md`]. We do the same for the Care Inspectorate's new framework (first brief §3.2).
- Never show only the best grade. Never use the Care Inspectorate logo. Never write "mostly 5s and 6s" without dates [`kirktonholme.md`].

**2. Quotes from the inspection report.**
- The most borrowable idea in the Scottish sample: Links and City use the inspector's words as their testimonial ("Children experienced a sense of belonging and nurturing support - Care Inspectorate") [`links-city-nurseries.md`].
- Storal puts short report phrases in a chip on each nursery card [`storal.md`]. Partou prints "inspection highlights" with the date [`partou-uk.md`]. Kido has "What Ofsted says" [`kido.md`].
- **Our rule:** copy the words exactly from the nursery's latest report, give the month and year, and link the report with the grades next to it. Never trim a sentence so it changes meaning. A quote is never a substitute for the grades.

**3. The nursery's own published reviews.**
- Google reviews the nursery can link to, quoted as text (no widget). Give the source, the date and a first name and initial, with the nursery's permission [`partou-uk.md`, `busy-bees.md`].
- Labelling a quote by room and date reads as more real than a name: "Parent of Hedgehog child, April 2026" [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`].
- Show 3 short quotes at most. Long testimonial pages don't get read [`littleforestfolk.md`].
- Never filter silently. Kids Planet at least says "Displaying our 4 & 5 star reviews" [`kids-planet.md`].

**4. daynurseries.co.uk, only with the score, the count and the date.**
- Write it as "9.6 from 41 reviews on daynurseries.co.uk, 13 October 2026", with a link. Use the nursery's own score, never a group score.
- Kids Planet's "9.5" disc covers 13,254 reviews across many nurseries, so a parent can't tell whose score it is [`kids-planet.md`]. Bright Horizons shows a 2025 score as current [`bright-horizons-uk.md`]. Don't do either.
- No widget: it loads a third-party script and shows other nurseries' reviews [`n-family-club.md`, `storal.md`].

**5. Awards they have really won.**
- Give the award name, the awarding body and the year, and say "shortlisted" or "finalist" when that's what it was [`tops-day-nurseries.md`].
- Banbury Cross (NDNA Nursery of the Year Scotland 2026) and Young Friends tell the story behind the award and credit staff and families [`banbury-cross.md`, `young-friends-kindergarten.md`]. Young Friends gives the judges' comments their own page.
- Use one award object, drawn well (the Bright Beginnings rosette is the centrepiece of its home page) [`bright-beginnings-leeds.md`]. Don't stack badges: Storal's four badges take about 1,000px on a phone [`storal.md`].
- Use an award body's badge only where its rules allow. Otherwise set the award in our own type.

**6. Other honest proof that costs nothing.** Each one only if the nursery confirms it:
- years open and the founder's real story [`busy-bees.md`, `kido.md`, `banbury-cross.md`];
- staff tenure ("Manager for 10 years") [`childbase-partnership.md`, `kirktonholme.md`];
- SSSC registration and PVG checks, named in plain English [`banbury-cross.md`, `links-city-nurseries.md`];
- funded-provider status with the council's name [`willowden.md`];
- the Care Inspectorate number in the footer [`willowden.md`, `banbury-cross.md`];
- Eco-Schools Scotland Green Flag, where held [`thrive.md`, `littleforestfolk.md`];
- the Food Hygiene Information Scheme result. Scotland's scheme gives "Pass" or "Improvement required", not a 0 to 5 score; Thrive's "Food Hygiene 5" is the English scheme [`thrive.md`].

**Not usable by us:**
- Ofsted wording ("Outstanding") [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`];
- celebrity quotes with no direct link to the nursery (Little Forest Folk's Attenborough quote) [`littleforestfolk.md`];
- group league tables [`childbase-partnership.md`];
- animated stat counters, which showed nonsense numbers mid-count [`fennies.md`];
- trust logos on a busy hero photo [`fennies.md`, `banbury-cross.md`].

### 1.5 Conversion: the visit flow

**What the best do:**
- **A visit button is always visible.** It's in the header on every page, and repeated in the hero, mid-page and footer [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `busy-bees.md`, `thrive.md`].
- **A phone button sits next to it on a phone.** Tops and Links put call icons in the mobile header [`tops-day-nurseries.md`, `links-city-nurseries.md`]. Banbury has no header action at all, and loses parents [`banbury-cross.md`].
- **"How visits work" in three steps.** Young Friends: tell us about your child, we arrange a time and send a short introduction, then a short low-key visit and time to ask questions [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`].
- **Honest expectations.** Kids Planet's form opens with "01 Request received: your tour isn't booked just yet" [`kids-planet.md`]. Thrive: "Is there any obligation? None at all." [`thrive.md`]. N Family Club: "no payment and no commitment", and a choice of tour or phone call [`n-family-club.md`].
- **"What to expect on a visit."** Partou covers ringing the bell, showing ID, a list of questions to ask, and a short survey afterwards [`partou-uk.md`]. Thrive covers the rooms, the outdoor area, meeting the team, the routine and your questions [`thrive.md`]. Young Friends: "a setting that hasn't been tidied up or staged for your arrival" [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`].
- **A named, cosy place to start.** Willow Den invites parents to "our welcome cabin" to meet the manager, by name [`willowden.md`].
- **Pick a time yourself.** Kido and Links embed booking calendars [`kido-schools.md`, `links-city-nurseries.md`]. We can't use a third-party widget. We can print the nursery's real visit times ("Tuesdays and Thursdays, 10am or 2pm") with a phone link.
- **Low-pressure events.** Open mornings and stay-and-play sessions with "save my spot" [`fennies.md`, `storal.md`, `thrive.md`].

**What to avoid:**
- 14-field forms that ask for an address [`storal.md`];
- forms that ask for the child's gender and surname before the parent has seen a room [`kids-planet.md`];
- forms on another domain with no phone number nearby [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `banbury-cross.md`].

The first brief's rule stands (§6.3): default to "Phone or email us". Add a form only when the nursery has a compliant way to receive it, and ask only for the minimum.

### 1.6 Waiting-list honesty

Scarcity sells when it's true and explained. Little Forest Folk is the model:
- a "Limited availability across all locations for Autumn 2026" bar;
- intakes in January, April and September;
- an aim of 12 to 24 months, and "we can never make a guarantee";
- "please wait until your new arrival has arrived safely" before joining [`littleforestfolk.md`].

Others do parts of it well:
- N Family Club publishes its priority order (current children, siblings, transfers, then date of registration), its fee rules and its minimum days [`n-family-club.md`].
- Willow Den states funded-place eligibility by date of birth ("Born 1 March 2023 to 29 February 2024: funded place in 2026/27") [`willowden.md`].
- Thrive: "Places fill quickly, so we recommend visiting early. You're welcome to visit before your baby is born." [`thrive.md`].
- Bright Horizons shows a dated "Limited availability for 2026/2027" notice [`bright-horizons-uk.md`].

**For us:** a "How places are offered" block with the nursery's own rules: intake months, priority order, minimum sessions, any fee, and how long the wait usually is. Write "phone to check" for anything the nursery hasn't told us. Any "limited places" notice needs a season and a show-until date (the `data-show-until` pattern). Never use it as standing marketing.

### 1.7 Food

Food is the most underused selling point, and Scotland gives every nursery a head start: funded providers must give a free meal at every funded session, following *Setting the Table* (first brief §3.1).

- **Name the cook and the dishes** [`kirktonholme.md`]. Tops named its chef (Nursery Chef of the Year) and gave "Our famous menus" its own page [`tops-day-nurseries.md`].
- **Print the weekly menu on the page**, with the vegetarian option [`links-city-nurseries.md`].
- **Say how food is served**, not just what it is. "Mealtimes are calm, social and unhurried... Food is never rushed, pressured or used as reward." Children serve themselves; staff sit with them [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`].
- **Food as learning:** kitchen garden, seed to plate, cooking with the children [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `tops-day-nurseries.md`].
- **A named nutrition adviser**, only if real [`monkey-puzzle.md`].
- **Recipes or "try this at home"** shot as flat lays of food and hands, with no children [`n-family-club.md`].
- **What's included** in the fee: meals, snacks, milk [`bright-horizons-uk.md`, `kids-planet.md`].

### 1.8 Settling in

This is the emotional centre. Kirktonholme's parent reviews are almost all about it [`kirktonholme.md`].

- **Write to the parent's feelings.** Thrive: "Sneaking off without saying goodbye can leave some children anxious... Letting your child see you smile during handover" [`thrive.md`].
- **Make a promise, not a policy.** Busy Bees: settling in at no charge, for as long as needed [`busy-bees.md`]. Kirktonholme: stay-and-play sessions that "could take days, weeks or even months" [`kirktonholme.md`].
- **Show the steps:** first visit, a short stay with you there, a longer stay, the first full session. Use simple icons and room photos, not children [`busy-bees.md`, `thrive.md`].
- **Say who does it:** the key person and a "buddy" for cover [`busy-bees.md`].
- **Put one parent quote about settling next to the steps** [`kirktonholme.md`].

### 1.9 Rooms named by the nursery

Named rooms turn "0 to 5" into a journey a child moves along:
- insects at Bright Beginnings (Caterpillars, Butterflies, Ladybirds, Grasshoppers), each with a hand-drawn creature [`bright-beginnings-leeds.md`];
- Scottish plants at Kirktonholme (Fern, Thistle, Heather) [`kirktonholme.md`];
- animals at Links (Hedgehogs, Squirrels, Foxes) [`links-city-nurseries.md`];
- stage names at Fennies and Kido (Explorers to Inventors) [`fennies.md`, `kido.md`].

Banbury makes each age group a full-width chapter in its own colour, and repeats the colour in a tab bar [`banbury-cross.md`]. Room cards should show the age range, and places and ratio only when the nursery gives them [`bright-beginnings-leeds.md`]. **Never invent room names.** If a nursery has none, use the age bands.

### 1.10 The parent app

The app is sold as a window, not a feature:
- "A little window into their world" [`busy-bees.md`];
- a daily unposed photo [`littleforestfolk.md`];
- the app login as a header button for current parents [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`];
- Famly, ParentZone and Tapestry all appear in the sample [`kids-planet.md`, `thrive.md`, `kirktonholme.md`].

**For us:** "We use [app] to share your child's day: photos, meals, naps and notes from their key person", only when confirmed. The login link goes in the Parents area and the footer, never in the main action slot.

### 1.11 Staff

- The manager's bio with one human line (a mum of four who cooks outdoors every weekend) [`willowden.md`].
- Management bios with hobbies, dogs and baking [`n-family-club.md`].
- A fun-fact Q&A per staff member [`fennies.md`].
- Qualification levels listed (Level 6, Level 3) [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`].
- The manager's name, photo and "Meet the team" link placed just before the address card [`bright-horizons-uk.md`].
- Years of service as proof [`childbase-partnership.md`].

All of this needs staff consent. Where there's no consented photo, use a drawn initial or role badge (the first brief's Key Worker rules).

### 1.12 Ten patterns to borrow, ranked

1. **A feeling-first promise, then a plain fact** [`busy-bees.md`].
2. **One true conviction, carried everywhere** [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `tops-day-nurseries.md`].
3. **A colour-card "why us" stack** of 3 to 6 short cards [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `n-family-club.md`].
4. **The inspector's own words**, dated and linked, beside the full grades [`links-city-nurseries.md`, `storal.md`, `partou-uk.md`].
5. **"How visits work" in three honest steps**, plus "what to expect" and "no obligation" [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`, `kids-planet.md`, `thrive.md`, `partou-uk.md`].
6. **Waiting-list rules published plainly**, with a dated availability notice only when true [`littleforestfolk.md`, `n-family-club.md`].
7. **Food made a headline act**: the named cook, the week's menu, how meals are served [`kirktonholme.md`, `links-city-nurseries.md`, `young-friends-kindergarten.md`].
8. **Settling in sold as a promise with steps** [`thrive.md`, `busy-bees.md`].
9. **Named rooms as colour chapters** with drawn creatures or plants [`bright-beginnings-leeds.md`, `banbury-cross.md`, `kirktonholme.md`].
10. **A visible "Book a visit" and "Phone" on every phone screen** [`links-city-nurseries.md`, `tops-day-nurseries.md`, `busy-bees.md`].

## 2. Blueprint for an engaging nursery site

This keeps every parent task from the first brief (§2, §6). The facts stay; the order and the dressing change so that they sell.

### 2.1 Page set (menu of 6, never more than 7)

| Menu item | Page | Sells by | Holds (first brief tasks) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | `index.html` | Promise, proof, invitation | See 2.2 |
| Visit and join | `join-us.html` | Lowering fear | How visits work (3 steps), what to expect, visit times, questions to ask, how places are offered, waiting list, settling in, what to bring, starting dates |
| Our nursery | `our-nursery.html` | Showing the day | A day here, named rooms by age, outdoors, food (menu, cook, allergies), sleep, additional support |
| Fees and funding | `fees-and-funding.html` | Making the price feel earned | Fee table with "Fees from [date]", what's included, funded hours (1,140, the council, start dates, eligible 2s), extras (optional only for funded hours), Tax-Free Childcare and Universal Credit links |
| Our team | `our-team.html` | Trust in people | Manager's welcome, room teams, qualifications, SSSC (as supplied), key person system |
| Parents | `parents.html` | Belonging, for current families | Closures and term dates, this week's menu, forms (HTML), paying fees, app login, policies |

- **Big Table (direction 5) may add "Food" as a 7th item.** No other template goes past six.
- The **footer** keeps: jobs, policies, privacy notice, accessibility statement, complaints, Care Inspectorate record, CS number and "Page last reviewed" (first brief §6.1).
- **Proof gets no menu item.** It lives on Home and Our nursery, with a link to the report. Optional `inspection.html` or `awards.html` pages, for a nursery with a real award or a long report it wants to quote, are reached from links, not the menu.

### 2.2 Homepage order on a 390px phone

The first screen still answers "Is this for us?" (first brief §6.2). The difference is that it says it with feeling, and the sections after it are ordered like a story: promise, proof, the day, the people, the price, the invitation.

| # | Section | Must hold | Sells with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Header** | Name or logo, "Phone" button, "Menu" button. Desktop adds "Book a visit" | The template's signature mark (sticker, dot, emblem) |
| 2 | **Hero** | `h1` with the nursery name and the promise together: `<h1><span class="eyebrow">Little Beehive Nursery, Cupar</span> Muddy knees, full tummies, happy goodbyes.</h1>`. Then the fact sentence: "For children from 6 months to 5 in Cupar. Open 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, all year." | Display type, the theme's illustration or texture. No text on a busy photo |
| 3 | **Key facts strip** | Ages, hours, funded provider, places ("phone to check"), grades with the date. Compact chips, 2 columns | Chips styled in the theme (stickers, colour pills, luggage tags) |
| 4 | **Two actions** | "Book a visit" (main) and "Join the waiting list" or "Phone us" | Full-width, 48px+, the theme's button shape |
|  | *First-screen check* | Items 1 to 4 fit in 844px at 390px wide. Budget: header 56, eyebrow 20, promise up to 3 lines, fact sentence up to 3 lines, strip about 150, buttons 112 | |
| 5 | **Already with us?** | One row of 4 links: closures, menu, forms, app or pay | A slim band, so current parents don't scroll through the pitch |
| 6 | **Proof ribbon** | Every grade, date and report link; one exact inspector quote; one real award or review score, if any | The template's proof device (pinned note, number tile, storybook page, badge) |
| 7 | **Why parents choose us** | 3 to 6 cards, each a real distinctive with one sentence and a link | Colour cards, stickers or drawn icons |
| 8 | **A day here** | Timeline from arrival to home time, with times only if supplied | Drawn icons or a photo of the room or table |
| 9 | **Our rooms** | Named rooms with age ranges (places and ratio if given) | Colour chapters, creatures or plants for room names the nursery uses |
| 10 | **Settling in** | 3 to 5 steps, the key person, a quote about settling | Step markers in the theme |
| 11 | **Food** | Cook's first name (with consent), today's or this week's menu, how meals are served, allergies line | Menu card, plate photo |
| 12 | **Outdoors** | What the space is and how often children go out (as confirmed) | Garden photo, kit-on-pegs photo, drawn map |
| 13 | **Our team** | Manager's welcome with a human line, link to the team | Portrait (consented) or a drawn badge |
| 14 | **Parents say** | Up to 3 short quotes with source and date | Quote style in the theme. No slider |
| 15 | **Fees and funding** | "From £[x] a day, fees from [date]" or "Phone us for our current fees"; what's included; funded hours in one line; link | A "what's included" list with drawn icons |
| 16 | **Come and see us** | How visits work in 3 steps, visit times, "no obligation", waiting-list summary, both actions again | The emotional close, with the promise repeated |
| 17 | **Find us** | Address, directions link (no map embed), parking or drop-off note if supplied | Drawn local map or a photo of the gate |
| 18 | **Jobs strip** | Only when there are vacancies | |
| 19 | **Footer** | Contact, hours, footer links, CS number | Curved or torn top edge, one-line promise |

**Flex rules:**
- Each direction may move sections 7 to 13 to suit its emphasis. Sections 1 to 6 and 15 to 19 stay in this order.
- Any section the nursery can't fill with real content is **removed, not faked**. That includes food with no menu, a team with no names, and quotes with no source. A short, true page sells better than a padded one [`banbury-cross.md`].

### 2.3 Sales devices every engaging template shares

- **A dated notice slot** (open day, limited places, closures). `data-show-from` and `data-show-until`, in the page flow, never as a pop-up.
- **A "How places are offered" block** on Visit and join (1.6).
- **A "Questions to ask on a visit" list** in our own words [`partou-uk.md`, `storal.md`].
- **A "What's included" list** on Fees and on Home [`bright-horizons-uk.md`, `kids-planet.md`].
- **A fee table that stacks into cards at 390px.** Willow Den's table breaks words on a phone [`willowden.md`].
- **A jump bar of chips** on long inner pages [`storal.md`, `kirktonholme.md`].
- **The grades panel**, with the same honesty rules as the first three templates.

**Rules that keep "fun" from becoming "cheap":**
- **One system per template.** One signature shape, one illustration style, 3 to 5 colours, 2 typefaces. The fun is consistent, not random [`kids-planet.md`, `n-family-club.md`].
- **Earthy or confident colour, not primaries.** Every site that felt premium used muted or deep colours: sage and gold [`storal.md`], terracotta and mustard [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`], navy with peach and mint [`n-family-club.md`], coral with forest teal [`littleforestfolk.md`].
- **Display type for headings only.** The body text is a plain, legible sans at 18px and weight 400 or more. Light 300 body text failed on several sites [`storal.md`, `monkey-puzzle.md`, `childbase-partnership.md`].
- **Illustration is the main event or it's absent.** Half-hearted doodles look template-made [`fennies.md`].
- **Real things beat drawn people.** We never draw children or adults. We draw animals, plants, objects and places, and we use children's real artwork.
- **Check every tilt, torn edge and sticker at 390px.** Rotation stays at 2 degrees or less, and never applies to body text [`tops-day-nurseries.md`].

**Motion, for every direction:**
- Use it only to reward a scroll or a tap: a reveal once, a sticker that lifts on hover or focus.
- Keep each movement short (about 150 to 400ms) and never looping. Anything that moves for longer than 5 seconds must have a pause control (WCAG 2.2.2), so we don't loop.
- No parallax, auto-play, count-up numbers or carousels [`fennies.md`].
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, everything is static and every element shows in its final state.
- Content must never depend on JavaScript or animation to appear. Young Friends' lazy-loaded frames showed empty outlines in our captures [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`].

**Photos, for every direction:**
- Use rooms (tidy and empty), gardens, the entrance, kit on pegs, food as served, hands at work, children's artwork, staff (with consent), and children from behind or at a distance.
- Hands in soil [`young-friends-kindergarten.md`], a hand in wet sand [`childbase-partnership.md`], a hand holding a leaf [`willowden.md`], empty insect rooms [`bright-beginnings-leeds.md`] and the empty teal room [`monkey-puzzle.md`] are the best images in the whole study, and none shows a face.
- Children from behind need care. The hi-vis line photos are allowed in spirit [`links-city-nurseries.md`, `banbury-cross.md`], but only where no child can be identified by hair, clothing or context. If in doubt, leave it out (first brief §5.2).

### 4.1 Cut and Stick

**One line:** a nursery site made like a nursery wall: cut paper, stickers of Scottish wildlife, the children's own paintings, and a noticeboard of honest proof.

#### Target nursery
- **Type:** playgroups, community and voluntary nurseries, and small to mid private nurseries (15 to 70 places) with a playful name or creature room names, and a strong making and art culture.
- **Suits nurseries with:** few good photos, lots of children's artwork and a warm, chatty, community feel.
- **Prospects it suits:** creature and nature names that the stickers can echo:
  - Little Beehive Nursery (Cupar, Strathkinness, Montrose, Newport-on-Tay);

  Community and voluntary groups:

**"Come and see what we made today."** Parents choose a small nursery for belonging. They picture their child coming home with a painting in their bag, and a nursery that feels like a community, not a chain.

The selling emphasis is **play and making**: what children make, find and bring home, and how the nursery and its families do things together. It borrows:

#### Copy voice
Warm, homely, sensory and specific. Food words, not nutrition jargon. Short sentences. Name the dish, not the "balanced diet".
