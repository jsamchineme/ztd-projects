## How it sells
- **Promise:** "The Right Start For Under 5's"; "a sense of family"; "magic".
- **Tone:** corporate-friendly, a little promotional. "Exceptional", "outstanding", "cutting-edge" appear often.
- **Real phrases:** "Be our guests"; "buggy sheds... especially for those tag-teaming drop-offs"; "parent spaces... a moment of tranquillity"; "The majority of our registrations come from refer-a-friend recommendations."
- **Proof:** parent quote carousel; Google and daynurseries.co.uk badges; B Corp; award badges; per-location testimonials; staff profiles with fun-fact Q&A; claims of a paid 2-day induction and an internal L&D team.
- **Differentiators:** on-site chefs and an EYA-accredited menu; specialists visiting for STEM, art and PE and language exposure; parent app with live updates; dedicated parent rooms; cooking studios at some sites; open 7:45-18:00 (7am early session) for 51 weeks; recently added CCTV in all nurseries; founding family still involved; buggy sheds; 10% off for referrer and friend; sibling and full-time discounts; accepts funding, Tax-Free Childcare, vouchers.
- **Urgency:** events with "SAVE MY SPOT" (open evenings, coffee mornings) rather than scarcity of places.

## Conversion mechanics
- **CTAs:** "Contact us" in the header (always), "Book a tour" in the hero, "Contact us" after the intro, "Get in touch" in Admissions, "Book a visit" with phone and email at the foot of every page, "Save my spot" on events.
- **Enquiry form (HubSpot):** First name, last name, email, phone number, **nursery location**, **number of days of attendance per week**, "where did you hear about Fennies?", and a marketing-consent tick box. Single step, 7 fields, no calendar.
- **Phone:** 0208 770 3222 shown repeatedly. **Event booking** for open evenings.
- **Fees:** fees are on each location page (day rate and monthly tables by age band), plus a "Fees & funding" page. FAQ says fees include meals, snacks, milk, nappies and extracurricular activities.
- **Pop-ups:** a cookie banner that overlays the hero and greys the page. A subscribe box in the footer.

## Visual design
- **Typeface:** **Raleway** (Google Fonts) for headings and body, in clean bold weights. Rounded-geometric, adult.
- **Palette:** slate navy #3c4c58 (the main), sunflower yellow #ffc100, buttercup #f7c948 / #ffd300, white, light greys, pale blue panels. The yellow-on-slate scheme is warm and corporate.
- **Illustration:** thin line-art clouds, sun and rocket in pale blue; soft blob shapes in pale yellow and grey; a yellow brush-stroke quote mark.
- **Photography:** large, professionally lit, children's faces front and centre (hero video, age-stage cards). Not usable under our rules.
- **Icons:** thin outline icons for features.
- **Motion:** hero video, count-up numbers, carousels (3 on the home page), scroll fades. Heavy for a nursery site.
- **Density:** medium; long location pages (2,000 words, 21,000 px tall on a phone).
- **Fun vs premium:** premium-corporate. The "fun" is limited to the line-art doodles, which sit in lots of white space and look unfinished next to the real photos. The yellow pills are cheerful.

## Phone experience
- Works: sticky dark header, big yellow buttons, location finder with "use your location" and list/map toggle, address and hours at the top of a location page, features as icons.
- Doesn't: cookie dialog on top of the hero on every page; hero video autoplay costs data; counters that animate to odd figures; three carousels; the location page runs to 21,000 px; the maps are heavy.

## Steal this
1. **Age-stage names as a brand device** (Explorers, Discoverers, Creators, Inventors). For a small nursery, name rooms after real local things (a hill, a bird, a tree on the site) and show each room as an illustrated card with the age range. Only use names the nursery uses.
2. **Location page structure:** hours by day, address, phone and transport in the first screen; then icon list of what the site has. Copy for a single-site nursery as "Find us" at the top of home.
3. **Icon features list** ("Garden, Sensory, Buggy store, Parent room, On-site parking") for facts only, never invented.
4. **Staff Q&A cards** ("What's a fun fact that makes you stand out?"). Works with first names, role and qualification; no photo needed (use an illustrated badge or a hand-drawn portrait).
5. **Admissions block with 7-field form** that asks the child's days per week. A small nursery can ask: parent name, phone, email, child's age, days wanted, start date.
6. **Events with a "save my spot" button** (stay-and-play, coffee morning, open evening) as a low-pressure way in.
7. **"Why us" list as separate short blocks with Learn more** rather than one wall; for us, 5-6 blocks max.
8. **Stats strip** done honestly: only real numbers (years open, places, staff) with no animation.

## Avoid this
- Cookie dialog covering the hero (our builds should use no tracking and no banner where possible).
- Auto-playing hero video with an overlay title (poor performance, poor contrast).
- Carousels and counter animations: against our rules and confusing at 390px.
- Thin line-art clouds and rockets next to polished photography: looks template-y. Make illustration the main event or leave it out.
- Faces of identifiable children in heroes.
- Generic superlatives ("exceptional", "cutting-edge").
