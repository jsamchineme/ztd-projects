# Storal

## At a glance
- **Who:** a large England-wide group of "nursery schools" (58 are listed on the home page, by town: Manchester, Stamford, Ashby de la Zouch, Hove, Leicester, Nottingham, Stoke-on-Trent, Chesterfield, Banbury, Loughborough and more, many under older names such as "Children 1st"). It celebrates "10 years" on the home page. Ages 6 weeks (some 4 months) to 5 years; core hours typically 7:30am or 8am to 6pm. No Scottish sites that I saw.
- **Price position:** mid to premium group. No fees on the pages I read; a "Calculate your fees" link on the funding page.
- **URL:** https://www.storal.co.uk/ redirects to https://www.storal.com/ (the crawler had to be restarted from .com). **Studied:** 13 Oct 2026. Crawl and screenshots in `study-1/storal/`.
- **Proof headline:** daynurseries.co.uk 9.4 from 5,164 reviews; Top 20 Most Recommended Large Nursery Groups 2026; Nursery World Awards 2024 and NMT Nursery Awards 2023 and 2024 winner; an Ofsted chip with two short inspection quotes on some nursery cards.

## How it sells
- **Promise:** "Where wholesome roots grow remarkable futures". The through-line is a plant metaphor: roots, growth, "wholesome", "every child has a new story to tell", "chapters", "begin their story".
- **Tone:** warm and literary; storybook language with grown-up reassurance ("calm", "cherished", "no pressure, no formal presentation"). A little corporate when speaking about the group.
- **Proof:** daynurseries score and live reviews; stacked award badges; short quotes from the inspection report inside each nursery card; named specialists with qualifications (a former Ofsted inspector, an award-winning SEN specialist); safety team named; the open-day page repeats the review block.
- **Differentiators:** three named pillars; a whole-child curriculum with five "My..." strands; Forest School sessions with story-based play (Gruffalo, Stick Man); on-site chefs; Makaton in nurseries; Famly app; free funding guide and a free "Open Day questions" guide; specialist support team for families.
- **Urgency:** a seasonal "Open Day this October" banner; "visit us this October" page; weekday one-to-one tours as an alternative. No false scarcity.

## Conversion mechanics
- **CTAs:** "Secure a place" (sage pill, top right of every desktop page); "Enquire" on phone; open-day banner (gold); a postcode search leading to per-nursery "Learn more" and "Enquire" pill pairs; "Begin their story" links on the admissions page.
- **Enquiry flow:** choose a nursery, then child first and last name, child date of birth (or due date), ideal start date, parent first and last name, email, phone, address, postcode, reason for enquiry, how you found out, optional message. One long page form, all mandatory except the message (the field names show an asterisk). I did not submit it.
- **Admissions (four steps):** 1 register interest, 2 visit, 3 confirm place (a registration fee and deposit), 4 start (parent app, settling-in sessions, key person and key team).
- **Fees:** not shown in the page text; "Calculate your fees" on the funding page.
- **Funding page:** England wording; jump-link chips (pink, blue, green, sand pills) to sections; "Stretched funding, Flexible session times, Simpler application" as three cards; eligibility lists; a table of application deadlines.
- **Pop-ups:** a cookie banner only.

## Visual design
- **Type:** Recoleta Alt (a soft, wide-eyed display serif with a retro-warm feel) for headlines at about 46 to 83px and for card titles; Avenir (light weights, 300 for body copy, 19px) for text. The serif plus light sans pairing is the most "premium and warm" of all five sites.
- **Palette:** sage green `#6C8D73`, warm cream `#F2F0EA` and off-white `#FCFBF8`, warm brown-black text `#38312C`, gold `#D5A06A` (approx; read from the banner), dusty rose `#D5A0A5`, pale blue and sand tints on tabs and chips. All muted, earthy, adult colours. Not a primary colour in sight apart from the supplied award badges.
- **Shapes:** rounded cards (about 20px), pills, rounded-square logo tiles, tab folders in different pastel colours, circular arrow buttons, big full-width coloured bands (sage, gold).
- **Illustration:** hand-drawn watercolour spot illustrations with black line (tree with roots, hands with a heart, a pirate hat, an aeroplane, a light bulb, a piggy bank). These make the site feel crafted and are the main reason it looks fun without being childish. A gold sparkle sits next to the "Find your local nursery" heading.
- **Photography:** large, natural, mostly identifiable children and carers; the video hero; the tight close-up of children's eyes behind the specialist cards. Some good garden photos (logs, tyres, wooden stage).
- **Motion:** a video hero, photo carousels with round arrow buttons, tabs.
- **Fun and engaging:** the watercolour icons and the pastel tab folders on the approach page. **Premium:** the serif, the sage and cream, the generous space. **Generic:** the reviews widget, the stacked award badges at phone width (one per screen), the mid-page map.

## Phone experience
- **Works:** cream cookie banner is polite; the sage header with a persistent "Enquire" pill; large serif headings; tab card stack; full-width cards with a big round arrow; good tap sizes; funding jump-chips wrap into pill rows.
- **Doesn't:** the four award badges stack into about 1,000px of scrolling; the nursery list is very long (58) before any reviews; the video hero background is dark and dims the text; body font is a light weight which loses contrast on cream.

## Steal this
1. **A serif display face paired with a light sans.** Self-host a warm serif (for example a free one with a similar feel; we need the licence) for headings only, with a humanist sans for text. This is the cheapest way to move from "informational" to "premium and warm".
2. **A muted earthy palette with one gold accent and a rose:** sage, cream, warm brown, gold, dusty rose. Pick two or three from the nursery's own sign, door or garden.
3. **Watercolour spot illustrations for the nursery's own principles** (a tree with roots, hands, a heart). Commission or draw simple ones, or paint them from the children's own artwork scans. No children's faces, and the same illustration set can run through the whole site.
4. **Folder tabs in pastel colours for a "how we help children grow" section** (5 strands, each with a short list). Fully accessible if built with real buttons and visible focus.
5. **Inspection-report phrases inside the nursery card** ("Confident bilingual communicators"). In Scotland: only exact quotes from the nursery's latest Care Inspectorate report, with the date, in an unstyled quote chip.
6. **A simple four-step admissions strip with big numbers** (register, visit, confirm, start). Matches how Scottish parents apply and then wait for the council.
7. **A "free guide" lead-in on the funding page** (Open Day questions list; funded-hours guide). Offer a plain "Questions to ask on a visit" list. Parent Club has a checklist; ours should be in our own words.
8. **"We're here to help" cards for named staff** with one sentence about their role. Only staff with consent.
9. **Chips as jump links on a long page** (Funding at Storal, Check eligibility, How to apply). Great on a phone.

## Avoid this
- A 14-field one-page enquiry form with a full address (too much for a first contact; ask name, phone or email and child's age first).
- Giant stacked awards. Use one small row.
- Light-weight body type on cream (check contrast at AA).
- A 58-card list; our sites are single nurseries so avoid the list pattern.
- The reviews widget (external script and scores of other nurseries). We should show three short quotes the nursery owns, or none.
- England-only funding rules. Scotland has its own: 1,140 hours, council application.
