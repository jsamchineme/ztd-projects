# Bright Horizons UK

## At a glance
- **Who:** the largest of my five: "around 270 community and workplace nurseries" across the UK, "over thirty years". It includes Scottish nurseries, for example Annandale Early Learning and Childcare (Leith, Edinburgh) and 24 and 44 St Swithin (Aberdeen), which makes this the most relevant site for us. Ages from 3 months (some from 2 years).
- **Price position:** mid to premium, with a corporate and workplace-benefit angle (a separate "Corporate Solutions" site and a charity "Foundation").
- **URL:** https://www.brighthorizons.co.uk/ (the whole homepage is a nursery finder). **Studied:** 13 Oct 2026. Crawl and screenshots in `study-1/brighthorizons/`. The nursery pages load their content with scripts, so the crawl captured only about 20 words from each; I read the Annandale page by rendering it in a browser.
- **Proof headline:** daynurseries.co.uk "overall rating" 9.5 (footnote: "correct as of 10/06/2025", so it is an old figure shown as current); on nursery pages Care Inspectorate "Good" with a "Download Report" link and the daynurseries score (Annandale 9.7).

## How it sells
- **Promise:** "This is more than childcare. This is childhood." The group's campaign line ("This is Childhood" film on the About page) and "extraordinary, thriving, happy, special places".
- **Tone:** warm, bright and parent-aware, with rhythm: "Behind every giggle, discovery and milestone", "Time to Just Be". Slightly American-corporate in places ("Early Childhood Specialists").
- **Proof:** daynurseries score and parent quotes copied from daynurseries.co.uk, the regulator grade with the report link on each nursery page (a good practice), "Great Places to Work" awards for staff, a former Ofsted deputy director quoted on the curriculum page, a parent panel (parents who share real stories), a named nursery manager with a photo on each page.
- **Differentiators:** The Nurture Approach(R) (a trademark with three pillars: what, how and who); Bright Beginnings Curriculum (five areas: Feelings and Friendships, Sharing Thoughts and Ideas, Technical and Life Skills, Thinking Creatively, Exploring and Learning About My World); in Scotland it names the Curriculum for Excellence; an inclusive fees list (nappies, wipes, meals, snacks, formula, milk, a "Little Box of Big Memories"); four menus; Eco Schools (over 65 nurseries); Forest School; a Family app; a charity foundation; workplace nurseries.
- **Urgency:** a yellow "limited availability" banner at the top of nursery pages (Annandale: "2026/2027").

## Conversion mechanics
- **CTAs:** a yellow "Book A Visit" and a navy "Apply for a Place" at the top of every nursery page and again in the info card; Download A Brochure and Ask A Question (outline pills) in the same card; a phone number in the page header (a 0800 number site-wide) and a nursery number on nursery pages (with line hours Mon to Fri 8am to 7pm). Brochure and funding-guide downloads are lead-gen.
- **Booking:** a form in a pop-up or on a new page (not completed). Fee calculator on the nursery page: child age in months, choose at least 2 days, then Calculate, an estimate "BEFORE childcare funding is applied". The page warns funding depends on each family.
- **Fees:** an estimate via the calculator plus a downloadable fee sheet; the page lists what is included.
- **Chat and pop-ups:** a chat button, the WhatsApp channel pop-up, and the cookie panel. Together these cover much of the first phone screen.

## Visual design
- **Type:** Quicksand (a rounded geometric sans, 300 to 700) for everything; headings 32 to 45px bold in navy. A script display face (probably the licensed "Broadstone", declared in the CSS; it renders in words like "Funding" and "bookworm") appears in some heroes as a handwriting accent. The rounded type is friendly, but with the small body sizes it feels a little childlike.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#20445D` (hero, bands, text), sunshine yellow `#F2BF00` (buttons, call circles, emphasis words), pale grey `#F5F4F4`, a teal block (about `#3A89A5`) behind the icon tiles, and green for ticks. Corporate navy and yellow: reliable, a bit bank-like.
- **Shapes:** wavy cropped hero images (a curved edge), large corner radii (about 20px) on images, pill buttons, circular icon tiles, folder-tab nav on nursery pages.
- **Illustration and icons:** a set of friendly line icons in white circles (buggy park, mud kitchen, water play, sensory play, chef onsite, ready for school), each printed in colour with a short label. A simple sun logo.
- **Photography:** professional lifestyle photography with identifiable children and carers (a carer in a blue polo shirt holding a child; a smiling toddler), and a hero video.
- **Motion:** video, carousels, tabs, a sticky nav.
- **Fun and engaging:** the "at a glance" icon grid with items such as "Mud Kitchen" and "Buggy Park", the curved photo edges, the yellow accent words. **Premium:** limited; it reads as a trusted big brand rather than boutique. **Generic:** the stacked pop-ups and the long text blocks. (The page looks greyed out in my screenshots because the cookie panel dims the page behind it until you answer.)

## Phone experience
- **Works:** a round yellow phone button in the header; the nursery finder as the first thing; "Book A Visit" and "Apply for a Place" in the first two screens of a nursery page; a card with the address, hours, call button and four buttons in a single column; icon tiles in a 3-up grid; the included-in-fees checklist.
- **Doesn't:** the cookie panel covers 40% of the first screen and, on the home page, a pop-up appears over it on desktop; "Show more" hides the copy; the fee calculator and tabs are several scrolls down.

## Steal this
1. **The "at a glance" icon grid on a coloured block** with ten labelled tiles. For a Scottish nursery: pick 6 to 10 truths the nursery confirms (garden, mud kitchen, food cooked on site, buggy store, rooms by age) and draw each icon in our house style. No people.
2. **A trust card at the top of the page with the regulator grade, a "Download report" link and the review score.** In Scotland: the Care Inspectorate grades with the heading names and date, and a link to the report. Never only the best grade.
3. **"Included in our fees" as a ticked checklist**, with the one footnote ("a small number cannot offer all; check with your nursery"). Only list items the nursery confirms.
4. **A named manager with a photo, a role and a "Meet the team" link, placed right before the address card.** Staff with consent only; otherwise a role and name with no photo.
5. **A single info card: address, map, daily hours, a call button and four buttons** (Book a visit, Apply for a place, Download brochure, Ask a question). Simplify to three for a small nursery.
6. **A limited-availability notice at the top of the page** with a season ("2026/2027") that the nursery can switch off. Only if true and kept current.
7. **Nursery-day pages by age** (baby, toddler, preschooler) as three cards. Illustrate with the room, not children.
8. **A "meet the parent panel" idea reworked:** one short, consented parent quote per month, as text only, with a first name and initial.
9. **A fees estimator** (age and days). Only if the nursery supplies prices; otherwise "phone for fees".

## Avoid this
- Stacked pop-ups (cookie, WhatsApp, chat).
- A stale rating shown as current (the footnote date was 10/06/2025).
- A trademarked philosophy name ("The Nurture Approach(R)") that adds jargon. Parents trust plain words.
- Collapsing the main description behind "Show more".
- Corporate "Corporate Solutions / Careers / Foundation" top bar. We are single nurseries.
