- **Why it is in the study:** it is an acclaimed winner with a **plain, dated site**. Useful as the contrast case: what winning nurseries do not need from their website, and what the site misses.

## How it sells
- **Promise:** "Excellence from the beginning, opening the door to a brighter future." Institutional.
- **Tone:** formal, policy-like. Long paragraphs lifted from inspection language ("children are given praise and encouragement to develop their self-esteem").
- **Real phrases:** "as a nursery with over 40 years of childcare provision on campus"; "around 70% of our families speak English as an additional language" (news post); "children have the opportunity to take controlled and calculated 'risks'".
- **Proof:** awards badge as the home-page centrepiece; Ofsted grade; a long testimonial; award news posts; staff qualifications listed (BA Hons, EYPS, NNEB, BTEC, apprenticeships).
- **Differentiators:** campus location; inclusion and SEND offer; an Atelier; an allotment; an annual production (mentioned under testimonials: "Feedback on our Annual Production"); a 20-minute virtual tour; room-by-room ratios published; salary-sacrifice scheme for university staff.
- **Urgency:** places allocated first come, first served, offered four months before the start date where possible.

## Conversion mechanics
- **CTAs:** very low-key. The header phone number; a "Tour" page; links to Registration. No large booking button.
- **Flow:** download or complete a Registration form; email it with a note asking for a tour; a manager arranges a tour. Virtual tour video on the site for first look. **Phone 0113 343 1818** and email on the tour page.
- **Fees:** published openly on a Prices page with a clear day and half-day rate, the explanation of the additional services charge, and the salary scheme.
- **Waiting list:** implied by the registration form (places offered four months ahead). **Chat:** none. **Pop-ups:** cookie bar; Translate button.

## Visual design
- **Typefaces:** a quirky hand-drawn caps display face for headings (looks like a chalky "Gloria Hallelujah"/"Chelsea"-style face; the exact face was not confirmed) and a plain bold sans in the menu (Montserrat is loaded). Body is default sans.
- **Palette:** dark slate header (#313131 / #32373c), white page, golden-orange links (#fcb900-ish), a multicolour logo (magenta, green, blue, yellow). The theme is a stock WordPress "Enfold" theme with default blocks.
- **Illustration:** child-like doodles of a butterfly, ladybird and grasshopper (hand-drawn, thick outline, bright colours) used in the curriculum and room pages. They are charming and the only identity on the site.
- **Photography:** the exterior of the building, empty rooms and gardens (Caterpillars, Butterflies, Ladybirds: warm, wood-lined, natural materials, rope tunnels, wicker domes), with **no children's faces on the pages we saw**. This is exactly the style our rules want.
- **Motion:** video tour player; a slider; no micro-interactions.
- **Density:** text-heavy; the curriculum page is a wall of bullets.
- **Fun vs premium:** neither. The insect doodles and room names are the fun part; the rest is institutional.

## Phone experience
- Works: header with tap-to-call phone number, readable body text, room cards with a photo and the age and ratio, a stacked virtual tour.
- Doesn't: cookie bar and Translate overlay the first screen; dark header with a long logo; title in a dim box over a hero photo; content is long and not chunked; Prices page tiny headings.

## Steal this
1. **Empty-room photography as the main imagery.** Warm, wood-and-wicker rooms with no children is a good base for our templates. We can ask nurseries for tidy, bright, empty-room photos and garden shots.
2. **Rooms named for creatures with a hand-drawn insect for each room.** In our builds, draw simple creature icons for room names the nursery already uses (never invent names).
3. **Room cards with age band, capacity and ratio.** Only where the nursery supplies ratios; otherwise "ask us about ratios when you visit".
4. **A virtual tour page** (video if the nursery has one; otherwise a room-by-room photo tour with captions).
5. **"The Atelier" or a signature space** with its own page and a "space designed for big ideas" line; every nursery has one room or garden that is the pride of the place.
6. **Fees page that states the extra charge and why** (clear, adult, honest). In Scotland, state clearly that funded hours carry no top-up fee; only name optional extras.
7. **Award as a visual object**: the NDNA rosette used big. We can design an "award shelf" strip if the nursery has awards, using their real badges.
8. **A news post that tells the story behind an award**, e.g. "around 70% of our families speak English as an additional language" (their claim), as an emotional proof point, if the nursery supplies similar real facts.

## Avoid this
- Dim translucent title boxes over photos.
- A wall of policy-style bullet points as the curriculum page.
- No clear booking button (a tour needs an email and a form).
- Stock theme look: default fonts and heavy plugin bars.
- Cookie bar and Translate overlay on first view (our builds should avoid both; consider a lightweight language link only if the nursery asks).
