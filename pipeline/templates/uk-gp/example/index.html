Appointments

Phone us or call in at reception to book. We take bookings Monday to Friday, 9am to 5:30pm.

Routine appointments

  • Book up to 2 weeks ahead with a GP.
  • Book up to 4 weeks ahead with a nurse.
  • Appointments in the next 48 hours may not be with the doctor of your choice.

Appointments run from 8:30am to 5:30pm. We also have early appointments daily from 7:20am to 8am. Ask reception about these.

Online booking is suspended for now. Please phone or call in.

Urgent appointments

If you need help today, phone us and tell us it's urgent. The duty doctor will call you back.

When we're closed, call NHS 24 on 111. In an emergency, call 999.

Change or cancel an appointment

Phone us on 01463 235245 as soon as you know. We can then give the appointment to someone else.

Home visits

Phone before 11am to ask for a home visit. We visit people who are housebound or too ill to come to the practice.

A GP will decide if you need a visit and how soon.

You may not need to see a GP

Our practice nurses and healthcare assistants can help with many things. You can book with them by phone, like a GP appointment.

Practice nurses can help with

  • contraception checks and coil checks
  • help to stop smoking
  • asthma, COPD, heart, stroke and diabetes reviews
  • wounds, dressings and stitches
  • smear tests
  • ear syringing
  • sexual health swabs
  • new patient checks
  • ECGs (heart traces)

Healthcare assistants can help with

  • all blood tests
  • blood pressure checks
  • ECGs (heart traces)
  • urine tests
  • new patient medicals

Repeat prescriptions

Give us 72 hours' notice, Monday to Friday. There are 3 ways to order.

Online

Use our online order form.

Order a repeat prescription online

Drop-off box

Fill in the re-order slip from your last prescription. Put it in the express drop-off box at the first reception desk.

By post

Post your re-order slip to us with a stamped addressed envelope:

Kingsmills Medical Practice
18 Southside Road
Inverness
IV2 3BG

We cannot take prescription requests by phone. Requests made by phone are more likely to be written down wrongly.

Sick and fit notes

You only need a note from us if you're off work for more than 7 days.

Off for 7 days or less

You do not need a note from a doctor. Fill in a self-certification form (SC2) for your employer instead.

Get the SC2 form on GOV.UK

Off for more than 7 days

Your employer can ask for medical evidence, such as a fit note. Phone us to ask about one.

Read about fit notes on NHS inform

Register with us

You can register if you live in our practice area.

  1. Check you live in our area

    Our practice boundary changed recently. If you're not sure, phone us on 01463 235245.

  2. Bring proof of who you are

    Bring a recent utility bill and one photo ID to reception.

  3. Fill in 2 forms

    A GMS1 registration form and a health questionnaire.

  4. Have a new patient health check

    We'll offer you a health check. You can get treatment from the day you register.

We have leaflets in many languages. Ask at reception.

Read about registering with a GP on NHS inform
