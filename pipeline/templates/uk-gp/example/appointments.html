When you phone us

Our receptionists will ask a few simple questions. This helps them find the right person or service for you, such as a nurse, pharmacist or physiotherapist.

Receptionists do not make medical decisions. They follow guidance from our doctors. You do not have to answer, but it helps us to help you.

Seeing the doctor you choose

For long-term problems, or new problems we're looking into, it's best to see the same 1 or 2 doctors.

  • Plan ahead if you can. If your problem can wait a few days, you're more likely to see the doctor you choose.
  • Appointments in the next 48 hours may not be with the doctor you choose.
  • Urgent appointments are with the first doctor available.
  • Some problems can be dealt with by phone. Reception will tell you when to phone back to speak to a doctor.

At lunchtime

We close for lunch from 1pm to 2pm. If you need urgent help then, phone us and follow the instructions. For anything routine, please phone after 2pm.

When we're closed

NHS 24 will ask about your health and tell you what to do. If you need to be seen, they may arrange for you to go to the urgent care centre in Inverness, or for a home visit.

Dental emergency? Contact your own dentist. If you do not have one, phone the Highland Dental Helpline on 0800 141 2362, Monday to Friday, 8:45am to 5pm.

You may not need to see a GP

Other people in our team can help with many things. Book with them by phone, like a GP appointment.

Practice nurses can help with

  • contraception checks and coil checks
  • help to stop smoking
  • asthma, COPD, heart, stroke and diabetes reviews
  • wounds, dressings and stitches
  • smear tests
  • ear syringing
  • sexual health swabs
  • new patient checks
  • ECGs (heart traces)

When you book, we'll ask what it's for. This is so you see a nurse who can help.

Healthcare assistants can help with

  • all blood tests
  • blood pressure checks
  • ECGs (heart traces)
  • urine tests
  • new patient medicals

Change, cancel or running late

Phone us on 01463 235245 as soon as you know. We can then give the appointment to someone else.

If you cannot come

Phone us to change or cancel, so we can offer your appointment to someone else.

If you're running late

Phone ahead to rebook. We may not be able to see you, as it would delay other patients. If there's time, we may see you briefly at the end of surgery.

If you miss an appointment

We'll write to ask if something stopped you telling us. If you keep missing appointments, we may remove you from our list.

Doctors in training and students

We're a training practice. Qualified doctors train with us to become GPs, for 6 months to a year.

Will a doctor in training see me?

They may. Doctors in training are fully qualified doctors. Some of our surgeries are set up to support them.

Can my appointment be recorded?

Doctors in training sometimes video their appointments, to learn from them. We will always ask you first. You can say no, or change your mind at the end of the appointment.

Saying no will not affect your care. We delete the recording once the doctor has reviewed it.

Will a medical student be there?

We teach medical students from the University of Aberdeen. They may sit in, or see you with a GP supervising. We'll always ask you first. Tell us at any time if you'd rather not.
