    Reception and the waiting area

    Check in at the reception desk. Then wait in the waiting area until you’re called.

    • A large, comfortable waiting area.
    • Baby changing and a place to feed your baby.
    • Art on the walls by local art groups and clubs.

    Feeling nervous? Tell us when you book or when you arrive. NHS advice is to let the dental team know, so they can help you feel more in control.

  3. 3

    Step 3 of 4

    The treatment room

    We have 10 bright treatment rooms. The General Dental Council (GDC) expects every dentist to explain your options and get your consent before treatment.

    • All our treatment rooms have digital x-rays.
    • Our own sterilising room for instruments, and a dental laboratory on site for making dentures.

    Agree a “stop” signal NHS advice for nervous patients: agree a signal with your dentist, such as raising your hand, so you can pause at any time.

Your first visit

What to expect

  • After your first appointment, we give you a printed estimate of what any treatment will cost.
  • Think you may not have to pay? Ask at reception if you’re exempt from charges.
  • We ask you to pay before treatment, by cash, debit card or credit card. Phone to check.
  • Can’t come? Give us at least 24 hours’ notice. If you give less, we may charge for the time.

Bring someone with you NHS advice for nervous patients: it can help to bring a friend or relative, or listen to your own music.

Call 999 or go to A&E if swelling makes it hard to breathe or swallow, or if bleeding won’t stop.

Costs

What NHS treatment costs in Scotland

We offer NHS and private treatment. These are the national NHS rules.

Free

Check-ups

NHS check-ups are free for everyone in Scotland.

Free

Treatment, if you’re…

  • under 26
  • pregnant, or had a baby in the last 12 months
  • getting help through income or benefits, such as an HC2 certificate
80%

Everyone else

You pay 80% of the cost of NHS treatment, up to £384 for a course of treatment. An HC3 certificate gives you some help with costs.

Private treatment has its own prices. Ask us for a written estimate before you start. Read about NHS dental charges on NHS inform.
