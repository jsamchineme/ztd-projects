About us

Our story

  • 1977: Chris Price takes over the practice at 97 High Street.
  • 1979: Charles Crummey joins him. The practice grows steadily from here.
  • 2001: We become a training practice, taking on new dental graduates each year.
  • 2013: We move into the Pat Gifford Building on the High Street.
