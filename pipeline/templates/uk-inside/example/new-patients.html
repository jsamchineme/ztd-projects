Joining

How joining works

There’s a queue to join. We’ll contact you when we can see you.

NHS

4 steps to your first check-up

  1. Send a registration requestFill in the short form on our current website, or phone us. You’ll need your name, address, phone number and email.
  2. Wait in the queueWe add you to our queue for registration. Phone to check how long the wait is now.
  3. We contact youOur reception team will get in touch to book your first appointment.
  4. Your first check-upA full check of your mouth, teeth and gums. NHS check-ups are free.
Send a registration request

Good to know

Once you’re registered

  • You stay registered for life, unless you or your dentist ask for it to end.
  • Your dentist decides how often you need a check-up. It can be anything from under 6 months to every 2 years.
  • Children can join too. We suggest your child’s first visit is before they’re 6 months old.

Source for registration and check-ups: NHS inform.

Your first visit

What happens at your first visit

Your first visit is a full check-up. Your dentist explains everything before any treatment.

At the appointment

  1. Fill in a medical history formWe’ll ask you to do this before your appointment.
  2. A full checkYour dentist looks at your mouth, teeth and gums. You may need x-rays.
  3. A talk about what we foundYour dentist explains any treatment you need and answers your questions.
  4. Your treatment planYou get a printed estimate of the cost. Treatment can go at a pace that suits you.
See inside before you come

What to bring

  • An up-to-date list of all the medicines you take.
  • Think you may not have to pay? Ask at reception if you’re exempt from charges.
  • A way to pay. We ask you to pay before treatment, by cash, debit card or credit card. Phone to check.
  • Someone with you, if it helps. NHS advice for nervous patients is to bring headphones and music, too.

Can’t come? Give us at least 24 hours’ notice. If you give less, we may charge for the time.

Questions

Common questions

Joining

Are you taking new NHS patients?

You can send a registration request and we’ll add you to our queue. Phone to check how long the wait is now.

Can I ask for someone else to join, like my child?

Yes. The registration form lets you ask for yourself or for someone else. We suggest a child’s first visit is before they’re 6 months old.

What if I can’t get a place with you?

Not all dentists can take new NHS patients. You can look for another NHS dentist near you, or contact your local health board, NHS Lothian.

Read how to register with a dentist on NHS inform.

Your visits

How often will I need a check-up?

Your dentist decides, based on what your teeth and gums need. It can be anything from under 6 months to every 2 years.

I feel nervous about the dentist. What can I do?

Tell us when you book or when you arrive. Your dentist will take time to explain what’s happening, and you can choose to stop at any stage.

Read more help for nervous patients.

What if I need to cancel?

Phone us on 01875 811136 at least 24 hours before. If you give less notice, we may charge for the time.

Urgent help

Toothache or a dental emergency

Severe toothache, a broken tooth or swelling? This is who to phone, even before you’ve joined.

When we’re open

If you’re registered with us, phone first thing in the morning. We’ll see you within 24 hours.

Call 01875 811136

When we’re closed

Phone us on 01875 811136 and you’ll be given the number for the emergency dental service.

Or phone NHS 24 on 111.

Not registered with a dentist yet? Phone NHS 24 on 111.

Read about dental emergencies on NHS inform.

If it’s life-threatening

Call 999 or go to A&E if swelling makes it hard to breathe or swallow, or if bleeding won’t stop.
