Our nursery

A purpose-built nursery in the countryside near Alloway, with two playrooms, a big garden and the farm park next door.

Our rooms

We have space for 57 children at a time, in two playrooms. Each room has its own storage, bathroom and changing area.

  • Ages 2 to 3

    Sunshine Room

    • Up to 25 children
    • 1 adult to 5 children

    A bright, open room full of natural things to play with. Children take their first steps towards doing things for themselves.

    A separate quiet room is kept for books, naps and cuddles. Play here follows what each child wants to do.

  • Ages 3 to 5

    Rainbow Room

    • Up to 32 children
    • 1 adult to 8 children

    This room is about following children's own interests. There are areas for role play, water play, mark-making and building.

    Natural materials are always out for children to use. It is a place to investigate and make first friendships.

Outdoors and animals

Our garden is shared by both rooms, and children can move freely between indoors and out. It is open in all weathers, so wellies and puddle suits are a must.

It has wooden play frames, a treehouse, tunnels, slides, sandpits, a mud kitchen, water play and a growing corner. On hot days children cool off in the mini stream.

Heads of Ayr Farm Park is next door. Our staff take children there regularly to see, feed and learn about the animals. We also visit the woods and a private beach nearby.

Children wash their hands after every time they touch an animal.

A day at nursery

The day follows what children choose to do, indoors and out. These are the fixed points.

  1. Breakfast

    8am to 8:30am, for children who need it.

  2. Morning snack, then toothbrushing

    We help children brush their teeth after snack every day, as part of Childsmile.

  3. Lunch

    A meal cooked here, with fresh fruit.

  4. Mid-afternoon snack

    For example, a homemade oaty bake or flapjack.

  5. Light tea

    For example, filled wraps or bagels with salad.

Ask us about the times in your child's room.

Seeing your child's day

We use an online Learning Journal to record and share what your child learns. You can see your child's profile at home and add comments.

No internet at home? You can see your child's profile at the nursery.

Food and allergies

Lindsey, our chef, plans and cooks our meals and snacks here. She holds a Diploma in Professional Cookery. Food can be pureed if your child needs it.

Our menu runs on a 10-week rota and may change with the seasons. Milk, water and fresh fruit are always available.

Allergies: tell us straight away if your child has any allergy. Our kitchen knows every child's allergies and the days they attend. You can ask the kitchen for ingredients and allergen information at any time.

All snacks may contain traces of nuts. We make other choices for allergies, special diets and fussy eaters.

Sleep and care

  • Sleep

    We follow the sleep instructions on your child's enrolment form. In the 2 to 3 room, we will not stop a child sleeping if they need it on a full day.

  • Nappies and changing

    Each playroom has its own bathroom and changing area. Bring nappies if your child uses them.

  • Keeping safe

    All entrances are security controlled. The garden is fully secure. CCTV covers the grounds and reception.

Support for every child

Every child should get the support they need. Sometimes we know before a child starts. We then plan with you and any professionals involved.

Sometimes a need shows up later. We will talk to you first and only refer your child to other professionals with your permission.

Speech, physio or occupational therapists can see your child at nursery. We tell you beforehand and keep you updated.

We follow Getting It Right For Every Child (GIRFEC).
