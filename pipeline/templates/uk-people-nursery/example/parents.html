If we have to close

Bad weather, a power cut or other emergencies can close the nursery. We will tell you by phone and with notices.

Any closure will be shown at the top of our homepage too.

Illness and medicine

If your child is ill

Phone us on 01292 388456 to tell us your child is not coming in.

Keep your child at home if they have a high temperature or feel unwell. Follow our exclusion policy.

After an infectious illness such as chickenpox, check with your doctor when your child can come back.

Medicine

If your child needs medicine at nursery, talk to us first. You fill in a consent form before we can give it.

We do not keep a stock of any medicine. Please leave 2 sachets of Calpol with us for emergencies.

If your child becomes ill at nursery, we will phone you or your emergency contacts.

Dropping off and collecting

  • We keep a list of the people you allow to collect your child.
  • Always tell us if someone else is collecting.
  • We cannot let your child go home with anyone under 16.
  • Please arrive by 5:50pm, so we can close at 6pm. Come earlier if you need a longer handover.
  • If you are held up, phone us so we can reassure your child. Late fees apply.

Visitors sign our visitors' book when they come in.

Paying fees

Fees are paid in advance. Bank transfer is our preferred way to pay. Ask the office for our bank details.

We bill extra sessions before they happen. A late payment costs £20.
