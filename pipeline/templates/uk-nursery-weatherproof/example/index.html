Whatever the weather

We're a fully outdoor nursery, so every season is part of the day. Here's what happens when it turns.

  • Rain

    Puddles become projects.

    When rain pooled in a dip in the woods, the children chose to bring tools and containers and play there.

    Seen by the inspector, February 2025

  • Wind

    Risks spotted and cleared.

    Staff spot risks on site and remove them. Children help weigh up the risks of their own play, too.

    Seen by the inspector, February 2025

  • Cold

    Cosy places to stop.

    The yurt has soft seating for a rest, there are hammocks in the woods, and our nappy-changing tents are kept warm.

    Seen by the inspector, February 2025

  • Sun

    Room to run.

    Long days in the wildflower meadow, the fields and on woodland walks. Ask us what to pack for sunny days.

Where we go

Our nursery is the woodland round Duffus Estate. These are the real places your child will know by name.

  1. The yurt base

    Our home base, marked “Welcome!” on our site map. The yurt has soft seating for quiet time.

  2. The parachutes

    A big parachute canopy strung between the trees, with log seats and benches underneath.

  3. The Big Field and the meadow

    Open space to run, with fields and a wildflower meadow.

  4. Squirrel Woods and the Haggis Tree

    Two places marked on our own hand-drawn site map, by the walled garden.

  5. The top woods

    What we call “the top woods”: one of our three main areas, with the yurt base and the parachutes.

A day outside

Where children eat, rest and go to the toilet: the questions every parent asks us.

  1. Arrive at the yurt base

    Sessions start at 8am or 9am.

  2. Play where they choose

    Children lead their own play. Staff follow their interests, from real tools to potions and berry dye.

  3. Wash hands, wherever they are

    Every play area has a portable handwashing unit.

  4. Lunch together

    Staff sit and eat with the children. On Tasty Tuesdays, children help make soup and bread.

  5. Rest

    A hammock in the woods, or the soft seats in the yurt.

  6. Home time

    Little Saplings go home at 1pm. Older children go home at 2pm, 3pm or 5pm, by session.

Fees and funding

We're a funded provider for Moray Council. Your child can use some or all of their 1,140 funded hours with us.

£50A full 6-hour day for children aged 3 and over, with lunch and snacks, when it isn't covered by funded hours.
£38A 4-hour morning in Little Saplings, with a snack.

Fees as published on our website in September 2026. Phone us to check before you book.
