Which domain should we focus on?

Recommendation

Focus on small independent nurseries first.

  1. Most need: 97% of the nurseries we graded have a poor, average or missing website. For dental it's 50% and for GP 42%.
  2. Easiest to reach: we have an email for all 802 nurseries and the manager's name for 795. For GP and dental we only have phone numbers.
  3. Weakest competition: nurseries mostly build their own sites on Wix or WordPress. GPs have a free NHS website (GP.scot) and vendors on yearly contracts.

Recommended order

  1. Nurseries, now

    Start with the 269 Bad or Average nurseries that already have a site (226 private, 43 voluntary). Email the named manager with a before-and-after. The 460 with no website come next. Templates, research and a board with 220 cards are already in place.

  2. Independent dental, next

    Target the 448 Bad or Average practices, plus 64 with no site, and leave out group-owned ones. Pitch practices moving towards private work. We have phone numbers only, so finding emails comes first. 501 cards are already on the main board.

  3. GP, only through partners

    Don't sell rebuilds to GPs. If we do anything, offer a restyle that works with their existing vendor, or partner with Genpra or a health board. Our GP list isn't on a board yet.

Caveats

  • The grades are our own reviews, not an independent audit.
  • The "84% start on Google" figure comes from a marketing agency's survey. A YouGov survey found only 12% of parents rely mainly on a nursery's website, with visits and word of mouth counting for more.
  • The nursery list deliberately includes only private and voluntary providers running 3 or fewer services, so it is pre-filtered towards buyers.
  • We have no data on budgets for any domain. One thing to test in the first pitches is what nurseries will pay.
