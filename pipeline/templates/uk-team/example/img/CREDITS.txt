Photo credits: Kirriemuir Dental Practice (worked example for the "Familiar Faces" template)

Every photo in this folder was published by Kirriemuir Dental Practice on its own website,
www.kirriemuirdentalpractice.com, and was downloaded on 11 October 2026 for a sales mock-up
shown to that practice. No stock or AI-generated images are used. Names and roles are taken
from the same page that publishes each portrait. Before going live, get the practice's
written confirmation that it holds consent from every person shown (see BRIEF.md, section 8).

Files were resized and recompressed (progressive JPEG). "-face" files are square crops of the
same portrait. Nothing else was edited (no retouching, no background changes).
