New patients

Join as a new patient

There are two ways to join. Both start with a phone call. When you join, we'll tell you which dentist will look after you.

Join on the NHS

  1. Phone 01575 572711 and ask if we're taking new NHS patients. We don't publish this online, so please phone to check.
  2. If we can take you, we'll book your first examination. NHS check-ups are free for everyone in Scotland.
  3. Bring a list of any medicines you take. You'll fill in a medical history form.

You can register your children at the same time, from birth.

Toothache or a dental emergency

Open Monday to Friday from 9am

If you're our patient

Phone us as soon as we open. Tell us it's urgent.

When we're closed

Phone NHS 24 on 111

Out of hours is 6pm to 8am Monday to Thursday, and 6pm Friday to 8am Monday.

Not registered with a dentist

Phone the Angus Dental Advice Line, Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm.

01241 432 481

In a life-threatening emergency, phone 999.

A chipped tooth, a lost filling or a broken denture with no pain is not an emergency. Phone us when we're open.

Costs

What it costs

Your dentist will explain the cost before any treatment starts.

NHS treatment in Scotland

Check-ups are free for everyone.

NHS treatment is also free if you are:

  • under 26
  • pregnant, or gave birth in the last 12 months
  • exempt because of your income or benefits, for example with an HC2 certificate

Everyone else pays 80% of the cost, up to £384 for a course of treatment. An HC3 certificate gives you partial help.
