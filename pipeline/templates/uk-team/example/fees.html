NHS

NHS treatment in Scotland

The Scottish Government sets NHS charges, and they can go up each year.

Who pays nothing

Check-ups are free for everyone.

All NHS treatment is also free if you are:

  • under 26
  • pregnant, or gave birth in the last 12 months
  • exempt because of your income or benefits

If you're exempt, tell your dentist and bring your proof with you.

  • Check if your benefits mean free treatment

What everyone else pays

You pay 80% of the cost of your treatment. You will never pay more than £384 for a course of treatment.

If you find it hard to pay, the NHS Low Income Scheme may help with some or all of the cost. You apply with form HC1.

You can ask your dentist for a treatment plan that shows the cost before you start.

Questions about the plan

Why join a plan?

A plan is a simple, flexible way to pay for private dental care, and it encourages you to visit us regularly. That means:

  • regular hygiene appointments, for healthier gums
  • prevention, to reduce decay
  • problems found early, so treatment is less complex and less expensive
What is the Dental Emergency Assistance Support product?

It's a discretionary support product that comes with both plans. You may be able to ask for help with:

  • emergency treatment when you're away from home or outside our opening hours
  • treatment after a dental injury
  • a hospital benefit
  • oral cancer

"Discretionary" means help isn't guaranteed. Phone to check what it covers.

How do I pay?

By monthly direct debit. If more than one person in your family is a member, we collect one direct debit for everyone.

Can I stay an NHS patient instead?

The plan is private. NHS check-ups are free for everyone in Scotland. Phone us to ask whether we can see you on the NHS.

If you don't come and don't let us know, we may charge you. This applies to NHS and private patients. Please phone us as early as you can if you can't come.
