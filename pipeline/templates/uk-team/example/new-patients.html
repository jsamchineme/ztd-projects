New patients

Two ways to join

You can register your children at the same time as yourself, from birth.

Join on the NHS

  1. Phone 01575 572711 and ask if we're taking new NHS patients. We don't publish this online, so please phone to check.
  2. If we can take you, we'll ask for your name, address, date of birth and contact details.
  3. We'll book your first full check-up. NHS check-ups are free for everyone in Scotland.
  4. Before your appointment, fill in a medical history form. Bring a list of any medicines you take.

You don't need to pay a deposit to register. A practice may put you on a waiting list until your first check-up.

What to expect

Your first visit

Your first appointment is a full check-up. Your dentist will explain any treatment you need, and what it costs, before it starts.

What to bring

  • your filled-in medical history form
  • a list of all the medicines you take
  • proof if you get free NHS treatment, for example because of benefits

If English isn't your first language, NHS patients can ask for a free translation service.

At your check-up

Your dentist will:

  • look at your teeth, gums, tongue and mouth
  • tell you how to keep your mouth healthy
  • explain any treatment you need, your options and the cost

If you need treatment, you'll get a treatment plan. You can take a copy home.

Who you'll see

When you join, you should be told which dentist will look after you. You can read about all eight of our dentists before you come.

A private new patient examination costs £85. NHS check-ups are free.

Questions about joining

Are you taking new NHS patients?

We don't publish this online. Please phone to check.

  • Call 01575 572711
Can I register my children?

Yes. Register them at the same time as yourself. You don't have to wait until their teeth come through. You can register them as soon as they're born.

Do I have to pay to register?

No. You don't need to pay a deposit for an appointment to register on the NHS. NHS check-ups are free.

How often will I need a check-up?

Your dentist will decide based on your needs. It could be anything from less than 6 months to every 2 years. If you're seen less often than every 12 months, that's a sign of good oral health.

I'm already registered with another dentist. Can I move?

Yes, if we can take you. When you're treated by a new dentist and don't tell them you're registered elsewhere, your NHS registration moves to the new dentist automatically.

I need help now but I'm not registered

Phone the Angus Dental Advice Line, Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:30pm. At other times, phone NHS 24 on 111.

  • Call 01241 432 481
What if I miss an appointment?

We may charge you if you don't come and don't let us know. This applies to NHS and private patients.
