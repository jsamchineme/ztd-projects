# Team photo shot list

**For:** the practice manager and a local photographer. **Time:** one half-day session, during a quiet clinic or before opening. **Outcome:** a consistent set of team portraits, two group photos and a few practice photos, ready for the new website and usable for 2 to 3 years.

## The shots

| # | Shot | Who | Framing | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Individual portrait** (priority) | Every dentist, hygienist, therapist, technician and manager. Nurses and reception if they'd like one. | Head and upper chest. Portrait orientation (4:5). Eyes about a third from the top. Leave space above the head and at both sides: we crop. Look straight at the lens. Natural smile. | Team cards, face pile |
| 2 | **Same portrait, relaxed** | As above | Same set-up, a genuine laugh or a half-turn. One frame each is enough. | Spotlight section |
| 3 | **Reception team** | Everyone who answers the phone | Standing together at the reception desk or the practice sign. Landscape (5:4 or 3:2). Faces at the same height. | Homepage hero: "They're who you'll speak to when you phone" |
| 4 | **Nursing team** | Dental nurses | Landscape (wide 2.5:1 is fine), two rows, front row seated | Team section |
| 5 | **Whole team** (optional) | Everyone | Outside the building or in the waiting area | About page |
| 6 | **The front door and reception** | No people, or staff only | Landscape, eye level, as a patient arriving would see it | "How to find us" (helps anxious patients recognise the place) |
| 7 | **A surgery** | Staff only, no patient in the chair | Landscape, chair and window, tidy | Treatments pages |

Don't shoot patients, before-and-after mouths or "smiling model" set-ups. They need separate consent, and NHS guidance warns against decorative images.

## Look and light

- **Background:** one plain, light wall (white, pale grey or the practice's colour), the same for everyone. A 1.5m roll of grey paper is ideal. No clutter, plants or windows behind heads.
- **Light:** soft and even from the front and slightly to one side (a large softbox or a big window with daylight). No hard flash shadows and no ring-light catchlights. Same set-up for every portrait so the set matches.
- **Camera:** a 70 to 105mm lens (or 2× on a recent phone), at eye level, about 2m away. Focus on the eyes. Shoot RAW or the highest quality JPEG.
- **Editing:** colour and exposure correction only. No skin smoothing, slimming or background replacement. We need to be able to say these are real, unaltered photos of real people.

## What to wear

- Usual practice uniform or scrubs, in the colours patients see. Name badges on are fine. Matching colours across the team look best.
- Plain colours. Avoid small checks, stripes and big logos (they shimmer on screens).
- Hair and make-up as on a normal working day. Glasses on if you usually wear them (tilt them slightly down to avoid glare).

## Consent (before anyone is photographed)

1. Each person signs a short consent form agreeing that their **photo, name, role, qualifications, GDC number and the bio they approve** can appear on the practice website and social media.
2. The form says they can **withdraw at any time**, and the photo will be removed within 5 working days (same day on request).
3. When someone leaves the practice, their photo comes down within 5 working days.
4. Keep the signed forms with the practice's GDPR records (lawful basis: consent). Don't photograph anyone who hasn't signed. They get an initials tile on the site instead, which looks fine.
5. **Bios:** each person writes or approves 2 to 4 sentences: where they trained and when, what they enjoy treating, and one thing about life outside work. First-person quotes are welcome, but only in their own words.

## Files to hand over

- **Name each file** firstname-lastname.jpg, for example `jane-leslie.jpg`, and include a spreadsheet: name exactly as it should appear, role, GDC number, start year, bio.
- **Originals:** full resolution, at least 1600px on the short side, sRGB, JPEG at high quality. Send by a file-transfer link, not email.
- **We produce web sizes:** portraits at 360px and 520px wide (15 to 35 KB each), face crops at 112px, group photos at 720px and 1200px (under 90 KB). The whole homepage stays under 700 KB on a phone.
