Photo credits: worked example (The Secret Garden Outdoor Nursery, Letham, Fife)

All photos are the nursery's own, as published on its current website, secretgardenoutdoor-nursery.co.uk.
They are used here for a sales mock-up only. Before handover, the nursery must confirm it owns them (or holds
a licence) and is happy for them to be reused. Every file was resized and re-encoded, which strips all metadata
(EXIF and GPS). Each size has a .jpg and a .webp of the same image.

Child photo rule (research brief section 5): no identifiable child appears in any file. Children are shown only
from behind or far away. Photos on the site with a child's face were left out (see "Not used" below).
