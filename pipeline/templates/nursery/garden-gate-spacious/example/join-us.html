Step one

Book a visit to the woods

A visit is the best way to see if we’re right for your child. Phone or email us to book.

What happens on a visit

  • You and your child see the nursery in action.
  • You can ask staff anything: the day, kit, funding, settling in.
  • Your child can be under 3 for a visit.
  • Phone to check where to meet. Come dressed for the woods.
Phone 07732 048289 Email the office
Who can come
  • New children start once they’ve turned 3.
  • New children come at least two days a week.
  • A child who defers school can stay another year, funded in the same way.
  • Some school children come one or two days a week for a “Buzzard” year, if there’s space.

Children whose school start is delayed, but who can’t defer, can come as paying places up to their 7th birthday, if there’s space.

Step three

Starting with us

Settling in

Every child settles at their own pace. Ask us how settling in works, and how you’ll hear about your child’s day.

Your child’s group

We have two groups, Buttercups and Foxgloves, each with its own team. Ask which group your child would join, and who their key worker would be.

What to bring

Waterproofs, wellies, warm layers, spare clothes in a dry bag and a packed lunch. We can lend clothing and boots.

Common questions

Questions parents ask us

Before you start

Is it really outdoors all day, all year?

Yes. We’re a fully outdoor nursery. The yurt, tarps and the fire give shelter and warmth, and children move a lot to keep warm.

What if my child gets cold or wet?

Good kit makes the difference: layers, waterproofs and wellies. Staff check gloves, hats and socks through the day. We can lend clothing and boots. See the kit list.

Can we use funded hours here?

Yes. We’re a Fife Council partner provider. Children can come on funded, paid or split places. Read how funded hours work.

Can my child also go to another nursery?

Yes. A split place means your child comes here and to another early learning or childcare setting. Phone us to talk it through.

Getting ready for school

Will my child be ready for school?

In our experience, almost all children settle at school with no problems. They meet numbers and letters through play here, and build the confidence to learn.

Can my child defer school and stay?

If your child can defer, the extra year is funded in the same way as their other nursery years. We’re happy to give advice.
