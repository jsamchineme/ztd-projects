# Photo shot list: "Walk through the gate before you visit"

**For:** the nursery manager, or a local photographer the nursery books. **Time:** about 2 hours, before children arrive or after they leave. A dry, bright, overcast morning is best. **Gear:** a recent phone is fine; a camera with a 24 to 35mm lens is better. No flash.

**Why these photos:** parents judge a nursery on the place and the people. These photos let a parent walk from the gate to the garden before they visit, so the visit itself is about questions, not first impressions. Each photo has a job. If a shot doesn't show a parent where something is or what it's like, skip it.

**The big rule:** no child's face, ever. Shoot the rooms empty, or with children only from behind or far away, so no one could recognise them. A website is public and permanent, and a parent can withdraw consent at any time. The site must look complete with no children in it at all.

## The walk-through: 14 shots, in order

| # | Shot | Framing | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The building or gate from the street** | Landscape. Stand where a parent first sees it: the pavement, the car park or the lane. Include the gate or door and any sign. | The most important photo. It answers "Is this it?" Overcast light, so the front isn't in hard shadow. |
| 2 | **The gate or front door, close** | Landscape, straight on. The sign, the door and the bell or entry buzzer. | Shows how you get in. Hide any door code. |
| 3 | **Drop-off and parking** (if you have them) | Wide, showing where parents stop or park. | Only if it's true and allowed. Blur readable number plates. |
| 4 | **Coming in: the first view inside** | Landscape, from just inside the door, at adult eye level (about 1.6m). | The cloakroom or hall a parent walks through every morning. |
| 5 | **The cloakroom and pegs** | Landscape, along the row of pegs. | **Hide every name label** (turn them, or shoot when they're off). Coats on pegs are fine. |
| 6 | **Each room, wide** (one per age group) | Landscape, from a corner, so you see the whole room. Then crouch to a child's eye height (about 90cm) for a second frame. | Tidy first (see the checklist). Natural light: blinds open, room lights on. |
| 7 | **A detail in each room** | Landscape or square, close. Reading corner, sand tray, home corner, a shelf of real objects. | Shows the kind of play, not just the furniture. |
| 8 | **The sleep or rest area** | Landscape, lights as they are at nap time. | **Empty.** Never photograph a sleeping child. |
| 9 | **The garden, from the door** | Landscape, from where children step outside. | Shows how big it is and how they get to it. |
| 10 | **The garden, from the far end** | Landscape, looking back at the building. | A second angle shows the whole space. Take a third from a corner if it's big. |
| 11 | **Outdoor kit and natural spaces** | Mud kitchen, planting beds, logs, den area, the woods or the beach (if you go). | The research says parents look closely at outdoor space. Show what's really there. |
| 12 | **The kitchen and food as served** | The kitchen (if children's food is cooked there), then a plate or a snack table, close. | Clean surfaces. No packaging brands. Shoot real food on a normal day. |
| 13 | **Children's work on display** | Square or landscape, close. Paintings, models, a nature table. | **No names** on the work. This is the site's decoration: real marks, not clip art. |
| 14 | **The people** | One headshot per manager and room lead, and anyone else who agrees. Head and shoulders, plain wall or soft outdoor background, window light from the side, the same crop for everyone. Portrait 4:5. | Optional for this template (it lists names without photos), but it makes the team page stronger. Each person signs the staff consent form below first. |

**Optional extras:** a calm 60 to 90 second phone video walking from the gate to the garden (no children in it, no music needed), and seasonal versions of shots 9 to 11 (snow, autumn leaves, summer planting) to swap in through the year.

## Tidy the room checklist (5 minutes per room)

- [ ] Children's names out of shot: pegs, drawers, labels, artwork, registers, birthday charts, photo boards.
- [ ] Noticeboards: nothing with children's names, photos, allergies or medical details.
- [ ] Screens off. Tablets and the nursery app closed.
- [ ] Clear clutter: bags, cleaning products, nappies, bins, cables, staff mugs.
- [ ] Toys back in their places, but not staged like a catalogue. It should look like a real morning.
- [ ] Blinds open, room lights on, doors to the garden open if it's dry.
- [ ] Outside: hoses coiled, gates shut, nothing broken in shot.

## Light and framing

- Daylight where you can. Turn on every room light. Avoid mixing very warm and very cold light.
- Keep verticals straight: hold the phone level, and step back rather than tilting up at a building.
- Landscape (3:2 or 4:3) unless the table says otherwise. Leave space around the subject; the website will crop.
- No filters, no heavy editing. It should look the same when a parent arrives.

## Consent

- **Children:** this template never needs a child's face. If a child is in the background, they must be from behind or too far away to recognise. If in doubt, leave the photo out. Never use photos of children in nappies, getting changed, sleeping or in the bath.
- **Staff:** use a photo of a staff member only with their signed consent. Keep the forms. Anyone can withdraw consent, and their photo comes off the website.
- **Old photos already on your website** may not be reused if they show children's faces, even with past consent: a new website is a new use.

### Staff consent form (one per person; copy onto nursery letterhead)

> I agree that [Nursery name] may use photographs of me, taken on [date], on its website and printed information for parents. They will be shown with my name, job title and the qualifications I list below. I can withdraw this consent at any time by telling the manager, and the photos will be removed from the website within 14 days. When I leave the nursery, my photo will be removed within 14 days.
>
> Name: ____________ Job title: ____________ Qualifications to show: ____________
>
> Signature: ____________ Date: ________

## Files to send

- Original files, full size, JPEG or HEIC, straight from the camera or phone. Not screenshots or WhatsApp copies.
- Name them by shot number: `01-gate.jpg`, `06-room-babies.jpg`, `09-garden-from-door.jpg`, `14-headshot-jane-smith.jpg`.
- Write down each room's name and the ages it's for, and each person's name, job title and qualifications exactly as they should appear.
- Send by a file-sharing link (for example OneDrive or Google Drive), not as email attachments.
- We resize everything for the website (each photo ends up about 30 to 120 KB) and remove all location data.

## Before you finish

- [ ] Shots 1, 2, 6 (every room), 9, 10 and 11 done: the essentials.
- [ ] No child's face in any photo. No names anywhere in shot.
- [ ] A signed consent form for every staff member pictured.
- [ ] Room names and ages, and staff details, written down.
- [ ] Originals sent by a sharing link.

## For an outdoor nursery (no building)

Swap shots 1 to 8 for: the meeting point (car park, park or lane), the path or walk to the site, the entrance to the site (a gate, an arch, a sign on a tree), each shelter (yurt, cabin, tarp, tent) inside and out, the fire circle, the toilet and handwashing set-up, and the kit store. Then shots 9 to 14 as above. The worked example is built this way.
