Photo credits for the Cut and Stick worked example (Little Bugs at Abercorn)
==========================================================================

Source: every photo below comes from the nursery's own website,
https://www.littlebugs.co.uk/, crawled on 13 October 2026 with
pipeline/crawl.mjs. No stock, no AI-generated images. Metadata (EXIF,
location) was stripped when each file was re-saved. Sizes: JPEG at
quality 74 plus WebP at quality 64.

Rule applied: no identifiable child, no child's name, no name pegs. Most of
the site's 102 images show children's faces, so most were excluded. The
crops below keep only hands, objects and places.

-----------------
- 002 founder with a child in the mud: identifiable child.
- 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 045, 046, 047, 049, 050, 056 to 059, 061, 063 to
  071, 075 to 082, 084 to 086, 088 to 092, 094, 096 to 102 and the blog
  thumbnails 017 to 026: identifiable children (faces, or hair, clothing and
  setting together). Not reusable for a sales mock-up.
- 094 chef Vas in the polytunnel: two children's heads in the foreground;
  a crop without them would cut the chef's face in half.
- 052 to 055 Wild Bugs staff selfies: they are school-age programme staff,
  not the nursery team shown in the example.
- 087 the yurt: people inside, low quality.
- 011 to 015, 051, 093: partner logos, video thumbnail, Wee Foragers logo.
  Not photos of the nursery.
- 016, 073, 074: newsletter and poster graphics with text.
- 030 to 044: Google Maps tiles (third-party, and no map embeds).
- 001, 008: the nursery logo and a placeholder avatar. The logo was not
  reused; the header mark is our own ladybird sticker.

Children's artwork: the site has no scans of children's paintings, so the
example uses the template's own stickers and paper-cut icons instead (the
"no artwork" fallback in BRIEF.md). Ask the nursery for 3 to 6 scans.
