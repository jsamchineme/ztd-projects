Worries and complaints

If something isn’t right

Please tell us, so we can put it right.

  1. Talk to us

    Speak to the staff in your child’s room, or to our manager, Naghmana Sajjad.

  2. Make a complaint

    If that doesn’t sort it out, ask us for our complaints procedure.

  3. The Care Inspectorate

    You can also complain to the Care Inspectorate at any time. Complain to the Care Inspectorate

Safety

Keeping children safe

All our staff are trained in child protection as part of their induction.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.

If you have a worry about a child’s safety, speak to the manager straight away. Ask us for a copy of any of our policies.
