Before you come

Questions to ask on your visit

Write them down and bring them along. A good nursery is glad to answer every one.

  • Who will my child’s key person be?
  • What are your staff ratios in each room?
  • How does settling in work, and how long does it usually take?
  • What are your opening hours and session times?
  • What will my child eat, and how do you handle allergies?
  • How will you tell me about my child’s day?
  • How do you support children with additional needs?
  • How do funded hours work here?

The first chapter

Settling in, from hello to home time

Every child is different, so settling in goes at your child’s pace. Ask us how the sessions work and how long they usually take.

  1. Chapter 1

    Hello

    Phone or email us to arrange a visit.

    Come and see our three rooms and two gardens, and ask us anything.

  2. Chapter 2

    Saying yes

    Spaces are very limited, so ask early. A £100 registration fee secures a place.

    Children receiving the 1,140 funded hours don’t pay it.

  3. Chapter 3

    Settling in

    Every child is different and settles at their own pace.

    Ask us how settling-in sessions work here, and how long they usually take.

  4. Chapter 4

    The first full day

    Meals, snacks and milk are part of the day, and included in your fees.

    Most days there’s time in one of our two gardens, whatever the weather.

  5. Chapter 5

    Home time

    At home time, the team tells you about your child’s day.

    Tired, fed and full of things to tell you.
