Funded hours

Funded hours for 3 and 4 year olds

We’re in partnership with the City of Edinburgh Council, so eligible children can use their funded hours with us.

1,140

hours a year of funded early learning and childcare for eligible children. It’s free to you.

£0

registration fee for children receiving the 1,140 funded hours.

You apply for funded hours through the council, using its own process and deadlines.

Every funded session includes a free meal for your child.

Find early learning and childcare on the council website

How it works here

Phone us to check which days and sessions you can use your funded hours for, and the cost of any extra hours.

When funding starts

The term after their 3rd birthday

Funding usually starts the term after your child turns 3. The council sets the exact dates.

2 year olds

Some 2 year olds get funded hours too

About a quarter of 2 year olds can get funded hours. It usually depends on the benefits your family gets, or if your child or a parent has experience of care.

Phone us to check if we offer funded places for 2 year olds.

  • Check if your 2 year old is eligible on mygov.scot

Paying

Paying your fees

When
Monthly, in advance, on or before the 1st of each month.
How
By standing order, or with Childcare Vouchers.
Weeks
We’re open 51 weeks a year and close for one week over Christmas. Your yearly and monthly fees reflect this.

Help with childcare costs

The government adds £2 for every £8 you pay in. Ask us if we accept it.
