If your child is ill

Phone us to say your child is off: 01224 741175.

We follow NHS guidance on how long children should stay at home. Ask us for a copy.

Sickness or diarrhoea

Keep your child at home until 48 hours after the last time they were sick or had diarrhoea.

Drop-off and what to wear

We go outside every day, whatever the weather.

Park in our car park

Please don't park on the street outside. It's a hazard for cars turning in.

Staff on the doors

A member of staff is on the door at drop-off and home time.

Every handover

Ask about food, naps and nappies. Staff tell you at every pick-up.

What to wear
  • A waterproof suit or jacket
  • Wellies
  • A hat, a scarf and gloves
  • Your child's name on everything

Paying fees

Invoices come monthly, in advance. Pay by bank transfer by the 6th of the month.

Our bank details are on your invoice. There's a £25 charge for each day a payment is late.

All our payment terms

Need to change your days?

Give us one month's notice in writing. Extra sessions depend on space and staffing, so ask the office.

admin@kingswelliesnursery.com

Staying in touch

You'll get a newsletter each term, emails from the office, and a chat at every pick-up.

We also post news and staff changes on our Facebook and Instagram pages. Staff photo boards are in reception and outside each room.

Stay and play

Several times a year we invite families in to play. Dates are in the newsletter.
