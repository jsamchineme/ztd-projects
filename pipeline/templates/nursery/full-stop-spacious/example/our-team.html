Your child's key person

Every child has a key person. They plan settling in with you, and they're who you talk to day to day.

For babies and toddlers, the key person feeds, changes and comforts them. When staff are off, our own team covers, or staff from our other Wellies nurseries.

Ask us about our staff ratios in each room when you visit.

Qualified and checked

All our staff are registered with the Scottish Social Services Council (SSSC) and checked through Disclosure Scotland's PVG scheme.

All our staff keep training on the job, to build their skills and qualifications.

What the letters mean

SSSC

The Scottish Social Services Council. Nursery staff must register with it. Anyone can check the register.

PVG

Protecting Vulnerable Groups. A Disclosure Scotland check for people who work with children.

SVQ and HNC

Scottish Vocational Qualifications and Higher National Certificates. These are the usual childcare qualifications for nursery staff.

PGCE, PGDE and SQH

Teaching qualifications, and the Scottish Qualification for Headship for head teachers.
