Funded hours for 3 and 4 year olds

Every 3 and 4 year old in Scotland can get up to 1,140 hours a year of funded early learning and childcare. The Scottish Government pays.

1,140funded hours a year, from August to July. That's about 30 hours a week in term time.
£0to you, for the hours that are funded.

We have a contract with Moray Council to provide funded hours. Your child can use some or all of their hours with us.

How to use them here

  1. Ask us for a place, and choose your sessions.

  2. Register for funded hours with Moray Council, on its website or by email or phone.

  3. Pay us for anything the funded hours don't cover, such as extra hours.

2 year olds

Some 2 year olds can get funded hours too, depending on the family's benefits or experience of care.

Eligible 2 year olds can use their funded hours with us. Some may have a longer day than the Little Saplings morning: phone us to check.

If your 2 year old isn't eligible, you pay for their Little Saplings sessions.

Check if your 2 year old is eligible

Little Saplings, 2 to 3

  • 9am to 1pm, Monday to Friday, term time
  • A snack included
  • A free set of waterproofs

What's included

Outdoor nursery comes with the outdoors.

  • Lunch and snacks in a full day for children aged 3 and over
  • A snack in every Little Saplings morning
  • A free set of waterproofs for Little Saplings
  • The woods, fields and meadow of Duffus Estate, every session
  • Staff trained in outdoor and paediatric first aid, and food hygiene
