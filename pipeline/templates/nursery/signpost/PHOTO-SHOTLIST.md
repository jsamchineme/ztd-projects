# Signpost: photos and film to ask the nursery for

Signpost needs the fewest photos of any nursery template: the boards, drawings and colour fields carry the page. These make it better, in this order. **No child's face in any shot.** Hands, backs, toys, food and empty rooms are fine.

## Must have
1. **The building front or entrance**, in daylight, from where a parent first sees it (the gate, the path). This replaces the drawn "You are here" scene on the homepage. Landscape, at least 1600px wide.
2. **The way in**: the footpath or gate from the street, so the "Find us" section can show it.
3. **The main playroom, empty and tidy**, shot from a corner at child height, lights on. Landscape.
4. **The garden, empty**: one wide shot that shows its size and the best features.

## Nice to have
5. Each other room (quiet room, family room), empty.
6. Lunch or snack as served: a plate or the snack table from above.
7. Children's artwork on the wall, with no names visible.
8. Hands at work: painting, planting, building, pouring.
9. Coat pegs and wellies (no name labels showing).

## Film (optional)
- **"The way in" tour, 30 to 60 seconds:** walk from the gate or front door, through the hall into the empty rooms and out to the garden. No children, no music needed. Phone held landscape, slow and steady.
- **Living photo, 6 to 10 seconds:** a locked-off shot of the garden or a sunny empty room (leaves moving, light changing). Landscape, no people.
- Send the original files. We self-host them small; we never embed YouTube or Vimeo.

## Staff
- Head-and-shoulders photos only with each person's own written consent, on a plain background in daylight. Name and role for each.

## Practical
- Originals straight from the phone or camera, not screenshots or Facebook downloads.
- Tell us which room each photo shows.
- We strip location data from every file before it goes on the site.
