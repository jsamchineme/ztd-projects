Funded hours

We're a funded provider for Renfrewshire Council, and have been since 1999. Children aged 3 and 4 can have up to 1,140 funded hours a year. That's up to 30 hours a week in term time.

Funded hours are free to you. Our sessions are flexible, so you can use your hours when you need them.

Paying

Fees are paid monthly in advance, by cash, cheque or bank transfer.

You can give the snack donation in cash, by standing order or bank transfer, or with any extra-hours fees.

Ask us about paying with Tax-Free Childcare.

5

Help with costs

  • Tax-Free Childcare: the government adds £2 for every £8 you pay in.
  • Universal Credit can pay back some of your childcare costs.
  • Scottish Child Payment is a weekly payment for families on some benefits.

Not sure what you can get? Phone us and we'll point you in the right direction.
