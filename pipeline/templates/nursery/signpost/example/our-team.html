2

Key workers

Every child has a key worker. They have the main responsibility for your child, plan their learning and keep you up to date.

At pick-up you collect your child from their key worker, so you can hear how the day went.

3

Training and registration

  • Our staff are registered with the Scottish Social Services Council (SSSC).
  • We're a Living Wage employer.
  • All staff are trained in first aid.
  • This year the team trained in The Promise, Book Creator, Talk for Writing and Bookbug leadership.
4

Staff ratios

We work at 1 adult to 8 children at all times. On outings it's 1 adult to 2 children, which is why we ask parents to help on trips.

5

Students and volunteers

Students, relief staff and volunteers have PVG checks before they start. A volunteer is never left in charge of a group of children.
