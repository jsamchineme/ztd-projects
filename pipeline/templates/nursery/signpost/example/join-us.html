Book a visit

Phone us on 01505 704839 or email enquiries@kilbarchancn.com, and we'll arrange a time for you to come and look round.

Bring your questions. Ask us about staff, lunches, the garden and how settling in works.

What's here

  • Big garden
  • Quiet room
  • Family room

The garden, quiet room and family room are ours. Children also use the primary school's gym hall and basketball area.

2

Find us

Meadside Avenue, Kilbarchan, Johnstone PA10 2LA. We're a building of our own in the grounds of Kilbarchan Primary School.

  • Use the footpath from the road. We call it the yellow brick road.
  • Please don't park in or walk through the staff car park. The only exceptions are breakfast club before 8:30am and collecting after 4pm.
  • At busy times a voluntary one-way system runs from Shuttle Street, along Meadside Avenue and down Wheatlands Farm Road.
  • Please use the crossing places marked on the road.

Open the address in a map app

Open 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, in term time

3

Who can come

We care for children from age 3 until they start school.

You can apply after your child's 2nd birthday. Children usually start the month after their 3rd birthday.

We also run a breakfast club and after-school care for children at Kilbarchan Primary School.

4

Applying for a place

Funded places are given out in line with Renfrewshire Council's admissions policy. We try to give you your first choice of hours. When a session is very popular, places are decided by a ballot at a panel meeting.

You can collect an application form from the nursery. Renfrewshire Council also takes applications online. Phone us to check which to use this year.

Nurseries and early learning on Renfrewshire Council's website

5

Settling in

  1. We meet you for an enrolment session at the end of the day, when the nursery is quiet.
  2. Your child comes in for a short play before their start date.
  3. You both meet your child's key worker, who looks after them day to day.

You're welcome to stay and join in while your child gets to know us.

6

What to bring

  • A small backpack with a full change of clothes. Socks are especially useful.
  • Soft shoes for indoors.
  • Wellies and outdoor clothes. We go outside in all weathers.
  • A hat, scarf and gloves in winter, and a sunhat in summer. Please put sun cream on before nursery.

Please put your child's name on everything.
