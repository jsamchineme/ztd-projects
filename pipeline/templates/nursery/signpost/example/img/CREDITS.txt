Image and video credits: Signpost worked example (Kilbarchan Community Nursery)
=================================================================================

Every photo comes from the nursery's own website, kilbarchancn.com, crawled on
14 October 2026. All were cropped so that no child can be identified: only hands,
the backs of sleeves, toys, food and empty spaces remain. Each crop was checked at
full size. EXIF and all other metadata were stripped (re-encoded with Pillow).
Sizes: WebP at 400px and 800px wide (the sources are 2020-21 phone photos).
