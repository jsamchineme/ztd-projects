Keeping in touch

We share your child's learning, photos and news on the Famly app. You can add your own comments and photos too.

We also send a monthly newsletter and emails, and post notices on the boards inside and outside the building. Chat to your child's key worker at pick-up.

There's also a parents' group on Facebook.

3

Drop-off and pick-up

A member of staff meets your child at the door. They change their shoes, hang up their coat and wash their hands before going into the playroom.

Only adults named on your enrolment form can collect your child. Tell us and the key worker if that changes. The door has an entry system, so please buzz and wait.

4

Illness and medicines

When to keep your child at home

Please keep your child at home if they have vomiting, diarrhoea, conjunctivitis, a rash, a raised temperature, or a cough or cold. They should stay home for 48 hours after the last episode.

If your child becomes unwell at nursery, we'll phone you to collect them.

Medicines

Tell us if your child takes any medicine. We can only give medicine labelled with your child's name and an expiry date, after you fill in a permission form.

Accidents and bumps

All our staff are trained in first aid. We record any accident and ask you to sign the record at pick-up. We always tell you about a bump to the head.

5

Get involved

We're a community nursery, run by a board of parent trustees elected at our yearly general meeting. Any parent or family member can become a member, free.

Last year our families raised just over £6,500 for new resources. You can join a working group, help with fundraising, or come on outings.
