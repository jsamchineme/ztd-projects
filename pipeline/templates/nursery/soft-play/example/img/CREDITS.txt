Soft Play worked example: Leaps and Bounds Nursery, 23 Clarence Street, Paisley PA1 1PX
Every photo is the nursery's own, downloaded from https://labnursery.co.uk/ on 14 October 2026
by pipeline/crawl.mjs. All are photos of EMPTY rooms and the garden. No child appears in any of them.

Processing for every file: re-saved with Pillow (all EXIF and other metadata stripped), resized to
400, 800 and (where the source allowed) 1200px wide, WebP quality 72 plus a JPEG fallback.

Blurring: several room walls carry pinned family photos and children's display photos in which small
faces are visible at full size. Those wall areas were softly blurred (feathered Gaussian blur) so no
face can be made out, in: big-room, dining-room, baby-room, cosy-room, block-play, quiet-room,
book-corner (small frames), dress-up (framed photos on the wall). Nothing else was altered.
