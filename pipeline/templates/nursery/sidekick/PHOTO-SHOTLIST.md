# Sidekick: photo shot list

Sidekick is built to look finished with **no photos at all**: the mascot carries the warmth. Photos make it better, and these are the ones that earn their place. Give this list to the nursery, or use it on a 30-minute visit after hours.

## The rule that overrides everything

- **No identifiable children.** No faces, no names, no name pegs, no nappies, changing, bathing or sleeping, and no uniform logo together with a face.
- Children **from behind**, at a distance, or **hands only** are fine.
- **Staff** only with their own written consent.
- Turn off location on the phone, or strip metadata before upload. Shoot landscape unless a shot says otherwise.

## Must have (the homepage strip and the gallery groups)

| # | Shot | Gallery group | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Each room, empty and tidied, from the doorway at child height | Rooms | One per room. Lights on, blinds open, clutter away. |
| 2 | The best corner of each room (book nook, home corner, sensory light panel) | Rooms | Close and colourful. |
| 3 | The garden from the back door | Garden | Wide. Include the sky. |
| 4 | Growing beds, planters, the mud kitchen | Garden | A watering can or wellies in frame helps. |

## Video (for the two video components)

- **Living photo:** 6 to 10 seconds, phone held still on a surface or very slow pan: leaves moving in the garden, a mobile turning, rain on the window, the sensory light panel. No children in frame. We loop it, mute it and keep it under 1.5 MB.
- **Tour film:** 20 to 45 seconds, a slow walk through the empty rooms and the garden after hours. Landscape. No talking needed; if the manager wants to speak, film her in an empty room and we add captions and a transcript.

If the nursery can't film, we make the living photo as an honest slow pan across one real photo, and the tour as a short film of real photos (as in the Jaybees example). Both are labelled for what they are.
