Funded hours

3 and 4 year olds in Scotland can get up to 1,140 hours a year of early learning and childcare, free to you.

We're a partner provider for West Lothian Council, so you can use your funded hours here. They're open to children whether or not you live in West Lothian.

We offer 4 different session patterns, depending on whether you need mornings, afternoons or full days. Phone us to talk them through. Every child at a funded session gets a free meal.

How to apply

You apply to your local council. If you live in West Lothian, apply on the council's website for funding to attend a partner nursery like us.

Apply to West Lothian Council

Help with costs

  • Tax-Free Childcare

    We're registered for Tax-Free Childcare. The government adds £2 for every £8 you pay in. We also accept childcare vouchers.

    Tax-Free Childcare on GOV.UK

  • Universal Credit

    If you get Universal Credit, you may be able to get back up to 85% of your childcare costs.
