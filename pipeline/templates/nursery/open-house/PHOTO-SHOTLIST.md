# Open House: photo shot list

Open House is drawn first and photographed second. It looks finished with **no photos at all** (the drawn house, the drawn rooms). Real photos make it better. Ask the nursery for these, in this order. Every one must be free of identifiable children: empty rooms, or children from behind, at a distance, or hands only.

**Never:** children's faces, names (pegs, artwork labels, coat hooks), nappies, changing, sleeping, uniforms with faces, stock or AI images. Strip metadata from every file (`img/CREDITS.txt` records the source and every excluded photo).

| # | Shot | Where it goes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The front door or entrance**, straight on, door open if possible, daylight | Home "Come in" (house-shaped frame), Visit and join band, tour poster | The most important shot. Square or portrait. Leave sky above the door: the frame cuts a pitched top off the upper 22%. |
| 2 | **The building front** from across the street | Optional: Visit and join, "Find us" | Lets parents recognise it on the day. Also tells us the real door colour for `--h-door`. |
| 3 | **Each playroom, empty**, from the doorway, at child eye height | Our nursery rooms, Gallery "Rooms" | One per room. Lights on, toys out, tidy. |
| 4 | **The kitchen, or a plate of lunch as served** | Our nursery "Food", Gallery | No staff faces unless they consent in writing. |
| 5 | **The garden or outdoor areas, empty** | Our nursery "Outdoors", Gallery "Garden" | Wide shot plus one detail (mud kitchen, planters). |
| 6 | **Hands at work**: painting, dough, planting, building | Gallery "Play" | Hands and table only. Check nothing in frame shows a name. |
| 7 | **Children's artwork** on a wall or table | Gallery | Blur or crop out any name labels. |
| 8 | **Staff**, only with each person's own written consent | Our team (replaces the drawn frame with initials) | Head and shoulders, plain background, natural light. |
| 9 | **10 to 20 seconds of video per room, empty**, phone held still or walking slowly from the door in | The "Come in" tour film | Landscape. No sound needed: we caption it. Start outside the front door. |

Sizes: send the largest originals. We crop to square (400 and 800px), 16:10 for video posters, and WebP plus JPEG.
