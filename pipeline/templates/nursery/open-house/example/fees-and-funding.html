Funded hours

We offer the Scottish Government's funded early learning and childcare for 3 to 5 year olds, and for eligible 2 year olds. That's up to 1,140 hours a year, free to you.

We spread the hours evenly across the year. That works out at 23 hours and 45 minutes a week.

Funded hours on mygov.scot

When funding starts

  • 3 to 5 year olds: from the Monday after their third birthday. It's applied automatically.
  • Eligible 2 year olds: from the term after their second birthday. You need to apply, and we're happy to help.
Which 2 year olds are eligible?

A 2 year old can get funded hours if the family gets certain benefits, such as Universal Credit (with take-home pay under a set amount), or if the child or a parent has experience of care.

See the full list on mygov.scot

Extra hours and fees

Phone us for our current fees.

Need more than the funded hours? You can pay to make a morning or afternoon session longer, or to stay for a full day.

Funded and paid sessions come together in one monthly payment, spread evenly across the year.

Other help with costs

  • Tax-Free Childcare: for every £8 you pay in, the government adds £2. Ask us about paying this way.
  • Universal Credit: you may get back up to 85% of your childcare costs.
  • Students: your college or university may help with childcare costs.
