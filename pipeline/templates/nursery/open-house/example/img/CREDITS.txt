Open House worked example: My Second Home Nursery, 50 Tweedsmuir Road, Cardonald, Glasgow G52 2RX
Every photo below is the nursery's own, taken from its current website (https://mysecondhomenursery.co.uk/,
pages /, /about-us and /programs) on 14 October 2026. Files were cropped inside the site's black rounded
frame, resized to 400px and 800px (WebP and JPEG) and re-saved with no metadata (no EXIF, no location).
This is a sales mock-up: the nursery must confirm it is happy for these photos to be reused before launch.

EXCLUDED (and why)
- Two children at a craft table ("Potions" sign), 3 versions: identifiable faces and uniform with nursery name.
- Child sitting on the floor among blocks (full photo): identifiable face (only the empty shelf crop is used).
- Child holding a magnet; child with a wooden box: identifiable faces, nursery T-shirts.
- Baby on a sensory light mat; baby by rope lights (full photo): identifiable children.
- Children doing yoga (with "Qualified early years yoga instructor" badge): identifiable faces.
- Group of children jumping outdoors: identifiable faces (and looks like a stock image).
- Child in a graduation gown; nativity scene with children: identifiable children.
- Staff member in a Cat in the Hat costume at graduation: a person, no confirmed consent for reuse; not needed.
- Adult and child in the garden with tyres: child identifiable from the front.
- "Parent Pack" and "Top 10 checklist" mock-ups, icons, logos (Care Inspectorate, Education Scotland, SSSC,
  Scottish Government, Glasgow City Council): marketing graphics or third-party logos. Never use the CI logo.
- "Best Golden Tour Award 2025" and "Steampunks" badges: third-party marketing badges, not used.
- The nursery's own logo (a hand-drawn house): not used in the example; the drawn house mark is ours. If the
  nursery wants its logo, put it in the header in place of #house-mark.

No garden, kitchen, food or building-front photos exist on the current site, so the example uses the template's
drawings for those (#room-garden, #room-kitchen and the #house-villa front).
