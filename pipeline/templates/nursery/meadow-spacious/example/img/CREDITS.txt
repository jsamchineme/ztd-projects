Photo credits and sources: Bardykes Farm Nursery School worked example (Meadow template)
Built 13 October 2026. Every photo is the nursery's own, taken from its current website
(bardykes.co.uk, crawled 13 October 2026). None shows a child.

How each file was made
- Downloaded at the original size the site links to, rotated by its EXIF tag, then saved
  as WebP (quality 62) at 400px and 800px wide (staff: 240px and 480px). Saving re-encodes
  the pixels only, so all EXIF, GPS and camera metadata is stripped.
- carrots-corn: cropped 14% from the left to remove a small figure at the edge.
- Several animal photos (puppies, horse, dachshund and others) carry or come from a
  "Cameron-Laird Photography" shoot (the watermark is visible on the puppies photo).
  Confirm with the nursery that it may reuse them on a new site.

EXCLUDED, and why
- Every photo that shows a child, from the front, side or behind, even at a distance:
  the community, charity and sustainability pages (img 010, 012, 015-019, 022-027),
  the guinea pigs with two girls (our-animals, "Ginger & Snap"), the woodwork photos
  (gallery 079, 080), the calf with a child (our-animals), the river and field photos
  with children (gallery), the parties, picnic, Easter, sports day and Halloween photos,
  the pocket-garden bath photos (news 065, 067), and the testimonials banners (094-096).
  Rule: no identifiable children, and we treat any old-site photo with a child as excluded.
- Award and partner logos (Care Inspectorate, Education Scotland, SSSC, South Lanarkshire
  Council, Eco-Schools, Living Wage, Sun Safe, nmt Nursery Awards, daynurseries Top 20):
  never use the Care Inspectorate logo; the others need the owners' permission.
- Scottish Nursery Awards 2025 ceremony photos (news): adults at a private event; not needed.
- Shop product photos (soft toys, hoodies): stock product shots, not the nursery.
- The Halloween poster (news/events): an illustration with ghosts, not the nursery's own photo.
- The pocket garden certificate (news 068): text in an image; the fact is written in HTML instead.
- YouTube videos (home, sustainability, bee blog): third-party embeds (GDPR); not reused.
