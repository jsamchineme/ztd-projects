Book a visit

The best way to choose a nursery is to see it. Phone or email us and we’ll find a time to show you round the farm, the playrooms and the animals.

Things you might ask on your visit

  • Which room will my child be in, and who will be their key worker?
  • What are your staff ratios?
  • What will my child eat, and how do you handle allergies?
  • How do children stay safe around the animals?

How to join

Our website says we have places for 2 to 5 year olds in 2026 to 2027, and baby room places for children up to 2. Phone to check what’s free now.

  1. Visit and talk to us

    Ask us about places and the days you need.

  2. Fill in our application form

    It takes about 30 minutes. Ask us for the link.

  3. Send us your documents

    Email a copy of your child’s birth certificate, 2 proofs of address and 1 photo ID.

  4. Pay the registration fee

    We hold a place once you pay the £80 fee and agree a contract with the nursery manager.

Funded hours

If your child is 3 or 4, or an eligible 2 year old, apply to South Lanarkshire Council for funded hours too.

Read about funded hours

Sessions

  • Opening hours: 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, all year.
  • Days: your child comes at least 2 days a week.
  • Changing your days: give us 1 month’s notice.
  • Half days: phone to check.

Settling in

Every child settles at their own pace. Ask us how we’ll help your child get to know their key worker, the rooms and the animals.

Finding us

Bardykes Farm, Bardykes Road, Blantyre G72 9UJ.

  1. Follow the postcode to Bardykes Road, then turn onto Callaghan Wynd, near the West End Bar.
  2. Take the first left onto Herbertson Grove.
  3. Turn sharp right into the farm, through 2 big white gates and down a long drive.
  4. The nursery is to the right of the main farmhouse. Park for free outside.
