# Make Waves: photo and film shot list

Give this to the nursery (or take it yourself on a visit). Make Waves shows photos in **ringed circles** and a **square-ish mosaic**, so every shot must still work when cropped to a circle: keep the subject in the middle third.

## Rules first

- **No identifiable children.** No faces, no names on pegs, trays or artwork, nobody being changed, bathed or asleep. Children from behind, far away, or just hands are fine.
- **Staff** only with their own written consent, recorded in `img/CREDITS.txt`.
- Shoot in daylight, lights on, blinds open. Tidy the room first, but leave the toys out: lived-in looks better than empty.
- Landscape or square, at least **1600px** on the long side. We export WebP at 400 and 800px.
- Turn off location on the phone, or we strip it anyway.

## The list (12 to 16 photos)

| # | Shot | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The building from the gate, whole front in frame | Gallery "Building", Our nursery head |
| 2 | The entrance or front door, close | Gallery "Building" |
| 3–5 | Each room, wide, from the doorway corner | Room cards, rings, gallery "Rooms" |
| 6 | One detail per room: a book corner, a tray of loose parts, a sensory table | Rings, gallery |
| 7 | The garden, wide | Living photo, gallery "Garden" |
| 8 | One piece of outdoor kit close up (mud kitchen, slide, planters) | Rings |
| 9 | **The local view** from the garden or a window (the sea, river, hills or street that the theme comes from) | Hero or page head, gallery |
| 10 | Lunch as served, from above, on a nursery plate | Gallery "Food" |
| 11 | Snack table set out | Gallery "Food" |
| 12 | Children's artwork on a wall (no names visible) | Gallery "Artwork" |
| 13 | Hands at work: painting, planting, building (no faces) | Gallery |

## Film (optional, for the two video components)

- **Living photo (6 to 10 seconds):** a phone on a still surface filming the garden, a room with fairy lights, or the view. No children in frame, no sound. We trim, loop and compress it to under 1.5 MB.
- **Tour (20 to 45 seconds):** walk slowly through the empty rooms and out into the garden in one take, phone held level. No children, no talking (we add captions). We compress it to under 4 MB.
- If there's no film, we make both from photos with a slow pan and label them honestly ("A slow pan across one photo", "made from still photos, not film"), as the Oban example does.
