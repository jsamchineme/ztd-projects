  1. The nursery: an old stone school from 1888, with a newer wing beside it.
  2. The entrance, with a gate and planters. The sign says “Please shut the gate”.
  3. A bright conservatory playroom with a glass roof, soft mats and baby chairs.
  4. A playroom with a red floor, a book corner and rainbow drapes at the window.
  5. A playroom with skylights, small wooden tables and a coloured-pencil mural.
  6. A playroom with tall windows, blue curtains and a music table.
  7. A playroom with a home corner, a dolls’ house and a big tree painting.
  8. A washroom with low sinks and a skylight.
  9. The playground: a soft red surface, picnic tables, tyres and a climbing frame.
  10. The tree house and slide. You can see the bay through the trees.
  11. The big garden: grass, a play tent, a climbing dome and a mature tree.
