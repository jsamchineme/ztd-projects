How visits work

A visit is the best way to see if outdoor nursery suits your child.

  1. Phone or email us

    Tell us your child's age and the days you'd like. Ask for Kristy, our nursery manager.

  2. We'll arrange a time

    Phone to check which days and times we show families round.

  3. Come and look round

    Wear boots and a warm coat. See where your child would play, and ask us anything.

Questions to ask on your visit

Every parent of an outdoor nursery wonders about the same things. Ask us these, and anything else.

  • What happens on the coldest, wettest and windiest days?
  • Where do children eat, nap and go to the toilet?
  • What kit do we need to buy, and what do you provide?
  • Who will be my child's key worker?
  • How many staff are with the children each day?
  • How do you share my child's day and learning with me?
  • How does settling in work, and how long does it take?
  • How do funded hours work with your sessions?

Parent Club, the Scottish Government's site for parents, has more things to consider when choosing a nursery.

Settling in

Starting outdoor nursery is a big step. We'll plan it with you, at your child's pace.

  • We get to know your child first. You'll fill in information about your child before they start, and staff use it.
  • Key workers. Parents told the inspector they talk through their child's learning with their key workers.
  • Learning journals. We keep a journal for each child, and you can add to it.
  • Ask us how settling-in sessions work and how long they usually take.

I feel like all staff have read the information I filled in before my son joined, as all staff have made specific comments that reflect the information in there.

A parent, in our Care Inspectorate report, February 2025
