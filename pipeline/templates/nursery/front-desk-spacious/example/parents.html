If your child is off or ill

Tell your child's key worker by 10am. Say why, and when you expect them back.

Phone or text: 07906 954441

If you know in advance, tell us in writing. If we haven't heard by 10am, we'll phone or email you. This is part of keeping children safe.

Illness and medicines

Keep your child at home if they are ill, so it doesn't spread.

Sickness or diarrhoea

Keep your child off until 48 hours after the last time they were sick or had diarrhoea.

Heavy colds and infections

Keep your child at home with a heavy cold, a virus or anything infectious.

Medicines

We only give medicine when it's needed, with a plan you sign first. Speak to your key worker.

Ill at nursery

We phone you or your emergency contacts, and look after your child until someone comes.

Accidents

We write down every accident, and ask you to sign it at pick-up. In an emergency we call an ambulance and stay with your child.

Fees when your child is off

Fees are still due. We staff the nursery for every child we expect.

Drop-off and collection

The front door is staffed from 9am to 9:30am and from 2:45pm to 3pm. At other times, ring the bell.

Morning drop-off
From 9am. Come into the hall and say goodbye.
Morning collection
From 11:40am, and by midday
Afternoon drop-off
Wait outside the door. We let you in at midday.
Home time
3pm, at the playroom door
Running late?
Children can still come in. There is no last entry time.
Someone else collecting?

Tell us in advance, or phone 07906 954441. In an emergency, give them your password. You agree it with us at your first visit.

Late to collect?

Phone us, so we can reassure your child. Up to 10 minutes late costs £5. After that, you pay for 2 staff to stay.

If no one comes and we can't reach you or your contacts, we must call the duty social worker.

Lunch and menus

Hot lunch is at 12:30pm. We send out the menu in advance.

Ask us if you need this week's menu again.

  • Lunch is free for children using funded hours.
  • You can send a packed lunch instead. Please add an ice pack.
  • We'll tell you if your child hasn't eaten well. Uneaten packed lunch comes home, so you can see what they liked.

Paying fees

We send your invoice on the 20th. Fees are due on the 1st of the next month.

Pay online, using the details on your invoice. Or pay into the nursery account at a Bank of Scotland counter. Fees paid after the 7th cost £25 more.

Worries and complaints

Talk to us about anything, big or small. We can usually meet you the same day.

Speak to any of us at drop-off or pick-up, or phone or email. We have an office for private chats.

Email the manager: manager@clarkstonnursery.co.uk

Making a complaint

  1. Write to our chairperson

    Mark your letter as a formal complaint. If the chairperson is away, write to the treasurer or secretary.

  2. We reply within 2 weeks

    The chairperson looks into it and replies within a fortnight.

  3. Still not happy?

    We'll tell you what you can do next. You can also complain to the Care Inspectorate at any time.

Keeping safe, and our policies

Every child has the right to feel safe. All our staff must act if they think a child is at risk.

Our child protection coordinator is Maureen McKendrick, our manager. Every member of staff has child protection training each year. We work with social work, health services and Police Scotland.

Ask us for a copy of our child protection policy, or make an appointment to talk to Maureen.

If a child is in danger right now, call 999.
