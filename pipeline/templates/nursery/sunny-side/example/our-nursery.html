Rooms by age

Each room is set up for its age, so children are ready for the next stage.

  • TiddlersUnder 2

    Three spaces: a quiet room for rest and sleep, a play room for stories and songs, and a messy room for sand, water, dough and paint.

    1 adult to 3 children

  • Toddlers2 to 3 years

    An open-plan room with its own door to the garden. Ring games, songs and stories build confidence and attention.

    1 adult to 5 children

  • Biggies3 to 5 years

    An open-plan room with areas for every part of Curriculum for Excellence. Children choose topics and build a learning wall together.

    1 adult to 8 children

Outdoors

Our garden is safe and secure, and every age group uses it. We have wetsuits, so children can play outside in all weather.

Children plant, play in groups and enjoy the fresh air. Our custom-built learning shed has a clear roof, so they can hear the rain and watch the snow fall.

We also walk in the local area, visit the park and the library, and the library bus comes to us regularly.

How children learn

Children learn as they play, and they lead their own learning. We follow local and national guidance, with nurture at the heart of it.

Every child has a key worker. They get to know your child, meet with you regularly and keep your child’s personal learning journey.

Play covers maths and numeracy, literacy, and health and wellbeing.

Food

Children eat together, with staff sitting at the table. Phone us to check what to send for lunch, and what snacks we provide.

Tell us about any allergies or dietary needs when you visit. We’ll talk them through with you.
