Worries and complaints

  1. Speak to your child’s key worker or a manager. Most things are sorted out quickly.
  2. If you’re still unhappy, phone or email the nursery manager, Denise Rankin.
  3. You can also complain to the Care Inspectorate at any time.

Read how to complain to the Care Inspectorate

Keeping children safe

Staff check every room each day to spot and remove risks. CCTV covers the outside of the building, and our policy explains how we use it.

We teach children SIMOA: Safe, Inspect, Monitor, Observe, Act. The SIMOA elephant visits home to share it with families.

In an emergency, phone 999.

Our policies
