What do the letters after their names mean?
MB ChB, MBChB, MBBS, BMBS
Medical degrees: bachelor of medicine and surgery.
MB BCh BAO
A medical degree from Ireland: medicine, surgery and obstetrics.
MRCGP, nMRCGP
Member of the Royal College of General Practitioners. It means they passed the GP exams.
DRCOG
Diploma of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (pregnancy and women's health).
DFFP
Diploma of the Faculty of Family Planning (contraception).
MRCS, MRCS Ed, MRCS RCSEd
Member of the Royal College of Surgeons (Ed and RCSEd mean of Edinburgh).
DCH
Diploma in Child Health.
BSc (Hons), BMedSci
Science degrees taken before or alongside medicine.

We are a training practice

Since 1995, a new GP registrar has joined us every year. Registrars are fully qualified doctors learning to be GPs.

Some of their appointments are filmed for training. If you do not want this, ask for the camera to be switched off.

Appointments

Phone 01506 670027 from 8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, or ask at the reception desk.

Book a separate appointment for each person who needs to be seen.

Routine appointments

These are booked about 10 to 14 days ahead.

Urgent appointments for today

Some appointments are only released on the day, for people who are unwell and need to be seen that day.

If you cannot come, please tell us so someone else can have the appointment.

Home visits

If you are too ill to come in, phone before 10:30am. Tell us as much as you can, so we can judge how urgent it is.

We may not be able to visit the same day if you ask for a non-urgent visit after 11am. If you can travel, please come in.

Repeat prescriptions

Allow 48 hours, not counting weekends and public holidays. We do not take requests by phone.

  • Order online

    First, apply for online access. You need your own email address. Shared accounts are not allowed.

    We will email you a link from Patient Services. It works for 10 days, so check your junk mail.

    Apply for online access

    Order on the Patient Services website

  • Hand it in or post it

    Use the re-order slip from your last prescription, or write your request on paper.

    Hand it in at reception or post it to us. You or someone you trust can collect it.

    For your prescription to be posted back, give us a stamped, addressed envelope.

  • Collect it

    Collect from the practice, or ask for it to go to a pharmacy. Our online form lists Dears, Boots and Linlithgow Pharmacy.

    Going to a pharmacy takes longer. Ask your pharmacy how long.

If you are about to run out

We only rush medicines you must not miss, for example diabetes medicines, inhalers, angina sprays and epilepsy medicines. These will be ready by 5:30pm.

We will not issue urgent prescriptions for things you can buy in a shop or pharmacy.

If we are closed, take your repeat slip to your usual pharmacy. They can give you a small supply.

Medication reviews

If your review with a doctor or nurse is overdue, we will ask you to make an appointment.

Test results and sick notes

  • Test results

    Phone 01506 670027 after 11am for blood, urine and X-ray results. Our phones are busiest early in the morning.

    We only give results to you, unless you have agreed something else with us in writing.

  • Sick notes and fit notes

    Ill for 7 days or less? You do not need a note from us. Your employer can give you a self-certificate form (SC2).

    Ill for more than 7 days? Fill in our online form. A doctor may call you or ask you to come in.

    If you need another note for the same illness, use the same form.

    Ask for a sick or fit note

    Download the SC2 self-certificate form from GOV.UK

Register with us

You can join if you live in our practice area and will be here for more than 3 months.

  1. Check you live in our area. It covers Linlithgow, Linlithgow Bridge, Kingscavil, Bridgend, Philpstoun and Threemiletown. Read our exact boundary.
  2. Fill in a registration form. There is one for adults and one for children under 16. It includes a health questionnaire.
  3. Choose whether to sign up for online services. You can do this on the same form.

Our building
