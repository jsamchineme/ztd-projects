Funded hours

Funded early learning and childcare

We’re a Fife Council partner provider, so your child can use their funded hours here.

How it works

  • Children aged 3 to 5 can get up to 1,140 funded hours a year. That’s about 30 hours a week in term time. It’s free to you.
  • You apply through Fife Council, not through us. Councils set their own dates and deadlines.
  • Children can come on funded, paid or split places. A split place shares the hours with another setting.
  • A child who is 4 on the school start date can defer school and keep their funded hours.

Source: funded early learning and childcare on mygov.scot. It explains how to apply through your council.

Help with costs

Other help with childcare costs

These schemes are run by the UK and Scottish governments. Check the official pages for the latest amounts.

Tax-Free Childcare

The government adds £2 for every £8 you pay in, up to £2,000 a child each year. The nursery must be signed up.

Tax-Free Childcare on GOV.UK

Universal Credit

If you get Universal Credit, you may get back up to 85% of your childcare costs. The childcare must be registered.

Universal Credit and childcare

Scottish Child Payment

A weekly payment for families on some benefits, for each child under 16. It isn’t only for childcare.

Scottish Child Payment on mygov.scot
