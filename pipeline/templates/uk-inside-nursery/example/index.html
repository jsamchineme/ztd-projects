From the park to the woods

There’s no front door here. This is the walk your child will take each day, in real photos of our woodland.

  1. Setting off
  2. The willow arch and yurt
  3. Round the fire
  4. The woods
  1. 1

    Stop 1 of 4

    Setting off

    Each day starts in the park. Children arrive dressed for the weather, then we all walk up the hill to the woods.

    • Drop-off and pick-up are in the park. Phone to check where to meet.
    • Arrive fully dressed, so your child can go straight into play.
    • In winter, children need a head torch for the walk back down in the dark.

    Waterproofs over wellies, please Our walk has some wonderful puddles.

  2. 2

    Stop 2 of 4

    Through the willow arch: the yurt

    The yurt is our shelter from rain, snow and wind. Tarps give more cover around the woods.

    • The yurt has a chimney, so it can be warmed by a fire.
    • A blanket-lined tent for any child who needs a rest in a quiet corner.
    • Toilets and handwashing: ask us when you visit.
  3. 3

    Stop 3 of 4

    Round the fire

    Children help to gather sticks and build the fire, inside a fire safety circle. It’s where we warm up, eat and hear stories.

    • Lunch comes from home. In winter, a warm lunch in a flask is best.
    • We provide snack, often with fruit and vegetables the children grow.
    • An adult tells a story after lunch every day.
  4. 4

    Stop 4 of 4

    The woods

    Children play freely across the woods: building dens, climbing, digging in mud and planting.

    • 25 acres of woodland, north of Letham village.
    • Raised beds where children grow food for snack.
    • Staff stay close and calm, “like trees”: near enough to help, without taking over the play.

A day at nursery

A day in the woods, in every weather

We go out in rain, wind and snow. The children help decide what we need each day.

  1. MorningDrop-off in the parkArrive dressed for the weather. A warm breakfast first helps.
  2. MorningWalk up the hill togetherAt a good pace, so everyone warms up.
  3. MorningCircle time“What will we need today?” Children help plan for the weather.
  4. All dayFree play in the woodsDens, climbing, mud, fire and planting, led by the children’s ideas.
  5. MiddayLunch, then a storyLunch from home, by the fire or in the yurt. One adult tells a story.
  6. AfternoonPlay, yoga or an adventureSometimes to another part of the woods.
  7. End of dayBack down the hill to the parkHome time. In winter, we use head torches.

We run two groups with slightly different start and finish times. Phone to check your child’s times. On Fridays we close at 1pm.

How to start

We take new children all year round. The first step is a phone call or an email.

  1. Contact the officePhone 07732 048289 or email us. We’ll send you an information and enrolment pack.
  2. Visit the woodsCome with your child to see the nursery in action. They can be under 3 for a visit.
  3. Send back the enrolment packYour place on the waiting list is confirmed once the pack is complete.
  4. Start once your child is 3New children come for at least two days a week. Ask us how settling in works.
