Our story

  • 2008: the nursery opens in the woods near Letham, registered with the Care Inspectorate as a fully outdoor nursery.
  • Our founder, Cathy Bache, believed children should have space and freedom to play in nature. She was awarded the British Empire Medal in 2017, and died later that year.
  • Today, we share our way of working through visits and workshops for practitioners.
