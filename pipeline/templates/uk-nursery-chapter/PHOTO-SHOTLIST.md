# Chapter One: photo shot list

Chapter One is complete with drawings only. Photos make it real. Ask the nursery for these, or take them on a visit. **No children in any photo, ever**: not from behind, not at a distance, not hands. No name labels, pegs with names, nappies, changing areas or sleeping areas in use (research brief §5.2).

| # | Shot | Where it goes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The baby room**, tidy and empty, in soft daylight | First room chapter (`.room__art img`) | Low angle, at a baby's eye level. Treasure baskets and soft mats in frame. |
| 2 | **The toddler room**, tidy and empty | Second room chapter | Show the play areas set up: dough table, books, home corner. |
| 3 | **The pre-school room**, tidy and empty | Third room chapter | Show a project wall of children's art (no names visible). |
| 4 | **The sleep room**, empty cots | Our nursery, "Sleep" (add a section) | Only if the nursery is happy to show it. Never with a child in it. |
| 5 | **A cosy corner or reading nook** | Visit and join head, or settling in | The empty armchair is the image parents remember. |
| 6 | **A treasure basket**, close up, from above | Baby room chapter or food section | Natural objects: wooden spoons, pine cones, fabric. |
| 7 | **The garden**, both areas if there are two | Our nursery, "Outside in all weathers" | Include the gate and any covered area. Wet days are fine: that's the point. |
| 8 | **The front door** | Home "Find us" | So parents recognise it on the first visit. |
| 9 | **The manager**, a consented portrait | Home team section, Our team | Written consent recorded in `img/CREDITS.txt`. Plain background, natural light, no children. |

## Format
- Crop rooms and garden to 1:1 for room chapters (the offset tile is square) and 4:3 elsewhere.
- Save at 720px and 1200px, WebP (quality 60 to 65) plus JPEG (about 72). Strip all metadata.
- Below-the-fold `<img>` gets `loading="lazy"`, `width` and `height`, and real alt text ("The Baby Room, with treasure baskets on a soft rug").
- Log every photo, its source and consent in `example/img/CREDITS.txt`, and every rejected photo with the reason.
- A photo replaces the drawing in that slot. Keep the drawings everywhere else: they are the brand, not a stand-in.
