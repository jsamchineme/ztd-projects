Chapter One worked example: Cosy Cottage Nursery School, Northfield, Edinburgh
Images and their sources. Checked 13 October 2026.

PHOTOS USED
None. The nursery's current site (cosycottagenurseryschool.co.uk, crawled
13 October 2026) has no photographs of its rooms, gardens, food or staff, so
the example uses the template's drawn fallbacks only. Every illustration is
drawn in code in the HTML (inline SVG). None shows a person, face or hand.

IMAGES ON THE CURRENT SITE, AND WHY EACH WAS NOT USED
- wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Cosy-Cottage-Logo-01-1.jpg (the nursery's logo:
  a painted cottage with a red door, pink roof and a blossom tree, with the
  name in hand lettering). Not reused as an image: it is a 171 KB JPEG with
  the name baked in. Instead its cottage, red door, window grids, chimney pots
  and blossom tree were redrawn as the brand mark and the homepage hero scene.
  Confirm with the nursery that it is happy for its logo to be redrawn this way.
- wp-content/uploads/2018/11/icon-lock.png (parent login icon). Generic icon.
- wp-content/uploads/2018/11/icon-fb.png (Facebook icon). Generic icon; we
  don't link Facebook from the footer.
- The site's background (clouds, rainbow, a cartoon ladybird and bee, painted
  tree) is from its website theme, not the nursery's own artwork. Not used.
