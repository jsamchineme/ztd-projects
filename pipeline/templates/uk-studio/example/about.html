How we work

Why we practise this way

Polwarth Dental Clinic has looked after people in this part of Edinburgh since 1955.

When you come in, we want to get to know you first: what worries you, and what you’d like from your teeth and your smile.

We believe that when you understand your own dental health, you feel calmer and can choose the treatment that’s right for you. So we explain what we find, show you photos when they help, and give you a written plan and estimate before anything starts.

And because your time is precious, we try hard to see you on time.

The team at Polwarth Dental ClinicLed by Dr Ross Saunderson, principal dentist

  3. 03

  4. 04

    We see you on time

    Your time is precious. We work hard to see you at the time we gave you.

The team

The people who’ll look after you

Every dentist, therapist and nurse is registered with the General Dental Council (GDC).

Dentists and therapist

  • Dr Ross Saunderson

    Principal dentist

    GDC number 244748

    Ross qualified from the University of Aberdeen’s graduate entry programme in 2013, after a BSc in Anatomical Sciences at the University of Dundee.

    He holds a Postgraduate Diploma in Prosthodontics from the Tipton Training Academy, where he won the “Best Dentist” award for his year. He provides ClearCorrect aligners and has a particular interest in composite bonding and cosmetic work.

  • Dr Jenny Wylie

    Dentist

    GDC number 208887

    Jenny qualified from the University of Dundee in 2011 and is a member of the Faculty of Dental Surgery at the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh.

    She has worked in restorative dentistry, oral surgery and children’s dentistry, in hospital (King’s College London) and private practice. A mother of two, she helps adults and children relax.

  • Dr Andrew Jeffrey

    Implant dentist

    GDC number 83228

    Andrew qualified from the University of Dundee in 2004, then moved to Edinburgh for postgraduate training. He has trained across Europe in surgical and implant dentistry.

    He explains each step clearly and involves you in planning your treatment.

  • Karen Reid

    Dental therapist

    GDC number 297373

    Karen provides hygiene treatment, fillings and gum treatment, and is trained in the full range of children’s dentistry.

    She is from Scotland and gained most of her experience in the North East of England. She grew up as a competitive swimmer.
