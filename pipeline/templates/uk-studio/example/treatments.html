All treatments

Check-ups and your first assessment

From £56

A thorough check of your whole mouth, so we can spot small problems early and plan with you.

Price
Clinical examination £56. Full case assessment for new patients £60. X-rays £18.
What we check
Your fillings, crowns, bridges and implants, your gums, and a mouth cancer check. We may take photos inside your mouth to show you what we see.
Afterwards
You get a written, itemised treatment plan and an estimate before anything starts.

Call 0131 229 8143 to book

Hygiene visits

From £65

A deep clean of the places your brush can’t reach, which helps prevent gum disease and bad breath.

Price
Scale and polish with a dentist £65. Hygiene treatment with our dental therapist from £84.
Who you’ll see
Karen Reid, our dental therapist, or one of our dentists.
Good to know
If you have a bridge, denture, brace or implant, we’ll show you how to keep it clean.

Call 0131 229 8143 to book

White fillings and bonding

From £129

Tooth-coloured fillings that repair decay or damage almost invisibly. The same material can reshape teeth (bonding).

Price
White fillings from £129. Bonding: ask for a price, as it depends on how many teeth.
Old metal fillings
We can replace them with white fillings.
How long it lasts
A filling can last many years. Bonding can last from a few months to many years.

Call 0131 229 8143 to book

Teeth whitening

£325

Professional whitening, using trays made to fit your teeth.

Price
£325.
How it works
We check what kind of staining you have, then make custom trays. You can also whiten at home more gradually, wearing the trays overnight.
Results
Results vary. They depend on your starting colour and the kind of staining.

Call 0131 229 8143 to book

Teeth straightening (clear aligners)

Price on request

A series of clear aligners, each made for you, that move your teeth in small stages.

Price
Ask us for a price at your consultation.
How long it takes
It depends on your teeth. 6 to 12 months is typical.
Who you’ll see
Dr Ross Saunderson, who provides ClearCorrect aligners. We also offer Smilelign aligners.

Call 0131 229 8143 to book

Dental implants

Price on request

A fixed replacement for one or more missing teeth, or a way to stop dentures moving.

Price
Phone to book a consultation and ask for a price.
How it works
A titanium post is placed in the jaw. After a short healing time, we fit a crown, a bridge or a denture to it.
Who you’ll see
Dr Andrew Jeffrey, our implant dentist.

Call 0131 229 8143 to book

Crowns and porcelain veneers

From £660

Crowns protect and rebuild a weak or broken tooth. Veneers are thin porcelain covers that change how front teeth look.

Price
Crowns from £660. Porcelain veneers from £660. Inlays and onlays from £660.
Visits
Usually two. At the first we prepare the tooth and fit a temporary crown. At the second we fit the new one.
Materials
Our crowns are designed by computer (CAD CAM) and made from zirconia, a strong, metal-free ceramic.

Call 0131 229 8143 to book

Root canal treatment

From £375

Treatment to clear infection from inside a tooth so it can be saved, rather than taken out.

Price
Front tooth £375. Premolar £495. Molar £535.
Visits
Most courses take two or more visits.
