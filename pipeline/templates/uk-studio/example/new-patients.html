How to join

Four steps to your first visit

  1. Phone or email us

    Call 0131 229 8143 or email us. Tell us if you’re nervous or in pain.

  2. Pay a deposit

    We take a deposit to secure your first appointment. It isn’t refundable, and it comes off the cost of your appointment. Ask us how much it is when you book.

  3. Fill in two short forms

    A medical history form and a dental health check. You can do them when you arrive, or ask us for them before your visit.

  4. Come to your full case assessment

    This is your first appointment. It costs £60. See what happens below.

Your first visit

What happens at your assessment

  1. We talk first

    Your dentist asks about any worries you have, and what you’d like from your treatment.

  2. A thorough examination

    We check your fillings, crowns, bridges and implants, your gums, and do a mouth cancer check. We may take photos inside your mouth so you can see what we see.

  3. Your plan and estimate

    We explain what we found. You get a full, itemised treatment plan and an estimate of the cost.

  4. You decide

    Take your time. When you’re ready, your dentist will explain the ways to pay, including interest-free payment plans.

Questions people ask

Can I bring my children?

Yes. We encourage parents to bring children from an early age. Phone us to book and to ask about prices.

Can I get in with a wheelchair or pushchair?

Our treatment rooms are on the ground floor, and we can put out a ramp. Phone before your visit so it’s ready.

What if I need help urgently and I’m not a patient?

Phone us during opening hours. We try to see visitors the same day, Monday to Friday. We take a deposit to book, which comes off the cost of your treatment.

When we’re closed, phone NHS 24 on 111.

Not sure about something? Phone us and ask. We’re open Monday to Friday from 9am.

Nervous patients

Feeling anxious is common. Tell us.

Many people put off the dentist, often after a bad experience. We’ll go at your pace.

  • Talk to us first. Phone or email about any worries, even if you’re not a patient yet.
  • Nothing is a surprise. We talk through every treatment in detail, so you know what to expect.
  • Someone is always there. The same team looks after you from your first call through treatment and aftercare.

Membership plan

Spread the cost of routine care

You can join our membership plan at your first appointment. It starts at £21.97 a month.

  • An agreed number of check-ups and hygiene visits each year, and routine x-rays.
  • 10% off any other treatment you need, except implants and orthodontics.
  • Worldwide Dental Emergency Assistance, a scheme run by ICP. It may help with emergency treatment away from home or out of hours, treatment after an injury, hospital benefit and oral cancer. ICP decides each request.
  • Family discounts on every plan.

Pay monthly by direct debit. Our reception team will help you fill in the form.
