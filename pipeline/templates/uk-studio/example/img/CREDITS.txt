Photo credits: Polwarth Dental Clinic (worked example for the "Studio" template)

Every photo in this folder was published by Polwarth Dental Clinic on its own website,
www.polwarthdental.co.uk, and was downloaded on 12 October 2026 for a sales mock-up shown
to that practice. No stock or AI-generated images are used. Names and roles come from the
same pages that publish each portrait. Before going live, get the practice's written
confirmation that it holds consent from every person shown (see BRIEF.md, section 11).

Files were resized and recompressed (progressive JPEG, quality 72 to 78). Portraits were
published as cut-outs on a transparent ground; they were placed on a plain warm grey
(#E6E0D6) and cropped to 4:5. Nothing else was edited (no retouching, no background
replacement beyond that plain fill, no filters).
