How we work

Why we practise this way

Polwarth Dental Clinic has looked after people in this part of Edinburgh since 1955.

When you come in, we want to get to know you first: what worries you, and what you’d like from your teeth and your smile.

We believe that when you understand your own dental health, you feel calmer and can choose the treatment that’s right for you. So we explain what we find, show you photos when they help, and give you a written plan and estimate before anything starts.

And because your time is precious, we try hard to see you on time.

In pain or had an accident?

When we’re open

Phone us on 0131 229 8143 as early in the day as you can. We offer same-day emergency appointments, Monday to Friday, to our patients and to visitors. If you’re not registered with us, we take a deposit to book the appointment. It comes off the cost of your treatment.

When we’re closed

Registered patients: phone the practice and follow the answerphone message to reach our on-call dentist. There’s a call-out fee of £135. Members of our plan can claim most of it back.

Everyone else: phone NHS 24 on 111.

A life-threatening emergency

Phone 999.
