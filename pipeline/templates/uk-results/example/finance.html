The Finesse Dental Plan

A fixed monthly payment by Direct Debit, run for us by Practice Plan. Your dentist or hygienist advises which plan suits your dental health.

  • Hygiene Plan

    from £12.00 a month

    For regular cleaning by a dental professional. Good if you are quitting smoking or have had an implant.

  • Standard Plan

    from £18.50 a month

    Your check-ups and hygiene visits, X-rays and preventive advice. The leaflet adds a 10% discount on many treatments.

  • Premium Plan

    from £19.50 a month

    Check-ups and hygiene visits, plus routine treatment such as fillings, extractions and root canal treatment.

What the Premium Plan covers

  • Check-ups and hygiene visits, as clinically needed
  • X-rays, fillings and extractions
  • Root canal treatment
  • Crowns, dentures and bridgework, not including lab fees

What no plan covers

  • Orthodontics, implants and cosmetic treatment
  • Lab fees and prescriptions
  • Specialist referrals and treatment elsewhere
  • Sedation, and taking out wisdom teeth

How to join

  1. Phone us or call in

    Phone 0141 889 7227 and we’ll book your first assessment.

  2. Choose your plan

    We make sure your teeth are healthy, then agree the plan that suits your needs and budget.

  3. Sign up

    We go through the application and contract with you, including anything the plan doesn’t cover. You get a welcome letter or email.

Good to know

  • A one-off fee of £9.50 is added to your first payment.
  • All plans include access to the Worldwide Dental Emergency Assistance Scheme, which can help after a dental injury or emergency. It decides each request case by case.
  • Plan members can phone the out-of-hours line on 0808 169 8117.

NHS

What NHS treatment costs in Scotland

The Scottish Government sets NHS dental charges. They are the same at every NHS dentist in Scotland. Phone us to check whether we can see you on the NHS.

Free

Check-ups for everyone

NHS dental examinations are free for everyone in Scotland.

Free

All NHS treatment if you are:

  • under 26
  • pregnant, or gave birth in the last 12 months
  • exempt because of your income or certain benefits

80%

Everyone else pays 80% of the cost

You never pay more than £384 for one course of NHS treatment.
