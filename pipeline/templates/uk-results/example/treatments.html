Cosmetic treatments

Porcelain veneers

From £350

A thin layer of porcelain fitted over the front of a tooth, like a false fingernail fits over a nail. Veneers can be made from composite, porcelain or ceramic such as Emax.

What it can help with
  • A discoloured or stained tooth
  • A chipped tooth
  • Small gaps, when braces are not suitable
  • A tooth slightly out of line
What happens
  1. We remove a tiny amount of the shiny outer surface of the tooth. You may not need an injection.
  2. We take a mould and match the shade to your other teeth.
  3. At your second visit, we show you the veneer on your tooth, then bond it in place.
  4. We check and polish it a week or so later.
How long it takes
At least two visits. Veneers should last for many years.

Single-visit veneers (Componeers)

Ask for a written estimate

Ready-made composite veneer shells, from 0.3mm thin. They combine a white filling with the look of a lab-made veneer, and are fitted in a single appointment.

What it can help with
  • Short front teeth
  • Spaces between front teeth
  • Mildly crooked teeth
  • Discoloured or misshapen teeth
What happens
  1. We prepare the tooth, removing very little of it.
  2. We shape and bond the veneer in the same visit.
How long it takes
One visit.

Teeth whitening

£150 one arch · £300 both

Take-home whitening lightens the natural colour of your teeth without removing any of the tooth surface. We use Optident whitening gels.

What it can help with
  • Staining from tea, coffee, red wine or smoking
  • Staining from some antibiotics or tiny cracks in the teeth
What happens
  1. We check your teeth and gums for decay, chips and gum health.
  2. We record your shade, take photos and take moulds of your teeth.
  3. A laboratory makes your custom trays.
  4. You wear the trays with gel at home, by day for 60 to 90 minutes or overnight, for about 2 weeks.
How long it takes
Trays take 5 to 7 days to make. The whole treatment usually takes 3 to 4 weeks.

Repairing and replacing teeth

White fillings

From £95

Tooth-coloured fillings, matched to the shade of your own teeth. They bond to the tooth, so less of it needs to be drilled away.

What it can help with
  • Decay
  • A chipped or broken tooth
  • Replacing old silver fillings
What happens
  1. We remove the decay and prepare the tooth.
  2. We bond the filling in place and set it with a light.
How long it takes
Usually one visit. On the NHS, back teeth get metal fillings; you can choose a white filling privately.

Crowns

Ceramic crowns from £300

A crown, or cap, covers a tooth that has lost a lot of its structure. All-ceramic crowns, such as zirconia, are strong and look natural.

What it can help with
  • A tooth with a large or discoloured filling
  • A tooth after root canal treatment
  • Holding a bridge or denture in place
What happens
  1. We numb the tooth and shape it.
  2. We take moulds and match the shade, and fit a temporary crown.
  3. At your second visit, we fit the permanent crown.
How long it takes
At least two visits, usually 1 to 2 weeks apart.

Bridges

From £350 a unit

A bridge fills a gap with a false tooth fixed to the teeth either side. We also make single-visit fibre bridges, which need very little drilling.

What it can help with
  • One or two missing teeth, if the teeth either side are strong
What happens
  1. We prepare the teeth either side, take moulds and match the shade.
  2. We fit the permanent bridge at a second visit.
  3. A fibre bridge is built and bonded in one visit instead.
How long it takes
Two visits, 1 to 2 weeks apart. A fibre bridge takes one visit.

Root canal treatment

From £175

Removes the damaged or dead nerve inside a tooth and seals it, so you can keep the tooth instead of having it taken out.

What it can help with
  • A tooth with an infected or dying nerve
  • Pain from deep decay
What happens
  1. We numb the tooth and isolate it with a rubber sheet to keep it clean.
  2. We clean and shape the root canals.
  3. We fill and seal them. A crown may be needed afterwards.
How long it takes
One or more visits, depending on the tooth.

Dentures

From £250

Removable false teeth in acrylic, chrome-cobalt metal, or flexible Valplast.

What it can help with
  • Several missing teeth
  • All the teeth in one jaw
What happens
  1. We take moulds and measurements.
  2. The technician makes your denture in stages, with fittings along the way.
How long it takes
A full set can take up to six visits.

Keeping your mouth healthy

Hygienist visits

From £75

Our hygienist removes plaque and tartar, polishes your teeth, and shows you how to keep them clean at home. Airflow polishing for stains is £50.

What it can help with
  • Gum disease
  • Tartar build-up
  • Staining
What happens
  1. We check your teeth and gums.
  2. We clean and polish your teeth.
  3. We show you how to brush and clean between your teeth.
How long it takes
We recommend a clean every 6 months, or as your dentist or hygienist advises. Included in all Finesse Dental Plans.

Biteguards and mouthguards

Splints from £100

A biteguard protects your teeth if you grind them at night. A custom mouthguard protects your teeth and gums during contact sport.

What it can help with
  • Teeth grinding
  • Worn or cracked teeth
  • Sports such as rugby, hockey and football
What happens
  1. We take a mould of your teeth.
  2. We fit your custom guard.
How long it takes
We fit your guard once the laboratory has made it.

Childsmile

Free on the NHS

We are a Childsmile practice. Children are seen from six months old for advice, check-ups and fluoride varnish.

What it can help with
  • Healthy teeth from the start
  • Advice on weaning, diet, teething and brushing
What happens
  1. Phone us to book your child’s appointment.
  2. We see your child regularly for check-ups.
  3. From two years old, we apply fluoride varnish twice a year.
How long it takes
Regular visits throughout childhood.
