Our rooms

The nursery is in the farm’s old dairy building.

  • Playrooms

    2 to 5 years

    Three playrooms in the old dairy. Children move freely between indoors and outdoors.

  • Outdoor playroom

    All ages

    A large outdoor playroom with its own toilets, next to the main building.

  • Baby room

    Up to 2 years

    For our youngest children. Phone to check for places.

  • Farmhouse kitchen

    For baking

    Children bake in the farmhouse kitchen. Ask us about it when you visit.

Ask us which room your child would be in, and about staff ratios.

Food

Children grow fruit and vegetables in the garden and pick apples in the orchard. Our staff prepare food we grow to serve to the children.

Parents tell us snack round the fire outside is a favourite. Ask us about meals, snacks and allergies when you visit.

Extra support

If your child needs extra help, talk to us. Our team includes a nurture nurse, Ruth, who supports children, families and staff.

Our story

Mrs Wilkie opened the nursery in August 1991, on her family farm. Her daughter Kimberley now helps run it.

Our motto: Be happy. Create friendships. Love nature. Stay safe.
