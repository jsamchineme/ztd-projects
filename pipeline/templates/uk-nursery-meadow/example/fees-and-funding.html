Funded hours for 3 and 4 year olds

In Scotland, every 3 and 4 year old can get up to 1,140 hours of funded childcare a year. That’s about 30 hours a week in term time. It’s free to you.

We work in partnership with South Lanarkshire Council to offer funded hours here. Phone us to check how you can use them at Bardykes.

  1. Choose your days with us

    Tell us when you’d like your child to come.

  2. Apply to the council

    Fill in South Lanarkshire Council’s funding application. We can give you the form.

  3. Check the details with us

    Phone us to check how funded hours and any paid hours fit together.

Paying your fees

  • Pay monthly, in advance, on the 1st of each month.
  • Fees stay the same during holidays, and there are no refunds for sickness or absence.
  • Give 1 month’s notice to leave, or to change or cut your sessions.
  • If you’re finding it hard to pay, talk to the nursery manager.
