What do the letters after their names mean?
MB ChB, MBChB, MBBS, BMBS
Medical degrees: bachelor of medicine and surgery.
MB BCh BAO
A medical degree from Ireland: medicine, surgery and obstetrics.
MRCGP, nMRCGP
Member of the Royal College of General Practitioners. It means they passed the GP exams.
DRCOG
Diploma of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (pregnancy and women's health).
DFFP
Diploma of the Faculty of Family Planning (contraception).
MRCS, MRCS Ed, MRCS RCSEd
Member of the Royal College of Surgeons (Ed and RCSEd mean of Edinburgh).
DCH
Diploma in Child Health.
BSc (Hons), BMedSci
Science degrees taken before or alongside medicine.

Questions about seeing a doctor

Can I choose which doctor I see?

Yes, you can ask for a doctor by name when you phone to book. Phone to check how soon they can see you.

Who is my named GP?

We have not published how named GPs work at our practice yet. Phone reception and they will tell you.

Can I see a male or female doctor?

Each doctor’s card on this page says if they are male or female. Tell us who you would prefer when you book.

Which days does each doctor work?

We do not publish this yet. Phone to check which days a doctor works.

Do I always need to see a GP?

Not always. When you phone, our receptionists ask what the problem is so they can direct you to the right person. That could be a nurse, pharmacist or physiotherapist.

Read about who works in a GP practice on NHS inform

What is a GP registrar?

A GP registrar is a fully qualified doctor who has worked in hospitals and is now learning to be a GP.

We are a training practice

Since 1995, a new GP registrar has joined us every year.

Some of their appointments are filmed for training. The films are kept confidential. If you do not want this, ask for the camera to be switched off.

Doctors on the West Lothian GP training scheme visit us for 2 days every 6 months. They do not see patients on their own.

Medical students sometimes sit in on our clinics.

The practice last updated its training information in 2014. Phone to check.
