# Photo shot-list: your practice, your people

A one-page brief for a practice manager with a good phone camera, or for a local photographer. Allow half a day for a team of about 40. Shoot on a quiet afternoon, or before opening.

**Golden rules**
- Photograph only real staff and your own building.
- Never photograph patients, screens, notes, prescriptions or anything with patient details, including whiteboards and labels in the background.
- No stock photos or AI images.

## 1. Before the day
- **Consent first.** Every person signs a photo consent form before their photo is used. The form says:
  - where the photo will appear (the practice website and printed leaflets);
  - for how long (while they work here, plus 3 months);
  - that they can withdraw at any time.

  Keep the forms with your GDPR records. Anyone who says no is shown as initials, and that looks deliberate, not missing.
- **A name list.** Name each person exactly as they want to be shown, with their role, qualifications (clinicians) and gender if they want it stated. Patients use this for the staff list the NHS asks for.
- **Clothing.** Wear what you wear at work: uniform, scrubs or lanyard. Avoid big logos and very busy patterns.

## 2. The shots

| # | Shot | Framing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Portrait of every person** | Head and shoulders, square. Eyes about a third of the way down. Space above the head. | Same wall, same distance and same light for everyone. This consistency is what makes the page look premium. |
| 2 | **Reception team at the desk** | Landscape, 3 to 6 people, chest up, behind or beside the desk. | This is "who answers your call". Smile at the camera. Keep the desk free of paperwork. |
| 3 | **A consulting room** | Landscape. Empty room, door open, chair and desk. | Shows patients what to expect. Check that no screen or paper shows any details. |
| 4 | **The building from the street** | Landscape. The entrance and the sign must be readable. Shoot from where patients arrive. | Morning or late-afternoon light, on a dry day. No cars across the door. |
| 5 | **The entrance and any ramp or accessible door** | Landscape, at eye level. | Supports the access information. Patients look for this. |
| 6 | **Signage and waiting area** | Landscape. Sign in focus. | Helps first-time visitors recognise the place. |
| 7 | **Optional: a whole-team group photo** | Landscape, 2 or 3 rows, everyone's face visible. | Used when someone prefers not to have a portrait. Write down names left to right, row by row. |

## 3. Portrait set-up (the shot that matters most)
- **Background:** a plain, light wall (white, pale grey or pale cream) with nothing behind the head. Stand 1m in front of the wall, so there are no hard shadows.
- **Light:** soft daylight from a window to the side and in front, or two lamps at 45 degrees. No overhead fluorescent light straight down, and no flash pointed straight at the face.
- **Camera:**
  - Hold it at eye height, 1.5 to 2m away.
  - Phone users: use the 2x lens, not portrait mode, and keep the camera upright.
  - Take 3 shots per person and pick the one with open eyes and a natural smile.
- **Pose:** shoulders turned slightly, face to the camera, relaxed, glasses on if usually worn. Be friendly but professional; no thumbs up.
- **Diversity:** photograph everyone who agrees, in every role. Don't choose people for how they look.

## 4. Files to send us
- **Originals:** full size, JPEG or HEIC, straight from the camera. Don't filter, beautify or crop them.
- **File names:** `firstname-lastname.jpg` for portraits (for example `louisa-johnston.jpg`), and `reception.jpg`, `building-front.jpg` and so on for the rest.
- **Spreadsheet:** one row per file, giving the name, role, consent form signed (yes or no), and anything to avoid ("please don't use my surname").
- **Sending:** use your NHS secure file transfer, or a shared drive link. Don't email large batches.
- **What we do with them:**
  - Crop portraits square and colour-match them.
  - Save them as small WebP files: portraits about 2 to 10 KB each, the building about 40 to 110 KB. The page stays fast on a phone.
  - Never use the originals on the site.

## 5. Keeping it fresh
- **New starters:** photograph them on day one, in the same spot.
- **Leavers:** remove their photo on their last day.
- **Every 2 years:** reshoot the building, the reception photo and anyone whose look has changed a lot.
- **Each update:** tell us, and we'll update the team list and page review date.
