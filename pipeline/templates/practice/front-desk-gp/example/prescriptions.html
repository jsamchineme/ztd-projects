How to order

There are 3 ways to order. Your prescription is usually ready within 72 hours.

Online

Use our online order form.

Order a repeat prescription online

Drop-off box

Fill in the reorder slip from your last prescription. Put it in the express drop-off box at the first reception desk.

By post or in person

Post your reorder slip to us, or hand it in during office hours. Send a stamped addressed envelope if you want us to post your prescription back.

Medication reviews

If you take repeat medicines, we'll ask you to have a review at least once a year.

Your yearly review

You'll see a doctor, nurse practitioner or practice nurse. Your repeat slip will tell you when your review is due.

Book the right appointment in good time, so your next prescription is not delayed.

Help from your pharmacy

If you have a long-term condition, your community pharmacy can also help you manage your medicines.

Questions about prescriptions

What if I need a medicine that's not on my slip?

Write the name and dose of the medicine on your reorder slip. A doctor will look at your request.

What if I cannot get to the surgery and do not have a computer?

Post your reorder slip to us. If you tell us which pharmacy you use, they can collect the prescription from us for you.

Can I order by phone?

No. Phone requests are more likely to be written down wrongly. This could mean you get the wrong medicine or the wrong dose.

Who can I ask about my medicine?

Phone us on 01463 235245. You can also ask any pharmacist.
