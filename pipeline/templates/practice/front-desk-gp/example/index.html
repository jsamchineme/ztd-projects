How can we help?

  • Book an appointment

    Phone or call in from 9am to 5:30pm. Urgent? The duty doctor will call you back.

  • Order a repeat prescription

    Order online, by drop-off box or by post. Give us 72 hours.

  • Get test results

    Phone us from 2pm to 5:30pm, Monday to Friday.

  • Change or cancel an appointment

    Phone us as soon as you can, so someone else can have it.

  • Get a sick or fit note

    Off work for 7 days or less? You do not need a note from us.

  • Register with us

    If you live in our area. Bring a utility bill and photo ID.

  • Ask for a home visit

    Phone before 11am if you're housebound or too ill to come in.

    Our GPs and nurses, their qualifications and the days they work.

Sick and fit notes

You only need a note from us if you're off work for more than 7 days.

Off for 7 days or less

You do not need a note from a doctor. Fill in a self-certification form (SC2) for your employer instead.

Get the SC2 form on GOV.UK

Off for more than 7 days

Your employer can ask for medical evidence, such as a fit note. Phone us to ask about one.

Read about fit notes on NHS inform
