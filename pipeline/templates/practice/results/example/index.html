Your first visit

  1. Phone us to book

    Call 0141 889 7227. Under 16s come with a parent or guardian.

  2. Arrive 10 minutes early

    We ask about your health and medicines on a small tablet, or on paper if you prefer. We’ll help you.

  3. Your examination, about 30 minutes

    The dentist checks your teeth, gums, bite and jaw, and checks for mouth cancer.

  4. Your plan and the cost

    We talk you through your options and what each one costs, before anything starts.

Bring any dental X-rays from the last 6 months, or email them to us.

Your smile, step by step

From first visit to aftercare

How a cosmetic treatment such as veneers works with us. No surprises on cost or timing.

  1. Consultation

    We examine your teeth and gums and talk about what you’d like to change. Ask us anything.

    About 30 minutes

  2. Your plan

    We explain your options and give you a written estimate before treatment starts.

    In writing, before you decide

  3. Treatment

    Porcelain veneers take at least two visits. Single-visit veneers and fibre bridges take one.

    Usually 1 to 2 weeks between visits

  4. Aftercare

    We check and polish a new veneer a week or so after fitting, then see you for regular check-ups.

    Check-ups as your dentist advises

Dental emergency

When we’re open
Phone 0141 889 7227. We try to treat you on the same day. If we can’t finish the treatment that day, we make sure you’re out of pain before you leave.
When we’re closed: our patients
Phone the practice and listen to the answer machine. Finesse Dental Plan members can also phone the plan’s out-of-hours line on 0808 169 8117.
When we’re closed: everyone else
Phone NHS 24 on 111.
If it’s life-threatening
For example, swelling that affects your breathing. Phone 999.
