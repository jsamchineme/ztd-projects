What we get up to

Every session is spent outdoors. Here's what fills the day.

  • Mud kitchen and loose parts

    Pots, tyres, logs and crates in the walled garden. Children cook up their own ideas.

    See our spaces

  • Growing our own

    Children plant, grow and harvest food in Mr McGregor's garden.

    See how we eat

  • Bug hunting

    Our wild corner has a bug hotel, owl boxes and hedgehog homes. Children once found rare newts.

    Visit the wild corner

  • Making with nature

    Natural art, den building, and friendship bracelets made from sticky weeds.

    See a day here

  • Woodwork and weather

    Spaces to try woodwork, measure the weather and learn about how we feel.

    See a day here

  • Songs and stories

    Bookbug sessions, books to share, and singing across the site, sometimes with a guitar.

    Meet the team

Our spaces

One nursery for ages 2 to 5, spread across a walled garden and the land around it.

  • The walled garden

    Our secure base camp

    The mud kitchen, scramble nets, loose parts and our main hut all sit safely inside the walls.

  • The Pheasantry

    Our main hut

    A cosy place to shelter and warm up in winter, and a shady spot in summer.

  • Mr McGregor's garden

    Across the path

    A big space for growing vegetables, building dens and making natural art.

  • The wild corner

    Left to nature

    Home to our bug hotel, owl boxes and hedgehog homes. We explore it quietly.

A day here

Our day follows the children. This is the usual shape of it.

  1. From 8amHello at the gate

    Staff open the secure gate and welcome each child by name.

  2. Base camp play

    Children choose where to play: the mud kitchen, the nets, the hut or the trees.

  3. Out exploring

    Walks beyond base camp to spot deer, pheasants and minibeasts.

  4. Lunch together

    We eat in the polytunnel. Children serve themselves and eat at their own pace.

  5. Rest when they need it

    Hammocks, hidey holes and low beds in a shelter, for a nap or quiet time.

  6. 1pm, 4pm or 6pmHome time

    Depending on the session you choose.

Starting at Little Bugs

A big step for you both. Here's how it goes.

  1. Come to an open evening

    Every family comes to one first, to be sure a fully outdoor nursery suits you.

  2. Ask for our handbook

    Email us and our office team will send the handbook and registration form.

  3. We confirm your place

    We'll confirm your child's start date, usually within 1 to 2 weeks.

  4. Settle in at your child's pace

    We changed how children settle in after listening to parents. Ask us how it works.

Lunch, close to home

Our meals come from Wee Foragers: local, seasonal food, made with our chef, Vas.

From our blog: wild garlic pasta

The children foraged wild garlic, learned how to spot it, made pasta from scratch, then ate it together. Later the pasta maker turned up in the mud kitchen.

Some of what we eat comes from plants the children grow. Staff sit with the children at lunch, which helps keep children with allergies safe.
