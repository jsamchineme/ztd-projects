Funded hours for 3 to 5 year olds

All 3 and 4 year olds in Scotland can get funded childcare. It’s free to you.

Up to 1,140 hours a year. That’s about 30 hours a week in term time, or about 22 hours a week all year round.

We’re a funded provider for eligible 3 to 5 year olds. You apply to your local council, then tell us you’d like to use your hours here.

Our sessions run all year, so ask us how your funded hours fit the sessions you want.

Read about funded childcare on mygov.scot

Can I split my hours? Yes, you can usually split funded hours between two settings, for example a nursery and a childminder. Phone us to check how that would work here.

2 year olds

Some 2 year olds can get funded hours too.

A 2 year old may get funded hours if the family gets certain benefits, or if the child has been in care. Phone us to check if we take funded 2 year olds.

Help with costs

Ways to pay less for the hours you pay for.

  • Tax-Free Childcare

    We accept it. The government adds £2 for every £8 you pay in.

    Read about Tax-Free Childcare

  • Childcare vouchers

    We accept them, if you’re already in a voucher scheme through work.

  • Universal Credit

    You may get up to 85% of your childcare costs back.

    Read about Universal Credit and childcare

Not sure what you can get? Our team can help with funding applications. Phone 01383 279 380.
