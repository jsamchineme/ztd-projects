Worries and complaints

We want to hear about anything that worries you.

  1. Talk to us

    If you can, tell us first. Phone 01383 279 380 or email info@littlebugs.co.uk, and ask for a copy of our complaints policy.

  2. Contact the Care Inspectorate

    You can complain to the Care Inspectorate at any time. You don’t have to come to us first. Phone 0345 600 9527.

    Complain about a care service

If a child is in danger right now, phone 999.
