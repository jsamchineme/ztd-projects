# Big Table photo shot list

Big Table sells a nursery through its food and its table. The template looks complete with no photos at all (the drawn lunch table, room scenes and recipe card carry it), but 3 to 8 good food and kitchen photos make it sing. Send this list to the nursery, or take the photos at a visit with the manager's permission.

## The rules (non-negotiable)

- **No child's face, ever.** No names, name pegs, nursery logos on children's clothes, nappies, changing, bathing or sleeping. Hands, backs and empty rooms only.
- **Staff only with their own written consent.** If the cook doesn't want to be shown, shoot their hands and apron.
- **Real food from this nursery's kitchen, as served.** Never stock food, never styled food bought for the shoot, never AI-generated.
- Strip location data from every file. Record each one in `example/img/CREDITS.txt`.
- Landscape 4:3 or square, at least 1600px on the long side, daylight, no flash.

## The shots, in order of value

| # | Shot | Where it goes | Tips |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lunch on the plate, from directly above.** 2 or 3 different dishes from the real menu, on the nursery's own plates or bowls. | Homepage food section (plate frame), Food page | Shoot straight down on the table, plate filling about two thirds of the frame. A spoon beside it. Square crop. |
| 2 | **The dining table laid and empty**, before the children come in. | Homepage hero on desktop (arched frame), Food page | Low angle from the end of the table, chairs tucked in, jugs out. This replaces the drawn table only if it's genuinely lovely. |
| 3 | **The kitchen**, clean, with the cook at work if they consent. | Food page, Team page | If no consent: apron, hands on a mixing bowl, a tray coming out of the oven. |
| 4 | **Hands at work with food**: kneading dough, pouring their own water, peeling a banana, picking beans. | Food page "How we eat" | Small hands only, cropped above the wrist. No sleeves with names or logos. |
| 5 | **The kitchen garden or planting beds**, if they have one. | Our nursery, Outdoors | Morning light. Include a watering can or tools for scale. |
| 6 | **Snack time set out**: fruit bowl, crackers, cups on a tray. | Food page | From above, like a flat lay. |
| 7 | **One room per age group**, tidy and empty. | Our rooms (arched frames) | Shoot from a child's eye height, doorway to window. Portrait or 4:5 works best in the arch. |
| 8 | **The garden or outdoor room**, empty. | Our nursery, Outdoors | Wide, with the mud kitchen if they have one. |

## Nice to have

- A **"try this at home" flat lay**: the ingredients for one recipe the nursery is happy to share, with hands mixing. Only if they give us the real recipe.
- The **menu board** or the printed weekly menu on the wall.
- **Children's own food art**: a painted vegetable print, a paper plate collage, with no names visible.

## Never

- Children eating with faces visible, even "from the side".
- Children in high chairs (the chair, tray and plate are fine, cropped below the chin).
- Stock food photography, recipe-site images or supermarket packaging.
- Photos from social media without the nursery confirming they own them and have consent.
