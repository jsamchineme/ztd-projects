Big Table worked example: Sheephouse Nursery, Fauldhouse (Care Inspectorate CS2011297290)
Photo sources and decisions. Crawled from https://sheephousenursery.co.uk/ on 13 October 2026.

Every photo below was cropped so that no child's face, name or identifying feature shows.
All EXIF, location data and JPEG comments were stripped (re-encoded with Pillow, no metadata).
Sizes: WebP (quality 64 to 66) plus JPEG (quality 76).

NOT USED, and why
- sheep-logo.jpg (logo): not reused as an image. The sheep was redrawn as an inline SVG mark.
- the-sheephouse-way-2-1 / nursery-care (homepage tiles), nursery-care.jpg, nursery-care-2.jpg:
  identifiable babies and children; no useful crop without faces.
- home-page-main-photo (hero): four identifiable toddlers in waterproofs.
- home-page-out-of-school-club (both sizes), out-of-school-club-2: identifiable children,
  nursery t-shirts with the logo and phone number.
- the-sheephouse-team-1 / -2-2: a group of children with staff on a field; faces visible.
- the-sheephouse-team-2-1: two staff at a stall; context unknown and staff consent not confirmed.
- grace-up-the-tree: identifiable child, and the file name carries a child's first name.
- booking-2: two identifiable toddlers at a drum.
- sheep_location.webp: the nursery's own drive-time map. Not reused as an image; its drive
  times are typed as text on the page and the map is redrawn as an inline SVG.

DRAWINGS (no photos)
All other imagery is drawn in code for this template: the sheep mark, the lunch-table hero,
the two room scenes, the stamped award plate, the local map, doodles and kitchen icons.
No people, faces or hands are drawn.

PARENT QUOTES
Taken from the nursery's own website (the sheephouse way page). The site labels them by
child's name ("Olly and Jude's mum" and so on); the example replaces those labels with
"A mum of two Sheephouse children" and similar, because we never publish children's names.
The third quote is one sentence from a longer review.
