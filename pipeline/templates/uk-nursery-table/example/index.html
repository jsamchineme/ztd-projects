Cooked here by our own cook

Fresh each day, with seasonal food where we can. Babies get purees or finger foods.

How we eat

  • We eat together in our dining room, right next to the kitchen.
  • Even some of our smallest children serve their own lunch, pour their drinks and tidy away.
  • On sunny days we eat in the garden, or have a picnic in the forest.
  • Water and milk are on offer all day, so no one gets thirsty.

Our rooms

Five playrooms, used flexibly. Children move on when they're ready, not on a set date.

  • Babies and young toddlers

    3 months to 2 or 2½ years

    Two lovely spaces full of things to explore: fabrics, pine cones, brushes and sponges, and sand and water trays. Babies have their own private garden.

  • Toddlers and pre-school

    2 or 2½ to 5 years

    Two bright, airy rooms at the front, with doors straight out to the garden. Children choose where to play, inside or out.

Settling in, gently

Leaving your child with someone new is a big step. We'll take it at their pace.

  1. Visit together

    Look round with your child and meet the team.

  2. Tell us about them

    What they like to eat, how they nap, what comforts them.

  3. Settling-in sessions

    Phone us to ask how these work for your child's age.

  4. Your first 4 weeks

    Covered by the Sheephouse guarantee.

The Sheephouse guarantee

If you or your child doesn't settle in at Sheephouse, you can leave any time in the first 4 weeks. We'll give you your money back for unused sessions, and waive the notice period.

Come and see us

We know choosing childcare can feel daunting. So come in, have a look around and meet the team. There's no obligation.

  1. Phone or email us

    Tell us your child's age and the days you're thinking about. We'll find a time that suits you.

  2. Look round

    See the playrooms, both gardens, the Baa'rn, the kitchen and the dining room where we eat together.

  3. Ask us anything

    Fees, places, sessions, food and allergies. We'd rather talk it through than just send a price list.
