A day here

Each room follows its own routine. These are the fixed points.

Funded days run from 8am to 6pm, or 8am to 1pm and 1pm to 6pm. Book Breakfast Club to come in at 7:30am.

  1. 7:30am

    Doors open

    Breakfast is served. Staff meet you at the door.

  2. 8am

    Funded days start

    For children using funded hours.

  3. Morning

    Play, inside and out

    A morning snack, and classes on some days.

  4. Lunch

    Two courses, hot

    Older children serve themselves.

  5. 1pm

    Half days change over

    Morning children go home. Afternoon children arrive.

  6. Afternoon

    Rest, play and tea

    A sleep if they need one, then a hot ‘mighty snack’.

  7. 6pm

    Home time

    A handover about food, naps, nappies and play.

Food, all day

Breakfast, a morning snack, a two-course hot lunch and a hot ‘mighty snack’ are all in the fee.

Learning through play

We follow Scotland's national guidance, Realising the Ambition.

Babies and toddlers follow Pre-Birth to Three. From age 3, children learn through Curriculum for Excellence, the curriculum Scottish schools use.

We share what your child learns in their Learning Journey, and pass a report to their new school.

Care, sleep and handover

The small things that matter most when you're at work.

A key person

Younger children's key person feeds, changes and comforts them.

Sleep

Babies keep their routine from home. A tired child can sleep through lunch and eat when they wake.

Every pick-up

A spoken update on what they ate, their naps, nappies and what they enjoyed.

Extra support

If your child needs extra help, talk to us before they start. Chloe Watson, our depute manager, has a particular interest in this.

We work with other professionals, and share what helps with your child's next school.

Our inspection

The Care Inspectorate made an unannounced visit from 22 to 24 October 2024. The report was published on 2 December 2024.

  • 4How good is our care, play and learning?Good
  • 4How good is our setting?Good
  • 4How good is our leadership?Good
  • 5How good is our staff team?Very good

Grades run from 1 (unsatisfactory) to 6 (excellent). No complaints have been upheld since the inspection before.

Read the full report on the Care Inspectorate website

What we're improving

The inspectors asked us to improve how we record and store medicines, and to help staff extend children's learning in play. We put the medicine records right during the inspection. Staff training on play continues.
