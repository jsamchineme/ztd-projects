A day here

From breakfast to home time. Every room keeps its own routine, and babies keep theirs from home.

More about the day

  1. 7:30am

    Doors open

    Breakfast is served.

  2. Morning

    Play, inside and out

    With a morning snack.

  3. Lunch

    Two courses, hot

    Staff sit at the table with the children.

  4. 1pm

    Half days change over

    Morning children go home. Afternoon children arrive.

  5. Afternoon

    Rest, play and tea

    A sleep if they need one, then a hot ‘mighty snack’.

  6. 6pm

    Home time

    With a handover about their day.

Settling in, at your child's pace

Every child settles differently. We plan it with you.

  1. Come and look round

    See the rooms, ask anything, and take our prospectus pack home.

  2. Meet your key person

    They plan your child's settling-in time with you.

  3. Tell us about your child

    We write a personal plan together, and keep it up to date.

  4. Stay a while on day one

    You're welcome to stay for part of the first session. Bring a comforter.
