Join as a new patient

There are two ways to join. You can join on the NHS, or join our dental plan to be seen sooner.

NHS

Join on the NHS

We add new NHS patients to a waiting list. The wait is usually 2 to 3 months.

  1. Pick up a registration form

    Ask at reception, 31/33 High Street, Monday to Friday.

  2. Join the waiting list

    The wait is usually 2 to 3 months.

  3. Come for your first check-up

    The dentist checks your teeth, gums and mouth, and explains any treatment you need. NHS check-ups are free.

What NHS treatment costs in Scotland

These are the official NHS charges in Scotland. They come from NHS inform.

Free

Check-ups for everyone

NHS check-ups are free for everyone in Scotland, whatever your age.

Free

NHS treatment if you are:

  • under 26
  • pregnant, or had a baby in the last 12 months
  • on certain benefits or a low income, for example with an HC2 certificate

80%

Everyone else pays 80% of the cost

The most you pay for one course of NHS treatment is £384. An HC3 certificate pays for part of the cost.

Private treatment costs are different. Ask us for the price before you start.

Treatments

We offer NHS and private dental care. Phone us to ask about any treatment, or whether it is available on the NHS.

Fillings

White fillings
Tooth-coloured fillings that blend in with your teeth.
Amalgam fillings
Strong, silver-coloured fillings.
Inlays and onlays
Made-to-fit fillings for bigger holes, made outside the mouth and fixed in place.

Saving teeth

Root canal treatment
Clears infection from inside a tooth so you can keep it.
Gum treatment (periodontal treatment)
Treats gum disease to help keep your teeth healthy.

Repairing and replacing teeth

Crowns
A cap that covers and protects a weak or broken tooth.
Bridges
A false tooth fixed to the teeth on either side of a gap.
Dentures and repairs
Removable false teeth, and repairs to dentures you already have.
Implant restoration
Crowns, bridges or dentures fitted on to dental implants.

How your teeth look

Veneers
Thin covers fixed to the front of teeth to change their shape or colour.
Tooth whitening
Makes your teeth a lighter shade.

Children

Children’s dentistry
Check-ups and treatment for children. NHS treatment is free for everyone under 26.
Childsmile
We take part in Childsmile, the NHS Scotland programme that helps children keep their teeth healthy.
