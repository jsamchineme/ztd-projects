Treatments

What each treatment is, how long it takes and what you might notice afterwards.

From £130

Anti-wrinkle treatment

Small injections of botulinum toxin relax the muscles that cause lines. The same treatment can help with tooth grinding (bruxism) and heavy sweating.

Upper face: frown lines, forehead lines and crow’s feet.

Other areas: gummy smile, bunny lines, dimpled (orange-peel) chin, down-turned mouth, nose tip lift, neck bands, tooth grinding, cheek slimming and heavy sweating.

How long it takes
15 to 30 minutes.
When it works
It starts to work over the next 14 days. Some movement comes back after 6 to 8 weeks, and more by 12 weeks.
How often
Every 3 to 6 months, if you want to keep the effect.
You may notice
A headache or flu-like feeling for 24 hours, and small bruises where the injections went in.
From £160

Dermal fillers

A gel made of hyaluronic acid, a substance your body makes naturally and loses over time. It is injected to soften lines, shape features and replace lost fullness.

Areas often treated: lips, cheeks, jawline and jowls, chin, the lines from nose to mouth and mouth to chin, and under the eyes (tear troughs).

How long it takes
30 to 90 minutes, over 1 or 2 appointments.
How long it lasts
6 to 24 months. It depends on the area, the product and your lifestyle.
You may notice
Redness, pin-prick marks, bruising or swelling for a few days, especially on the lips. You may feel the filler for about 4 weeks.
£100 for 1ml

Injectable moisturiser

Vivacy Hydro, a gel that hydrates dull skin and softens fine lines. It is usually used under the eyes, around the mouth and on the neck.

How many sessions
A course of 2 or 3 treatments, 3 to 4 weeks apart. You can repeat it every 6 months.
Does it hurt?
It is usually comfortable. Numbing cream can be used if you need it.
You may notice
Small bumps on the skin for 48 hours.
£130

Dissolving filler

If you change your mind about dermal filler, it can be dissolved with an injection of an enzyme called hyaluronidase.

Before treatment
A consultation, and a skin test to check you’re not allergic.
How long it takes
30 to 45 minutes.
You may notice
Swelling or puffiness for 5 to 7 days, and redness for a few days.

Aftercare

How to look after your skin after treatment. Select a treatment to see what to do.

After anti-wrinkle treatment
  • For 24 hours, avoid alcohol, aspirin and exercise.
  • Don’t touch the area. That evening, you can gently use your normal cleanser and moisturiser.
  • For 7 days, avoid saunas, steam rooms, facials and very hot or cold places.
  • Small bruises are normal, and you may have a headache the next day.
  • Wear SPF 50 sun cream every day.
After dermal fillers
  • For 12 hours, don’t wear make-up or touch the area. This helps prevent infection.
  • Be gentle, and don’t massage the area.
  • For 24 hours, avoid alcohol, aspirin and exercise.
  • Avoid sunbeds and saunas, and wear SPF 50 sun cream every day.
  • Lips may be more swollen the next morning, and may look uneven. This usually settles over a few days.
After dissolving filler

The filler usually dissolves within minutes. The area, and the skin around it, may be swollen or puffy for 5 to 7 days, and red for a few days.

Tell Dr McArdle before treatment if you have ever had an allergy to bee stings or insect bites. Contact her if you notice any signs of an allergy afterwards.

After injectable moisturiser

Small bumps on the skin are normal for 48 hours. Come back for the rest of your course 3 to 4 weeks later.

Get help after dermal fillers

In the first 3 days: severe pain, or skin that looks mottled, cold or grey

Phone Dr McArdle straight away on 07483 342808.

Redness, tenderness, itching or swelling that gets worse after 3 days

Contact Dr McArdle. Phone the practice on 0131 665 2786, or her emergency number above.

If it’s life-threatening

Call 999.
