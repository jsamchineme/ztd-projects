A day at nursery

Children come for a morning, an afternoon or a full day. They come at least 2 sessions a week.

  1. 9am

    Doors open

    Come into the hall with your child and say goodbye. Children play until welcome time.

  2. 9:30am

    Welcome time

    Each child joins their key worker's group for a song, the register and a chat about the day.

  3. 10am to 10:45am

    Snack, then tooth brushing

    Milk or water, fruit, then toast, crackers or oatcakes. Children brush their teeth after snack.

  4. Midday

    Morning children go home

    Collect from 11:40am, and by midday. Afternoon children arrive at midday.

  5. 12:30pm

    Hot lunch

    You can send a packed lunch instead.

  6. 3pm

    Home time

    Wait at the playroom door. Staff hand each child over to you.

Starting at nursery

We take it slowly, so your child feels safe with us.

  1. Visit us

    Phone 07906 954441 to arrange a visit before you apply.

  2. First visit: about half an hour

    You fill in forms with the manager while your child meets their key worker.

  3. Second visit: half an hour on their own

    You leave your child with us, then come back to collect them.

  4. Third visit: one hour

    If all goes well, we agree a start date with you.

What to bring

  • Clothes that can get messy
  • Soft shoes, like gym shoes
  • A change of clothes
  • Nappies, wipes and bags, if your child uses them

Please put your child's name on their things.
