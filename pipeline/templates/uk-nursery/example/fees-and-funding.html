Funded hours for 3 and 4 year olds

We're a funded provider for East Renfrewshire Council. We have worked with the council since 1999.

£0

for sessions paid for by your child's funded hours. Lunch is included.

1,140

funded hours a year for every 3 and 4 year old in Scotland.

The 1,140 hours are set by the Scottish Government. Read about funded hours on mygov.scot

How to use your funded hours here

  1. Tell us you want to use your child's funded hours with us.

  2. We send a funding request to East Renfrewshire Council for you.

  3. If you only use us, you don't need to fill in a council application form.

Tell us early. We have to tell the council by 31 March which children will use their funding here. Phone us to check the dates for your child.

Read East Renfrewshire Council's guide to nursery places

2 year olds

Children aged 2 are welcome here. Most 2 year olds pay our session fee.

Some 2 year olds can get funded hours, for example if your family gets certain benefits, or your child has been looked after. Phone us to check if we can take a funded 2 year old.

Paying your fees

You pay one month in advance.

Invoice sent
On the 20th of the month
Fees due
On the 1st of the next month
How to pay
Online, using the details on your invoice. Or pay into the nursery account at a Bank of Scotland counter.
Leaving
Give us one month's notice

If fees are not paid by the 15th, your child can't come to nursery until they are paid. Continued non-payment may mean losing your place.

Fees are still due when your child is off. We staff the nursery for every child we expect.

Questions about fees? Phone 07906 954441 or email manager@clarkstonnursery.co.uk.

Help with costs

You may get help paying for sessions that are not funded. Ask us about paying when you visit.

  • Tax-Free Childcare

    The government adds £2 for every £8 you pay in, up to £2,000 a year per child. Ask us if we accept it.

    Check Tax-Free Childcare on GOV.UK

  • Universal Credit

    If you work and get Universal Credit, you may get back up to 85% of your childcare costs.

    Check Universal Credit help on GOV.UK
