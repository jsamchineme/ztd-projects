## At a glance
- **Page:** https://www.busybeeschildcare.co.uk/nursery/melrose. One long page per nursery, generated from a template and filled with that nursery's data.
- **The nursery:** Busy Bees Melrose, Huntlyburn Road, Melrose TD6 9BX, in the grounds of Borders General Hospital. Open Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 6:00, 52 weeks, ages 6 weeks to 5 years. Centre Director Sara Farquharson (since April 2023). It shows Google 5/5 and a daynurseries score of 10, and has a "Top 20 2026" daynurseries award.
- **Sub-pages at nursery level:**
  - `/contact/melrose` (a branching contact form).
  - `/leave-notification/melrose` (leavers form).
  - A **"Book a visit" modal** (a 4-step booking flow with a calendar).
  - A fee sheet download (`/download-fee-sheet/213`).
  - A "Watch video" modal.
  - Everything else links back to group pages (Eating Well, Bee Curious, Funding, App).
- **Overall feel:** **the best of my sites at "colourful, fun and not too text dense".** The nursery page swaps the group site's lifestyle photos for **a cartoon bee mascot** that appears in almost every section doing something (waving, holding a magnifier, carrying breakfast, reading, flying to a school house). It adds **red highlighter boxes behind single words**, a **sticky in-page tab bar**, **dashed "path" timeline cards** and **giant clock numbers**.

## Why it feels colourful and fun
- **Palette (tokens on this page):**
  - `--bb-color-primary-bb-red #E6344A` for the highlighter boxes, the hours band and the Book buttons.
  - `--bb-color-primary-bb-yellow #FECD1B` for the yellow buttons and the scroll-spy underline.
  - `--bb-color-primary-sunbeam #FEE6A6`, a **pale yellow highlighter** behind "Expertly", "taught" and "A head start".
  - `--bb-color-brand-secondary-midnight #2F597B` for the safeguarding band and the director's letter.
  - `--bb-color-light-grey #FAF6F1` (warm off-white) for card grounds.
  - Text `#242F3A` and black. Also defined: tangerine `#FF9E18`, raspberry `#A33165`, sky `#36A9E0`.
  - Facility tags use pale blue and mint tints.
- **How colour is used:**
  - **Whole-band colour twice** (the red hours band, the navy safeguarding band). Everything else is white or warm grey, with colour **in small, loud doses**: one highlighted word per heading, a yellow button, a red roundel.
  - Because the page is mostly white, the bee and the highlights pop.
- **The highlighter device:** one word of nearly every heading sits in a solid box ("Your nursery in **[Melrose]**", "A **[warm]** welcome", "Meet the amazing **[team]**", "We take **[pride]**", "Our weekly **[menu]**", "**[Funding]** and fees"). Red box with white text, or sunbeam box with black text. It uses `display:inline` with a padding and background, so it wraps cleanly.
- **Mascot and illustration:**
  - The Busy Bees bee in about 12 poses. It peeks over cards, sits on the booking pill, and illustrates each timeline slot.
  - A cartoon school house for "A head start in life".
  - The illustrations are flat vector with a soft ground shadow.
  - The mascot carries all the warmth, so **the page needs very few photos of children** (only the hero slider and the funding blob).
- **Shapes:**
  - Card radii of 15px, plus 60px and 64px for big containers and 9999px pills.
  - **Dashed 2px borders on rounded cards**, joined by dashed connector lines.
  - **Wavy section edges** on the hours and safeguarding bands.
  - A **blob photo mask** on the funding photo.
  - "Clock boxes" for the hours: white 2px outline, radius about 8px, with a small filled AM/PM tag.
  - Card shadows are flat offsets (`0 8px 0 -4px rgba(0,0,0,.05)`), a sticker-like lift rather than a blur.
- **Icons:** Font Awesome 7 duotone in the "Special things" cards (map pin, people) and the facility tags (parent room, P for parking, sensory).
- **Type:**
  - **Hero Junior** (a rounded geometric sans, licensed, self-hosted). Weight 900 for headings: 64px H1, 48 to 56px H2, 40px H3.
  - Tiny 12px/900 uppercase **kickers** above each heading ("SPECIAL THINGS ABOUT MELROSE", "OPENING TIMES", "CURRICULUM AND EDUCATION").
  - Body 15px/24px.
  - Headings mix weights inside one heading: "Arrival and **welcome**", "Morning **time**", "**Our weekly** menu".
- **Motion:**
  - The hero slider (dots).
  - Scroll-spy on the tab bar.
  - "Learn more" expanders with a chevron.
  - A `bounce` keyframe (the bee on the sticky button).
  - Button hover lifts.
- **Spacing:** very airy. 80 to 120px between sections, cards with 32px padding, and lots of white around the mascot.

## How it keeps text light
- **Truncate and expand everywhere:** facilities, the director's letter, each curriculum card and each timeline slot show 1 to 3 lines with a fade and "Learn more ⌄". **About 1,419 words exist, but under 500 are visible** before any tap.
- **The kicker, highlighted-word heading, one sentence pattern** repeats in every section, so each screen is a short heading plus a line or two.
- **Facts as objects:** clock boxes for the hours, score roundels for the reviews, tags for the facilities, cards for "Unique to Melrose".
- **Timeline instead of prose** for the day: six cards, each with a title, a time and one sentence.
- **Two-word buttons** ("Book visit", "Find us", "Fee Sheet", "Learn more").
- **In-page tabs** let a parent jump straight to Fees or Hours, so the long page doesn't feel long.

## Inner pages (nursery level)
- **Rooms:** no room pages and no room names. "Facilities" is one text block, the facility tags list Parent room, Parking and Sensory room, and the hero slider shows the rooms. This is a gap: parents can't see which room their child would be in.
- **Fees and funding:** the "Funding and fees" section on the page, a **Fee Sheet** download per nursery, and the group "Funding Eligibility" page (with Scotland, England and Wales tabs). The group fee calculator is England only, and no price shows on the page.
- **About and team:** the director's letter (first person, career story) and team photo cards with first names and job titles (including the **chef**).
- **Parents:** the App section, the leavers form, the "existing parent" branch of the contact form, and the menu link.
- **Contact and visits:** the sticky "Book a visit" pill opens the **4-step modal** (step 1 is a calendar; we didn't go further than step 1). The contact page asks one branching question first. The phone numbers differ: new parents 0330 333 8133 (central), existing parents 01896 820400 (nursery). The **desktop contact page has no side gutters**: the heading and form sit flush against the left edge (`clean-contact/sheet-desktop-1280-1.jpg`).

## Conversion
- **Book a visit is everywhere:** the header button, the **sticky bottom pill with the bee**, "Book visit →" buttons in the intro and safeguarding sections, and "Find us" in the hours band.
- **The booking flow** ("Book your visit in under 60 seconds", 4 steps, a calendar first) with a "Call Us" escape on every step.
- **The contact form branches** on "Does your child already attend?" so existing parents and new enquiries don't mix.
- **Fee Sheet and Funding Eligibility** are buttons, not links in text.
- **Proof right under the title:** Google 5/5, daynurseries 10, facility tags.

## Phone experience
- **Works:**
  - The sticky in-page tabs (sideways scroll) and the sticky bottom booking pill: everything important is one tap away.
  - The highlighted headings read well at 40px.
  - Lots of white space.
  - The bee makes long scrolling feel like a story.
  - Clock boxes for the hours are instantly readable.
  - The safeguarding claims are short.
- **Doesn't:**
  - The bottom pill and a floating orange circular widget button cover content.
  - "Special things" cards cut off at the edge.
  - Hidden "Learn more" text means a parent must tap 10+ times to read everything.
  - The Cookiebot dialog on first load (see group study).
  - The hero slider.
  - The 18-screen page.
  - The tab bar's later items ("App", "Contact") are off-screen with no scroll cue.
  - The modal booking calendar is the only route besides the phone number.

## Borrow this
1. **A highlighter word in every heading.** One word in a solid colour box (`background: var(--accent); color: #fff; padding: 0 .15em; box-decoration-break: clone`), alternating a strong colour and a pale "sunbeam" tint. It's the cheapest fun device in the study and needs no images. Check contrast for both box styles.
2. **A drawn mascot or motif instead of children's faces.** Small nurseries won't have a bee, but they can have **one simple SVG character or object** that recurs: the nursery's logo animal, a sun, a welly boot, a leaf. Pose it once per section (waving at the top, holding a spoon at lunch, asleep at nap time). Keep it as inline SVG, a few KB each, decorative (`aria-hidden`).
3. **A sticky in-page tab bar** (About · Rooms · Hours · Fees · Day · Food · Visit) with scroll-spy. It keeps a long one-page nursery site navigable. Make it real anchor links, keep it to 7 items, and fit it on a 390px phone without hidden overflow, or show a scroll cue.
4. **Hours as clock boxes:** "7:30 AM to 6:00 PM" in giant numerals on a colour band. Facts become graphics.
5. **"A day at nursery" as a dashed path of rounded cards** with times and a motif per slot, a different colour per age tab, and one sentence each.
6. **"Unique to [nursery]" cards** with only the nursery's confirmed specifics (location, long-serving staff, an award with the year). No invented claims.
7. **Facility tags under the title** ("Garden", "Parking", "Sensory room", "Funded 1140 hours"). Only show what the nursery confirms.
8. **A sticky bottom visit bar** on phones: name, hours, a "Book a visit" or "Call" button. Make sure it doesn't cover content (add bottom padding equal to its height) and doesn't trap focus.
9. **Gallery, our way:** a hero-adjacent photo set of **empty rooms and the garden**, like Melrose's, but as a 2-column grid (not a slider), with descriptive alt text ("Sensory room with soft-play blocks and a light panel") and lazy-loaded, `srcset` WebP. Add a room filter only if there are more than about 12 photos.
10. **Video, our way:** a poster-first, click-to-play inline video (not a modal), `preload="none"`, self-hosted MP4/WebM with a WebVTT captions track **and** a transcript under it. Sound only once the user presses play. For a small nursery that means the manager's 60-second welcome, filmed in an empty room after hours, with no children in frame.

## Avoid this
- Meaningless alt text ("Photo 0") and a hero slider.
- Hiding most of the page behind "Learn more". Keep the visible text short instead, and use expanders only for genuinely secondary detail.
- Too many floating layers on a phone (sticky bar, accessibility button, cookie dialog, modal close button).
- Generic group video (a group executive) on a local nursery page.
- No rooms section at all.
- The desktop contact page with no gutters.
- Two different "book now" phone numbers on one bar ("0330 333 813" is missing a digit compared with 0330 333 8133 in the same footer).
