## Why it feels colourful and fun
Thrive is **soft, not loud**. On this page the colour comes from **pastel bands, rounded white cards and circles**.

- **Palette** (computed):

  | Role | Hex |
  | --- | --- |
  | Mint | `#d8eada` (64% band), `#badecf` (card) |
  | Sand | `#e1dbd1` / `#e0dad0` |
  | Blush | `#f4d9d2` / `#f4dad3` |
  | Sage (buttons) | `#94b09e`, deep sage `#547a67` |
  | Ink (all text) | `#111921` |
  | Leaf art | `#e2aea1` on blush |

  There is no saturated colour at all except the daynurseries red badge.
- **Shapes:** a **25px radius** on everything (cards, photos, sliders), **28px pill** buttons, **50% circles** for photos (35 circles on the page), the **overlapping smaller circle** motif, a speech-bubble tail, and a `64px 0 0 64px` half-pill on some elements.
- **Line art:** thin outline leaves and sprouts in a slightly darker tint of each band. They are decorative and nature-themed, which suits a "Nature Kindergarten".
- **Type:** **Omnes Pro** (Adobe Fonts), a very rounded sans. Headings are **800 weight at 40px** in ink; body is Omnes 600 at 16px with a 22px line height. The round, heavy headings carry most of the friendliness.
- **Icons:** solid sage glyphs (globe, tree, piggy bank, pin, phone).
- **Photography:** a **real, local, professional shoot** (filenames `DSCF4169…4456` and `DJI_20251017…` show a camera and drone session on 17 October 2025). It covers aerial views of the villa, garden, playrooms, the forest area and campfire, cooking outdoors, and children reading and eating. The **room cards use stock** (a baby in a flat cap on white, a toddler with a toy train).
- **Motion:** sliders (Swiper), transitions on buttons (`transform .2s`, `all .5s`). Nothing animates on scroll.

## How it keeps text light
**It doesn't.** It is the densest page in this batch, with 4,275 words in 18 blocks. What saves it is **chunking**:
- every block is its own coloured band with one circle photo;
- **cards** for features, manager, contact and hours in the first screen, so the key facts (address, phone, hours, manager) are visible without reading;
- **"bold lead + short line"** patterns (Regular Updates / Daily Moments / Instant Notifications);
- FAQs closed in an accordion;
- carousels that hide 13 of 17 activity cards.

The long SEO text (Outdoor Discovery, Approach) sits in the middle where few will read it.

## Gallery
- **Where:** the **hero slider is the gallery**. It holds **29 photos** (2 drone shots, about 18 empty rooms and outdoor spaces, about 7 with children, mostly side-on or from behind and some identifiable, and 1 portrait). Further galleries are the activities carousel (17 cards), the food carousel (4) and the manager slider (2).
- **Layout:** one image at a time, 595×417 on desktop with a 25px radius, a single round "next" arrow, no dots, no thumbnails, no captions, no lightbox and no filter. A parent would have to click 28 times to see them all, and most never will.
- **Sizes:** WordPress `768w` derivatives with `srcset` (68 images on the page have a srcset), 67 lazy-loaded.
- **Alt text:** present but **SEO-generated and generic**, for example "Bright, welcoming childcare playroom with age-appropriate toys…" repeated with small changes, some starting "1." This is machine-written filler.
- **What the photos show:** the best-documented premises in the batch: the stone villa from the air, an acre of garden with a tree house, gravel pit, allotment, bug hotel, pond, fire pit, outdoor classroom and bird hut (all named in the text), wooden playrooms, a book corner and a cabin art nook.

## Borrow this
1. **A first-screen fact card:** the nursery's address, phone and one "Book a visit" button in a white rounded card on a pastel band, next to the nursery photo. Then an opening-times card. All the essentials before any prose.
2. **A drone or wide exterior photo as the first image** of a nursery: it shows the building, the garden and the setting at once, with no children. Only if the client has one; never stock.
3. **A circle photo with an overlapping smaller tinted circle and outline leaf art:** a soft, distinctive section motif. Apply it to room, garden and food photos.
4. **A floating "Call" plus "Arrange a visit" pair on phones:** fixed bottom, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, a 48px tap height, labels that don't clip, and hidden when the footer contact block is in view, so it never covers the same content twice.
5. **Pastel band rotation** (mint, sand, blush, white) for a calmer template. Ink text `#111921` on all of them passes AAA.
6. **A "Meet the Manager" card** with a round photo, name and role (with consent), near the top.
7. **A list of named garden features** (tree house, pond, bug hotel, fire pit, allotment) as an icon list or chips. It is concrete, fun and costs 15 words. Only list what the client confirms.
8. **A gallery under our rules:** instead of a 29-photo slider, show a **visible grid of 6 to 9 premises photos** (exterior, garden, each room, food, artwork), each with a short human caption, and a "See all photos" link to a gallery page grouped by **Rooms / Garden / Food / Art**. Use `loading="lazy"` and `srcset`, and keep alt text short and true.
9. **Video under our rules** (Thrive has none): a 30-second self-hosted garden walk ("Our acre of garden") with a poster, captions, `preload="none"` and no faces would suit a Nature Kindergarten well.

## Avoid this
- A 20-screen nursery page, with 4,275 words and the same template text as every other Thrive nursery.
- A **29-image slider as the only gallery**, with one arrow and no captions.
- Stock photos for the room cards next to a real local shoot.
- Machine-written alt text.
- **Missing Scottish regulatory facts** (Care Inspectorate grades, 1,140 funded hours) and a brochure link to another nursery.
- A long tour form that asks for the child's name and date of birth up front.
- Adobe Fonts, Mapbox, Cookiebot, reCAPTCHA and the daynurseries widget.
