# Cherubs Nurseries

Studied 13 October 2026: crawl (40 pages; the crawler wrote every page but crashed while writing SITE.md, so I worked from `site.json`), screenshots at 390px and 1280px of the homepage, two nursery pages (Kimberley and Ilkeston), Parent Hub, FAQ, Blog, Our Story, Our Curriculum, Community Life, Our Awards, Careers, Contact, Funded Hours and the Kimberley 360° tour, plus frames pulled from both videos. Scratchpad folder: `study2-a/cherubs/`, with video frames in `study2-a/video-frames/`.

## At a glance
- **Site:** https://cherubsnurseries.co.uk/
- **Who runs it:** Childcare East Midlands LLP, trading as Cherubs Nurseries. It has 12 day nurseries across Derby, Mansfield and Nottingham (Bingham, Bulwell, Edwinstowe, Executive House, Ilkeston, Kimberley, Longdale, Mapperley, Ruddington, South Normanton, Sutton-in-Ashfield, Wynndale). The founder and CEO, Susan Mills, opened the first nursery in January 1993. The central phone is 0330 055 3373. The site was built by Warm & Fuzzy on WordPress (Jupiter theme, WPBakery).
- **Positioning:** "Award-winning childcare in the East Midlands". It is all-inclusive (meals, nappies, sun cream, app, enrichment), and its approach blends Montessori, Hygge, Reggio and Forest School into an "I Can" curriculum.

## Why it feels colourful and fun
It is the calmest site in the batch: it feels **fresh** rather than playful. Its colour comes from **flat blocks of three pastel-bright colours** and a lime strip.

- **Palette** (computed):

  | Role | Hex |
  | --- | --- |
  | Teal (brand, headings, footer, buttons) | `#2d927d` |
  | Lime / chartreuse (strip, tiles, buttons) | `#d4de58` / `#d4de59` |
  | Blush pink | `#eab7be` |
  | Pale tints at 10% | `#2d927d` 10%, `#d4de58` 10% |
  | Cream band | `#f9f9ee`-ish |
  | Coral for lead sentences | `#f07b5a`-ish |

- **Shapes:** almost none. Square tiles with a 0 to 2px radius, circle photos on Curriculum and the area manager, a big circle "GO" button. The **active-tab nub** under the menu is the one playful detail.
- **Type:** **Proxima Soft** (Adobe Fonts), the rounded cousin of Proxima Nova. **Light 300 at 33px** for H2s in white or teal, giving an airy, friendly feel. Body is 18px, weight 300 to 400, with a 25px line height. Buttons are 16px, square, lime with white text (which fails contrast), and teal blocks use letter-spaced small caps ("Book a tour").
- **Icons:** on nursery pages, a row of **13 line-drawn "Key Features" icons** (sensory room curtain, garden, explorers trees, on-site chef, football, family room, nursery app, buggy store, parent parking, baby-sensory mobile, bottle, toddler, pre-schooler) on a pink band.
- **Photography:** professional shoots with natural light. **Nursery heroes are empty rooms** (Kimberley: a vaulted room with wooden toys; Curriculum: a teepee room with cones). Elsewhere it is mixed: the home "Our Story" photo is a child's face, and the Curriculum circles look like stock.
- **Motion:** almost none. Colour transitions on links, a WhatsApp button and a review "Next".

It offers drag, arrow keys, auto-rotate and full screen, and it is **of empty rooms**. In headless Chrome it rendered blank (it needs WebGL), so I couldn't screenshot it.

## Gallery
**There is no photo gallery anywhere.** Each nursery page has one hero photo, one manager photo, a 360° tour thumbnail and a food photo. The **360° tour stands in for the gallery**, and it is the safest form: rooms only, no children. Images are lazy-loaded (23 lazy on Kimberley). Alt text is mostly empty (52 of 77).

## Conversion
- On the homepage, a **postcode search** ("Find your nearest nursery and book a tour") with the phone number beside it. That is a strong, single, obvious first action.
- On nursery pages: an availability banner (phone), a "Book a tour" bar, a "Contact Cherubs Kimberley" bar, the phone in large letter-spaced type, and WhatsApp.
- Parent Hub "GO" and "Pay invoice" serve existing parents straight from the homepage. That is a good split between new and current parents.

## Phone experience
- The JUMP-to list under the hero is excellent: 5 big tap targets to the facts parents want.
- The video is small (180px) with tiny native controls.
- The WhatsApp button and the reCAPTCHA badge overlap content on the right edge.
- The lime buttons with white text are hard to read.
- `viewports.mjs` flagged the CookieYes banner overlapping headings, the reCAPTCHA badge past the edge, and the "go to top" button past the edge on desktop.

## Borrow this
1. **A "Jump to" bar on the nursery page:** 4 to 6 lime-style chips (Opening times, Where we are, What we have, Fees, Meals). On a phone they become full-width buttons right under the hero. Use real in-page anchors, with no JS needed.
2. **Facts as big single lines:** opening hours in one 28 to 32px line on a colour band, and meal times as a two-column timetable. It is light, scannable and needs no paragraphs.
3. **Fees in plain HTML rows** with a "fees include" list, **only if the client gives us the figures**. Otherwise write "Phone to check our current fees" with a call button.
4. **A key features icon grid:** one line icon and a 1 to 2 word label each (Garden, Sensory room, Own chef, Buggy store, Parent parking…). It is fun, factual and light. Show only features the client confirms.
5. **A split homepage action pair:** "Find out about a place / book a visit" (new parents) next to "Parent Hub / pay / forms" (current parents), each a big colour tile.
6. **An empty-room hero photo** on each nursery or room page. It is bright, wide and inviting, with no children.
7. **The manager's name and photo** on the nursery page, if the client consents.
8. **A 360° tour of empty rooms as the "gallery"** if the client has one made. Self-host it (Pano2VR or Marzipano export), open it from a static thumbnail on its own page (not inline), and add a plain photo list fallback for keyboard and screen-reader users.
9. **Video under our rules:** keep Cherubs' structure (a self-hosted MP4, native controls, a poster, no autoplay) but fix the rest. Use **`preload="none"`**; **a 30 to 45 second cut, not 2:24**; **720p at about 2 to 5 MB**; **rooms, garden, food and hands** instead of faces; **WebVTT captions** and a one-line text summary; and the poster as a real room photo. A staff-intro film (like the careers one) works for the team section if staff consent, with captions.

## Avoid this
- An **81 MB 1080p video with `preload="auto"`** on the homepage, and no captions.
- A wrong `poster` attribute (a page URL).
- Light-weight (300) grey body text at 18px, which is thin on a phone. White on lime `#d4de58` (about 1.5:1) fails badly.
- WhatsApp, reCAPTCHA, CookieYes, Google Maps and Adobe Fonts are all third parties.
- Stock-looking curriculum photos next to real nursery photos.
- A video that leads with close-ups of children's faces (not allowed for us).
