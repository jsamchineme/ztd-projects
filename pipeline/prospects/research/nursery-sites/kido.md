# Kido

## At a glance
- **Who:** an international nursery group (UK, US, India, China; offices in Houston, London, India, Singapore). In London there are about 23 nurseries (the fee calculator lists Barnes, Battersea, Bethnal Green, Camden, Chiswick, Clerkenwell, Crouch End, Elephant Park, Fulham, Golders Green x2, Greenwich, High Barnet, Imperial Wharf, Isleworth, Reading, Streatham, Tolworth, Tooting, Wandsworth, Wandsworth Bridge, Waterloo, Windsor). It says it was founded by a group of parents, and has served "over 15,000 families" since 2014.
- **Price position:** premium. Fee sheets are per-nursery downloads and a 5-step funding and fees calculator.
- **URL:** https://kidoschools.com/ (country chooser) and https://kidoschools.com/uk/. **Studied:** 13 Oct 2026. Crawl and screenshots in `study-1/kido/`.
- **Proof headline:** "Top 20 Nursery Groups by parents on Daynurseries.co.uk" (the site says "three years in a row" on most pages, "five years" on the Wandsworth Bridge open-day page; inconsistent), several Ofsted Outstanding settings.
- **Note on fit:** the least "design-led" of my five. It is a polished corporate template, useful mostly for its funding, calculator and per-nursery card patterns.

## How it sells
- **Promise:** "early years, reimagined"; "magical places where children play, learn, grow and thrive"; "more than school ready". Founded "for parents, by parents".
- **Tone:** polished and a bit generic, brochure language ("highest-quality", "most advanced early years programs anywhere", "thinking-based, project-based and problem-based learning for the first time").
- **Proof:** Ofsted and daynurseries scores on every card, Top 20 badge, "What Ofsted says" quote on nursery pages, Google stars, review lists.
- **Differentiators:** named programmes: Kido Languages (Spanish and French), Sports and Dance, Cultural Events, Outings, Forest School (with partners), STEM, Explorer Day (month-long projects), Inspiring Me (emotional workshops), School Readiness (workbooks and book bags), International Zoom Club (children link up with other countries). Room names Discoverers, Adventurers, Experimenters, Pathfinders. Kido Club online platform. Open 51 weeks.
- **Urgency:** "With the new childcare funding rolling out, spaces are disappearing fast." Open day dates (for example 26 Sept 2026, 10am to 2pm). A "Recommended time to apply" funding table with exact date windows.

## Conversion mechanics
- **CTAs:** "Book a tour" (navy) and "Contact Us" (orange) side by side on every nursery row and page; "Find a nursery near you" on the hero; "Download Funding Guide"; "Click here to see how much you can save" (calculator).
- **Booking:** a 30-minute tour. I did not complete the booking form. The nursery page also gives phone and email (a per-nursery enquire address).
- **Funding calculator:** "Step 1 of 5", starts with choosing a nursery. I only saw step 1 (the later fields were not checked).
- **Fees:** per-nursery fee sheet download, plus the calculator. No fees on the page.
- **Funding page:** England-only wording (15 hours, 30 hours from 9 months, Tax-Free Childcare, Universal Credit); funding stretched across 51 weeks; a table of "apply between these dates".
- **Pop-ups:** large black cookie banner (reject and accept). Social bar fixed to the right edge.

## Visual design
- **Type:** Montserrat for body and nav (400, 600, 700), Quicksand for the main page title (rounded, 600, set capitalised). Body copy 18px grey `#444`. A white hand-lettered script logo.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#00356B` (header, footer blocks, headings), burnt orange `#BD5319` (buttons, region labels), white. Wide white space.
- **Shapes:** pill buttons (25 to 30px radius), soft-corner cards with thin borders, rectangular full-bleed heroes with image overlay text.
- **Illustration:** almost none; the logo is the only hand-drawn element.
- **Photography:** close-up lifestyle portraits of children's faces (the hero is a boy covering his mouth in surprise on a teal wall), plus wide photos of nursery rooms made of timber shelving and furniture. All children identifiable.
- **Motion:** carousels with outline arrow buttons.
- **Fun:** the playful hero face and the lowercase headline. **Premium:** the clean navy, big interior photos. **Generic:** long centred paragraphs, stock-style family photo on funding, carousels everywhere, an embedded video.

## Phone experience
- **Works:** simple hamburger, a clear nursery list with an obvious Book a tour button per row, readable 18px type.
- **Doesn't:** the cookie banner hides the hero; the hero overlay text is small and low contrast on the funding page (white text over a busy photo); centred long paragraphs are tiring to read; carousels hide cards; the first screen has no tour button.

## Steal this
1. **One row per nursery with a Book a tour and a Contact button side by side** (for a Scottish single nursery: two buttons always visible in the header on phone: "Book a visit" and "Phone").
2. **A "what Ofsted says" block quoting the inspection report.** For us: quote a sentence from the Care Inspectorate report, with the date and a link, and only if it is a real quote. No children needed.
3. **A "when to apply" table with exact date windows.** Scottish councils set their own funded-place deadlines, so ours would link to the council and show "phone to check". The table format is the idea.
4. **Named programmes with a one-line description** (for example "Forest school", "Languages") only where the nursery confirms them. Show them as a grid of icon tiles rather than paragraphs.
5. **Room names as a progression** (Discoverers to Pathfinders). Let each Scottish nursery name its own rooms. Show them as four coloured cards with the age range.
6. **A fees calculator as a multi-step flow** only if the nursery gives its fee table. Otherwise offer a "Fee sheet" link.
7. **A fact list at the foot of the nursery page** (age range, languages, outdoor play, meals, hours): a small, scannable key-facts block.

## Avoid this
- A cookie wall and a social bar competing with the hero.
- Long centred paragraphs of corporate pedagogy.
- Claims of "most advanced in the world" which we must never write for a client.
- Inconsistent proof numbers across pages ("three years" vs "five years").
- England-only funding wording; Scottish funded hours differ (1,140 hours, council application).
- Embedded YouTube video on load (third-party cookies, GDPR). Use a plain image with a link if the nursery has video.
