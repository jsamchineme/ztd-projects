# Studio photo shot list: the rooms, the building and the people

**For:** the practice manager and a local interiors or architectural photographer. **Time:** one half-day, before opening or on a quiet afternoon, with no patients in the building. **Outcome:** a set of calm, consistent photographs of your own practice that let a nervous patient see exactly where they'll be, plus matching team portraits. Usable for 3 to 5 years.

This is the material the Studio website is built around. A practice with good photographs of its own rooms looks like a private clinic worth paying for. Stock photos of models can't do that, and patients can tell.

**Don't shoot:** patients (or anyone who looks like one in the chair), mouths, before-and-after set-ups, models, or a staged "smiling patient" scene. Before-and-after photos are a separate clinical process with each patient's written consent, taken in the surgery.

## Look and light

- **Daylight first.** Shoot on a bright, overcast day or with the sun off the windows. Add soft fill, never on-camera flash. All lights on, matching colour temperature.
- **Straight verticals.** A tripod at chest height (about 1.4m), a 24 to 35mm lens, camera level so walls and door frames stay upright. No fish-eye.
- **Tidy, not empty.** Clear worktops and cables, hide bins and paperwork, add fresh flowers to reception. Leave the character in: artwork, fireplaces, plants.
- **Leave space for cropping.** The website crops to arches (a semicircle on top) and 4:5 portraits, so keep the subject in the middle with space above and to the sides.
- **Edit honestly.** Exposure, colour and straightening only. No virtual staging, no sky replacement, no removing features of the building.

## Team portraits

- Same background, light and camera position for everyone, so the set matches.
- Usual uniform, plain colours, name badges fine. Glasses on if usually worn.
- Each person signs a consent form first (see below). Anyone who prefers not to be photographed is listed by name and role without a photo.

## Consent and rights (before the day)

1. Staff consent: each person agrees in writing to their photo, name, role, qualifications, GDC number and approved bio appearing on the website and social media, and can withdraw at any time (photo removed within 5 working days, same day on request).
2. When someone leaves, their photo comes down within 5 working days.
3. The photographer assigns or licenses the photographs to the practice for web, print and social use, with no time limit. Get this in writing.
4. Keep the forms with your GDPR records.

## Files to hand over

- Full resolution, at least 2400px on the long side, sRGB JPEG at high quality, by a file-transfer link.
- Name files by room: `reception-wide.jpg`, `surgery-2.jpg`, `front-door.jpg`, and portraits as `firstname-lastname.jpg`.
- A spreadsheet: file name, what it shows, who is in it (staff names, as they should appear), and any room names patients will hear ("Surgery 2").
- We produce web sizes: 480 and 800px wide (25 to 65 KB each), portraits at 304 × 380. The homepage stays under 800 KB on a phone.
