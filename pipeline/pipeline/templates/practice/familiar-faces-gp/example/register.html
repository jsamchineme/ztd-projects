Register with us

You can join our practice if you live in our area and will be here for more than 3 months. It is free to register.

Who can register

You can register with us if:

  • you live inside our practice boundary
  • you will be staying in the area for more than 3 months

If you register but live outside the boundary, we will remove you from our list.

Here for less than 3 months?

You may still be able to see a doctor as a temporary patient. Phone reception and ask to collect a temporary resident form.

Living in Linlithgow Care Home?

There is a separate form for residents of the Cockelroy, Avonfield and Cairnpapple units.

Not sure if your address is inside the boundary? Phone reception and they will check for you.

Read the exact boundary

The boundary starts where the A801 meets the A706. It follows the A801 to the River Avon, then the border between West Lothian and Falkirk to the A904.

It then follows the A904 and B8046 to Waterstone. From there, minor roads run west past Little Ochiltree, Wester Ochiltree, Riccarton, Hillhouse, Kipps, Craigend and Wester Woodside to the A706. It then follows the A706 back to the A801.

How to register

  1. Check you live in our area. The form asks you to confirm this first.
  2. Fill in the online form. There is one form for adults (16 and over) and one for children (under 16). Fill in one form for each person.
  3. Answer the health questions. The form asks about your medicines, past illnesses, allergies and family history.
  4. Choose your options. You can sign up for online services, choose where to collect prescriptions, and agree to texts from us.
  5. Wait to hear from us. NHS inform says the practice will tell you by phone or email if your registration is accepted.

Online services

You can ask for online access on the same form. With it, you can:

  • view and cancel appointments
  • order repeat prescriptions

You need your own email address. Shared accounts are not allowed.

What to have ready

The form asks for these details. Having them to hand makes it quicker.

  • your address and postcode when you were last registered with a GP in the UK
  • the name, address and postcode of that GP practice
  • your town and country of birth
  • your medical card, if you have one: it shows your CHI number and NHS number
  • a list of any medicines you take regularly
  • names, addresses and phone numbers of 2 next of kin or emergency contacts
  • if you have served in the British Armed Forces, your service number and dates
  • if you have come from abroad, the date you came to live in the UK

If you need an interpreter or sign language support, you can say so on the form.

Proof of who you are and where you live

NHS inform advises giving something that confirms who you are and your address. This helps match you to your NHS records.

You should not be delayed or refused if you cannot provide this. Phone us to check what we need.

Questions about registering

Do I have to pay to register?

No. Anyone in Scotland can use a GP practice without charge.

What if I live outside your area?

You will need to register with a GP practice near where you live.

Find a GP practice on NHS inform

Can I move to you from another practice nearby?

NHS inform says you can ask to move to a different practice in the same area. Phone to check if we can take you.

Will my medical records follow me?

Yes. The form asks about your last GP practice so we can get your records. If this is your first time with a GP in the UK, records from other countries will not be traced.

Can I use a paper form?

Phone reception to check.

I am moving house. What do I need to do?

Tell our receptionists your new address by phone or in writing. If you move outside our area, you will need to register with a GP practice near your new home.
