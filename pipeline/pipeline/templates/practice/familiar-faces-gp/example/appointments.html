How to book

  • Phone us on 01506 670027, Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
  • Or come to the reception desk at 288 High Street.
  • Book a separate appointment for each person who needs to be seen.

What reception will ask you

To check they are talking to the right person, they will ask for your name, address, date of birth and contact details.

They will also ask for a short outline of the problem. This helps them find the right person for you. You do not need to give lots of detail.

Types of appointment

We keep different kinds of appointment, so everyone is seen in good time.

  • Routine appointments

    Booked about 10 to 14 days ahead.

  • Appointments on the day

    Only released on the day. They are for people who are unwell and need to be seen that day.

  • Urgent appointments for today

    Available Monday to Friday, for people who need to be seen urgently. The duty doctor assesses every request first.

  • Appointments a GP asks for

    Some appointments can only be booked when a GP recommends one.

Early morning appointments, 7am to 8am

Book these in advance. The building is only open for these booked appointments until 8am. Phone lines open at 8am.

Change or cancel an appointment

If you cannot come, please tell us so someone else can have the appointment.

  • Phone us on 01506 670027.
  • Use your text reminder. It gives you the option to cancel.
  • Cancel online, if you have online access.

We send text reminders for appointments. If you do not want them, tell our reception desk.

Home visits

If you are too ill to come in, phone before 10:30am. Tell us as much as you can, so we can judge how urgent it is.

We may not be able to visit the same day if you ask for a non-urgent visit after 11am.

A home visit takes much more of a doctor’s time. If you can travel, please come in.

Questions about appointments

Can I book online?

Online access lets you view and cancel appointments, but not book them. To book, phone us or come to reception.

Find out how to get online access

Can I book for my family in one call?

Please book a separate appointment for each person who needs to be seen.

Can I see the same doctor each time?

Ask for the doctor by name when you book.

Find out about seeing the same doctor

Will my appointment be filmed?

Our GP registrar films some appointments for training. The films are kept confidential. If you do not want this, ask for the camera to be switched off.

Can I stop text reminders?

Yes. Tell our reception desk if you do not want them.
