Veneers

A thin layer of porcelain or ceramic fitted over the front of a tooth. Veneers can change the colour, shape and position of front teeth. How it works

Teeth whitening

Take-home whitening lightens the natural colour of your teeth without removing any of the tooth surface. How it works

Before
After
Case 04

Whitening of the upper and lower teeth

How it works
Custom trays made from moulds of your teeth, with whitening gel you wear at home
How long
Trays take 5 to 7 days to make. The whole treatment usually takes 3 to 4 weeks.
Cost
£150 for one arch, £300 for both

You choose daytime gel, worn for 60 to 90 minutes, or a night-time gel worn in bed. Results are much the same.
