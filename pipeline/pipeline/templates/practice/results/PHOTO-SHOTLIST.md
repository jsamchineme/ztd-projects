# Results: photo shotlist

What a practice, or a photographer we send, should shoot so a Results site looks as good as the work it shows. It doubles as a paid add-on: **"Results photo session"**, sold as a half day at the practice plus an ongoing clinical-photo routine for the team.

Everything here is of **real patients who consent and real staff**. Never stock, never AI-generated, never models presented as patients.

## 1. Before-and-after clinical photos (the heart of the template)

These are taken by the dental team as part of treatment, not by the photographer. The add-on includes a one-page routine and a 30-minute training slot so every new case is web-ready.

**Consent first.** Use a separate written consent form for website and marketing use, distinct from clinical-record consent. It names the photos, where they'll appear (website, printed leaflet), says the patient can withdraw at any time, and is kept on file. No form, no web use.

**The routine (so before and after line up in the slider):**

| Setting | Do this | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera | The same camera or phone, same lens, for before and after | Colour and distortion match |
| Distance and zoom | Fixed: mark the spot on the floor, fix the zoom or use a 100mm macro lens | Teeth are the same size in both photos |
| Framing | Upper front teeth centred, midline on the centre of the frame, 3:2 landscape | The slider crop needs no guessing |
| View | Front view, teeth slightly apart, plus a relaxed smile without retractors if the patient agrees | The smile shot is what patients relate to |
| Retractors | Clear retractors, the same type each time; cheek-tissue dry | Clean, consistent edges |
| Light | Ring flash or twin flash, same settings; no overhead light in the frame | Colour accuracy, especially for whitening |
| Shade | Hold a shade tab in frame for whitening and veneers | Honest proof that colour changed |
| Background | Black contraster card for close-ups if available | Looks deliberate in the dark lightbox |
| File | Keep the original JPEG or RAW; never edit | We crop only; "not retouched" must stay true |

**Name files** `case-[treatment]-[yyyymm]-[before|after].jpg` and log the consent reference, not the patient's name.

Aim for **2 to 3 cases per treatment** the practice wants to grow (veneers, composite bonding, whitening, bridges, implants if placed in-house).

## 2. The room as the brand (photographer)

Shoot in soft daylight, mid-morning, with lights on. Tidy every surface; remove sharps bins and paperwork from view, not the equipment that shows care.

| Shot | Notes | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waiting room, wide, from the door | The best feature in frame (a bay window, fireplace, cornice). Landscape, 3:2, at least 2400px wide | New patients section, `.place` |
| Waiting room detail | A chair, the window light, a plant: no people needed | Spare, for inner pages |
| Reception desk with the receptionist | Natural, mid-task (on the phone, greeting someone); with consent | Founder or team section |
| Each surgery, wide | Chair upright, window visible if there is one | Results page call to action |
| The building from the street | Morning light, no parked vans, signage readable | Visit section |
| The front door and step | Shows step-free access or the steps honestly | Visit section, accessibility |
| Decontamination room | Clean, a nurse at work | Trust, inner pages |

## 3. People (photographer)

| Shot | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Principal dentist at work** | Talking a patient through a plan on screen or with a mirror, the patient seen from behind or with consent. Landscape 4:3. This is the founder "why" image. |
| **Principal portrait** | Plain wall in the practice, natural light, chest up, 4:5, at least 1200px tall. Smiling, not posed with arms crossed. |
| **Every team member** | Same wall, same light, same crop (4:5, eyes on the upper third), in their normal uniform. Consent on file. Replace whenever someone joins. |
| **Team together** | One wide group shot, landscape, for the about section. |
| **A consultation moment** | Dentist and patient looking at a shade guide or a before photo together; consenting patient. |

Avoid: gloved hands holding dental models (stock cliché), mirrors held up to perfect smiles, anything that looks like a stock library.

## 4. Delivery spec

- JPEG, sRGB, long edge 2400px minimum, no filters, no skin smoothing.
- One folder per section above, file names describing the shot.
- A signed list of who appears and their consent date.
- We then resize for the web (two sizes per image, under 120 KB each) and credit every file in `img/CREDITS.txt`.

## 5. If the practice can't do a session

Results still works with: the practice's own existing clinical photos (cropped consistently), its existing team headshots, and one honest exterior. Drop the sections that would need stock (see BRIEF.md, section 7).
