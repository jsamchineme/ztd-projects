# Photo shot list: your nursery, your team

A one-page brief for a nursery manager with a good phone camera, or for a local photographer. Allow half a day for a team of about 20. Shoot before the children arrive, or on a closure day.

**Golden rules**
- Photograph only your own staff and your own rooms, garden and food.
- **No identifiable child in any photo.** That means no faces, no names, no name labels or pegs with names showing, and no children in nappies, getting changed, washing or asleep. A child seen from behind or far away, where nobody could tell who it is, is the only exception.
- No stock photos and no AI images, of people or of places.
- Tidy every surface first. Take down anything with a child's name, photo, date of birth, allergy list or medical note, including wall displays and room whiteboards.

## 1. Before the day
- **Staff consent first.** Every member of staff signs a photo consent form before their photo is used. The form says:
  - where the photo will appear (the nursery website, and printed leaflets if you want them);
  - for how long (while they work here, plus 3 months);
  - that they can say no, or withdraw later, at any time, with no effect on their job.

  Keep the forms with your GDPR records. Anyone who says no is shown with their initials, and that looks deliberate, not missing.
- **A name list.** Write each person's name exactly as they want it shown (first name only is fine), their role, their room, their qualifications, their SSSC registration, and how long they have worked here. Parents read every word of this. Only include what each person agrees to.
- **Children's artwork.** If you want to show it, use only pieces with no name on them, and ask the family first.
- **Clothing.** Staff wear what they wear every day: uniform, fleece or lanyard. Avoid big logos and busy patterns.

## 2. The shots

| # | Shot | Framing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Portrait of every member of staff** | Head and shoulders, upright (4:5). Eyes about a third of the way down, with space above the head. | Same wall, same distance and same light for everyone. This consistency is what makes the team page look premium. |
| 2 | **The manager, a little wider** | Upright, waist up, standing in a room or doorway. | For the welcome on the homepage. A real nursery wall behind, in soft light. |
| 3 | **Each room, empty and tidy** | Landscape, from the doorway at adult eye height, then one from a child's height. | One room per age group. Write down which room each photo shows: the website names them. |
| 4 | **The garden and outdoor kit** | Landscape, 3 or 4 angles, with no children in it. | Parents look closely at outdoor space. Include the mud kitchen, planting beds, shelter and the gate. |
| 5 | **Staff at work, children unidentifiable** | Landscape. Hands only: reading to a child seen from behind, planting, painting, building. | 2 or 3 shots. Check every frame for a face, a name or a reflection before you send it. |
| 6 | **Food as served** | Landscape or square, close up, on a plain table. | A real lunch and a snack. The chef can be in the shot if they have signed a consent form. |
| 7 | **The entrance, from the car park or street** | Landscape. Sign readable, door visible. | Helps grandparents and new families find you. Shoot on a dry day, with no cars across the door. |
| 8 | **Optional: the sleep room, cloakroom and changing area** | Landscape, empty, lights on. | Only if names on pegs and cots are hidden or removed. |

## 3. Portrait set-up (the shot that matters most)
- **Background:** one plain, light wall, or one real nursery wall that is calm and the same for everyone. Stand 1m in front of it, so there are no hard shadows. Nothing growing out of anyone's head.
- **Light:** soft daylight from a window to the side and in front. No overhead fluorescent light straight down, and no flash pointed straight at the face.
- **Camera:**
  - Hold it at eye height, 1.5 to 2m away, upright.
  - Phone users: use the 2x lens, not portrait mode.
  - Take 3 shots per person and keep the one with open eyes and a natural smile.
- **Pose:** shoulders turned slightly, face to the camera, relaxed, glasses on if usually worn. Warm and friendly; no props, no thumbs up.
- **Everyone:** photograph every member of staff who agrees, in every role, including the cook, the office and apprentices. Parents meet all of them.

## 4. Files to send us
- **Originals:** full size, JPEG or HEIC, straight from the camera. Don't filter, beautify or crop them.
- **File names:** `firstname.jpg` for portraits (for example `kerri.jpg`), and `room-sunshine.jpg`, `garden-1.jpg`, `lunch.jpg` and so on for the rest.
- **Spreadsheet:** one row per file, giving the name, role, room, qualifications, consent form signed (yes or no), and anything to avoid ("first name only, please").
- **Sending:** use a shared drive link. Don't email large batches.
- **What we do with them:**
  - Crop portraits to the same framing and colour-match them.
  - Save them as small WebP files, which also removes the location and camera data hidden in every photo.
  - Check every image again for a child's face or name before it goes live.
  - Never put the originals on the site.

## 5. Keeping it fresh
- **New starters:** photograph them in their first week, in the same spot.
- **Leavers:** remove their photo on their last day.
- **Consent withdrawn:** tell us the same day. We replace the photo with initials.
- **Every 2 years:** reshoot the rooms and garden, and anyone whose look has changed a lot.
- **Each update:** tell us, and we'll update the team page and its review date.
