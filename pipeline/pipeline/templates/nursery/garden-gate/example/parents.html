Kit list

What to wear and pack

Good kit keeps children warm and happy all year. Don’t let the list put you off: we can lend clothing and boots.

Every day

  • Waterproof trousers and jacket, or a waterproof suit
  • Wellies or boots
  • Fleece trousers and top
  • Wool socks
  • Spare clothes in a dry bag
  • A packed lunch and a water bottle

In winter, add

  • Thermals
  • Insulated wellies
  • A warm, waterproof hat or hood, and a snood
  • Waterproof mittens with a strap
  • Clean “lunch gloves” in the lunch box
  • A head torch for the walk down

Packing the bag

  • Gloves and water bottle in the side pockets
  • Lunch at the top: it’s the first thing your child needs in the woods
  • Waterproofs over wellies

Second-hand kit Charity shops and second-hand apps have good finds.

Food

Lunch and snack

We provide snack, often with fruit and vegetables from our raised beds. Your child brings lunch from home.

In cold months, warm and filling food is best: porridge, beans, pasta, soup or leftovers in a flask. Home baking is great too.

The woods have no bins, so reusable boxes, flasks and wax wraps are best. Everything comes home again.

Meals at funded sessions: phone to check.
