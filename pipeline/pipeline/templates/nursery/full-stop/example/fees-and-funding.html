Funded hours for 3 and 4 year olds

We're a partner provider for Aberdeen City Council. All 3 and 4 year olds can get up to 1,140 funded hours a year.

30funded hours a weekIn 38 funded weeks

10hours in a funded day8am to 6pm

How it works here

  • Your funded days are the first three days of your week.
  • A funded day is 8am to 6pm, or a half day from 8am to 1pm or 1pm to 6pm.
  • The other 12 weeks of the year are paid at our normal rates.
  • You don't have to use all 30 hours.

Splitting your hours

You can split funded hours between us and another nursery or childminder. You'll need to book at least 2 days or 4 half days with us. We'll give you split days when we have space.

How to apply

We send you the council's form each summer. Fill in Part A, sign Part B, and give it back to us with your child's birth certificate or passport. For 2026 to 2027, forms were due by Friday 10 July 2026.

Funded hours for 2 year olds

Some 2 year olds can get funded hours too, usually if the family gets certain benefits.

We accept funding for eligible 2 year olds. Ask Aberdeen City Council if your child qualifies: email eligible2@aberdeencity.gov.uk.

Other funding

We also take funding from colleges and universities. Any time it doesn't cover, such as holiday weeks, is paid by you.

Paying your fees

When
Monthly, in advance. Pay by the 6th of each month.
How
By bank transfer. Our bank details are on your invoice.
Paying late
£25 a day after the 6th.
Holidays and illness
Booked sessions are still charged. Christmas and New Year are not.
Collecting late
Charged at the hourly rate.
Changing or leaving
One month's notice in writing to leave, reduce days or change your start date.
Swapping days
Not possible. Staff are booked for your sessions.
If we have to close
For reasons outside our control, such as severe weather, fees aren't refunded.
