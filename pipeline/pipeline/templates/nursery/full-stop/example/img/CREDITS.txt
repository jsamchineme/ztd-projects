Full Stop worked example: KingsWellies Nursery, Prime Four Business Park, Kingswells, Aberdeen AB15 8PU
Photo sources and decisions. Crawled 13 October 2026 from https://www.kingswelliesnursery.com/

Every photo below is the nursery's own, from its current website. All were re-encoded with Pillow
(WebP quality 62 and JPEG quality 72, progressive), which writes no EXIF, GPS or camera metadata.
No photo shows a child or any identifiable person. No staff photos are used (no consent on record),
so team cards use initials.
