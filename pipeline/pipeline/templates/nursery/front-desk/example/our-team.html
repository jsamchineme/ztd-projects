Your child's key worker

Every child has a key worker: one member of staff who gets to know them and you.

  • Your child meets their key worker at their first settling-in visit.
  • Each morning at 9:30am, your child joins their key worker's group for welcome time.
  • In the pre-school year, key workers help children get ready for primary school.
  • You can book a chat with your key worker at parent appointments, or check in at drop-off and pick-up.

Our child protection coordinator

What the letters mean

The qualifications our staff hold, in plain words.

BA Childhood Practice

A university degree in leading and managing early years and childcare services.

HNC

Higher National Certificate. A college qualification in early education and childcare.

SVQ 3

Scottish Vocational Qualification at level 3. It is earned by showing your skills while working with children.

NNEB

The diploma of the National Nursery Examination Board. It was a common nursery nursing qualification before today's awards.

SSSC

The Scottish Social Services Council. It keeps a register of people who work in social services, including nurseries, and sets standards for their work.

Our parent committee

Parent volunteers run the nursery's business: money, fees and fundraising. They are there on behalf of every family.

We are a charity, and we can't run without them. New members join every year. Phone us to find out who is on this year's committee, or how to join it.
