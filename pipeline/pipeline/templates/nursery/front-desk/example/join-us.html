Join the waiting list

Places depend on who is leaving, so phone us to check. You can join the list from when your child is 18 months old.

  1. Ask for our registration form

    Email info@clarkstonnursery.co.uk or phone us.

  2. Fill it in

    Phone us if you have a question about any part of it.

  3. Send it back to us

    Email it to us, or post it to Clarkston Nursery, Williamwood Parish Church, Seres Road, Clarkston, G76 7PG.

  4. We'll be in touch

    When we can offer a place, we arrange your settling-in visits.

Going to another nursery too?

That's fine. If your child goes to the other nursery more often, it is their main nursery for reports and parent meetings.

Starting school

Children stay with us until they start primary school. In their pre-school year, key workers give them extra help to get ready for school.

Settling in

Your child gets to know us over 3 short visits before they start.

  1. First visit: about half an hour

    You fill in the registration, medical and consent forms with the manager. Your child meets their key worker and the other children.

  2. Second visit: half an hour on their own

    You leave your child with us and come back for them. This helps us see how they manage when you go.

  3. Third visit: one hour

    We check your child is comfortable with us. If all goes well, we agree a start date.

"Mum comes back!"

Our handbook calls coming back to collect your child "the big learning curve". It is when your child learns that you always come back.

We can't look after your child until we have their completed forms.

What to wear and bring

Please put your child's name on their clothes and belongings.

  • Clothes you don't mind getting dirty or painty
  • Clothes for the weather, as we aim to go outside every day
  • Soft shoes, like gym shoes, for indoors Leave outdoor shoes and wellies on your child's peg.
  • A change of clothes, in case of accidents
  • Nappies, wipes and bags, if your child uses them
  • Sun cream on sunny days Put it on before nursery and pack a tube. We help children top up.
  • A red sweatshirt or polo shirt, if you like Our uniform is optional. Ask us where to buy it.
  • Each child has a numbered peg in the foyer for coats and bags
