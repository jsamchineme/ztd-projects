How visits work

Come to an open evening first. It’s how we make sure a fully outdoor nursery suits your family.

  1. Book an open evening

    They’re free, and we hold at least one every term.

  2. Come and look around

    Meet the team, see the walled garden and ask us anything.

  3. Ask for our handbook

    Email us and we’ll send it with the registration form.

Why open evenings only?

We’re a small team working across three settings. So we can’t offer visits between open evenings, except in exceptional cases, at the owners’ discretion.

Can’t make the date? Phone us on 01383 279 380 and we’ll tell you when the next one is.

Questions to ask on a visit

Parents often ask us these. Here are our answers, so you can ask the next question.

How do you keep track of children outdoors?

The garden has fences all round and a secure gate that staff manage. Staff do regular head counts and use walkie-talkies. A bird-call signal brings everyone back to one spot.

What happens in cold or wet weather?

Children are outdoors all session, all year. The Pheasantry, our main hut, is a cosy place to shelter and warm up. There’s a yurt for shelter on our second site too.

Where do children rest or nap?

There are hammocks, dens and hidey holes, and a shelter with low beds for children who need a sleep.

Where are the toilets and nappy changing?

In our log cabin, which also holds the cloakroom. There are toilets and a changing area on the second site too.

Is risky play safe?

Climbing and exploring help children learn to judge risk for themselves. We check every risk and weigh it against what children gain.

What are your staff ratios?

The inspector noted a high staff-to-child ratio. Ask us for the numbers for your child’s age when you visit.

How places are offered

Places are for children aged 2 to 5.

  1. Get in touch

    Tell us about your child and the sessions you’d like. We usually reply in 1 to 2 days.

  2. Come to an open evening

    Every family comes to one before a place is offered.

  3. Register

    Fill in the registration form and send us the documents it asks for.

  4. Get your start date

    We confirm your place and start date, usually within 1 to 2 weeks.

Is there a waiting list? Phone us to check for places, how long the wait is, and how we decide who is offered a place first.

Funded hours: we’re a funded provider for eligible 3 to 5 year olds. You also need to apply to your council. Read about funded hours.

Starting at Little Bugs

A big step for you both. We go at your child’s pace.

Your child will have a key worker who gets to know them as a person. We changed how children settle in after listening to parents, so it’s calmer and goes at each child’s pace.

Ask us how the first few sessions will work for your child. We’ll agree it with you before they start.
