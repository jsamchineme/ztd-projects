How we work

What the inspector found when they spent a day with us.

  • A key worker for every child

    Someone who gets to know your child as a person.

  • Six months of induction

    New staff learn the job over six months, using Scotland’s national induction guide.

  • Singers and musicians

    Staff bring their own music to the day, and share Makaton signs with each other.

  • Familiar faces

    Cover comes from our regular supply staff and our sister nursery, so children know them.

Qualifications and ratios

Ask us about each person’s qualifications and our staff ratios when you visit. The inspector noted a high staff-to-child ratio, and that staff were recruited safely.

Staff have monthly supervision with a manager and an appraisal each year, which shapes their training.
