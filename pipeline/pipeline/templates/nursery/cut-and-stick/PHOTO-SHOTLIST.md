# Cut and Stick: photo and artwork shot list

For the nursery's own staff to shoot on a phone. Cut and Stick looks complete with **no photos at all**: stickers, paper-cut icons and colour carry it. Photos and children's artwork make it theirs. Ask for 4 to 6 photos and 3 to 6 artwork scans.

## The rules (non-negotiable)

- **No child's face, ever.** Not side-on, not blurred, not small in the background. Hands, backs of coats from a distance, and empty spaces only.
- **No names**: no name pegs, labels, drawers, coat hooks, signed paintings or name tags in shot. Fold or cover a child's name on artwork before you scan it.
- **No children** in nappies, getting changed, sleeping or toileting, even from behind.
- **Staff** only with their own written consent. Record it in `img/CREDITS.txt`.
- **Turn off location** on the phone camera. We strip metadata anyway.
- **Landscape**, in daylight, phone held level. No filters. We crop to 4:3.

## Photos (4 to 6)

| # | Shot | Why | Tips |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The making table, set up and empty** (paint pots, brushes, paper, dough) | The heart of "what we made today" | Shoot from above at an angle, before the children arrive |
| 2 | **A wall or line of children's paintings** | Belonging; the nursery's own wall | Check no names are visible. Fill the frame |
| 3 | **Hands at work**: dough, paint, soil, planting | Shows play without showing a child | Get close: hands and material only |
| 4 | **The garden or outdoor space**, empty | Parents judge the outdoors (research brief §4) | Early morning light. Include a gate, den or path |
| 5 | **The lunch table, laid or served** | Food is the most underused selling point | Real plates of that day's meal |
| 6 | **The outside of the building or the gate** | Helps parents find you; it's the "front door" | Include the sign if there is one |

Also welcome: kit on pegs (no names), a found treasure (a feather, a conker, a toad) in a hand, the book corner empty, wellies in a row.

## Children's artwork (3 to 6 scans)

- Paintings, prints and collages work best. Bold colour, big marks.
- **Scan flat** on a printer or photograph flat in daylight, straight on, filling the frame.
- **Fold back or cover the child's name** before scanning.
- Get the nursery's permission to publish children's work, and record it in `img/CREDITS.txt` (the piece, the date, "name removed").
- Save as `img/art/art-1.jpg` and so on. Use one as the hero texture or a full-bleed divider behind a solid text panel (`BRIEF.md` §6).

## Never

Stock photos, AI images, clip-art children, "children's art" from the internet, or any photo from the nursery's old website that has a child's face in it.
