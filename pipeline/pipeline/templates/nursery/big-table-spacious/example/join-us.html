Book a visit

We know choosing childcare can feel daunting. So come in, have a look around and meet the team. There's no obligation.

  1. Phone or email us

    Phone 01501 770235 or email hello@sheephousenursery.co.uk. Tell us your child's age and the days you're thinking about.

  2. Come and look round

    We'll show you the playrooms, both gardens, the Baa'rn, the kitchen and the dining room.

  3. Ask us anything

    Fees, places, sessions, food and allergies. Rather than just send you our fees, we'd like you to see what we do first.

Visit timesPhone us to arrange a time that suits you. If you'd like to see lunch being served, ask us when to come.
Questions you might ask on a visit
  • Who will look after my child each day?
  • How do you settle children of my child's age?
  • What's on the menu this week, and how do you plan around allergies?
  • How often do children go outside, and to the forest?
  • What are your staff ratios?
  • What do your fees include, and how do funded hours work here?

Settling in

Entrusting your child to someone new is not something to take lightly. We know.

  1. Visit together

    Look round with your child and meet the team.

  2. Tell us about them

    What they like to eat, how they nap, and what comforts them.

  3. Settling-in sessions

    Phone us to ask how these work for your child's age.

  4. Your first 4 weeks

    Covered by the Sheephouse guarantee.

The Sheephouse guaranteeIf you or your child doesn't settle in at Sheephouse, you can leave any time in the first 4 weeks. We'll give you your money back for unused sessions, and waive the notice period.
For babiesTwo cosy baby rooms with their own private garden, and lots of cuddles, make for a gentle move from home to nursery.
