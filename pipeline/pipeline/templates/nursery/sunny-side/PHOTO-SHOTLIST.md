# Sunny Side: photo shot list

Sunny Side works with **no photos at all**: the emblem, the sky and the petal colours carry it. Three good photos lift it a lot. This list is for the nursery, or for whoever visits with a phone camera.

## The rules first
- **No identifiable children, ever.** No faces, no names, no name pegs or labels, no nappies, changing, bathing or sleeping. Hands at work and children far away from behind are allowed only if the nursery has a consent process for website use. When in doubt, leave them out.
- **Staff** only with each person's own written consent, and only named if they agree.
- **Real things only.** No stock, no AI images, no photos borrowed from another nursery.
- Strip metadata (EXIF and location) from every file before it goes on the site.

## How to shoot
- Phone camera, landscape (4:3), the main lens (not the wide angle, which bends walls).
- Daylight. Lights on. Blinds open. Shoot from a child's height (crouch to about 1 metre) for rooms.
- Tidy first: clear the tables, hide the bins, the paperwork and the nappy shelves. Take down anything with a child's name on it.
- Take each shot three times, a little left and right. We pick the best.

## The list, in order of value

| # | Shot | Used for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The garden** in sunshine, wide, from a corner | The homepage living photo (intent section) and Our nursery | The most important photo. Flowers, planters, the mud kitchen, the shed. A 10-second steady clip of the empty garden (trees moving) makes the best living photo. |
| 2 | **The front of the building** with the sign, from across the road or car park | Tour poster, Gallery, Visit and join | Blue sky if possible. Include the door. |
| 3 | **The front door** close up, open | Tour film start | Sunflower-style: the door is the welcome. |
| 4 | **Each playroom, empty**, from the doorway | Our nursery, Gallery, tour | One per room. Sun through the window is ideal. |
| 5 | **Food as served**: one plate, or the snack table laid out | Food section, Gallery (Food) | From above or at a 45° angle. Real food on a normal day. |
| 6 | **Children's artwork** on the wall, no names | Gallery (Artwork) | Crop out names. |
| 7 | **Close-ups of things to do**: the reading corner, sand tray, paint pots, a puzzle | Gallery | Hands at work only with consent. |
| 8 | **The sign or the way in** from the road | Visit and join, "Find us" | Helps parents spot it on the first visit. |
| 9 | **Staff portraits** (optional) | Our team | Plain wall, head and shoulders, smiling, uniform on. Written consent per person. |

## Video (optional)
- **Living photo:** 6 to 10 seconds, camera still on a ledge or tripod, the empty garden or sunlight crossing an empty room. No sound needed. We re-encode it under 1.5 MB.
- **Tour film "A morning at [name]":** a slow walk from the front door through the empty rooms to the garden, before children arrive. 40 to 60 seconds. Hold the phone level and walk slowly. We add captions.
- If there's no footage, we make an honest "slow pan across one photo" or a photo film, and label it as such on the page.
