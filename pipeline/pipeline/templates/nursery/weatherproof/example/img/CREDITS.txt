Weatherproof worked example: Earthtime's Forest School Nursery, Duffus, Elgin
Image sources and decisions. Checked 13 October 2026.

Every photo below comes from the nursery's own website, earthtime.co.uk
(WordPress media library), downloaded 13 October 2026. Each was cropped,
resized to the widths shown, saved as WebP and JPEG, and re-encoded with
no EXIF, GPS or other metadata. None shows a child.
