A day here, food and keeping safe

Our four rules

They’re carved on the signpost, and every child here can tell you them.

  • We take care of ourselves

    Being independent, knowing ourselves, and feeling well.

  • We take care of each other

    Friendships, kindness and community.

  • We take care of the place we are in

    Respecting and looking after nature.

  • We take care of all of the things

    Valuing what we use, and the world around us.

What we get up to

Every session is spent outdoors. Here’s what fills the day.

  • Mud kitchen and loose parts

    Pots, tyres, logs and crates in the walled garden. Children cook up their own ideas.

  • Growing our own

    Children plant, grow and harvest food in Mr McGregor’s garden.

  • Bug hunting

    Our wild corner has a bug hotel, owl boxes and hedgehog homes. Children once found rare newts.

  • Making with nature

    Natural art, den building, and friendship bracelets made from sticky weeds.

  • Woodwork and weather

    Spaces to try woodwork, measure the weather and learn about how we feel.

  • Songs and stories

    Bookbug sessions, books to share, and singing across the site, sometimes with a guitar.

Our spaces

One nursery for ages 2 to 5, with all its rooms outdoors.

  • The walled garden

    Our secure base camp

    The mud kitchen, scramble nets, loose parts area and main hut, all inside the walls.

  • The Pheasantry

    Our main hut

    Somewhere cosy to shelter and get ready for more play in winter, and a shady spot in summer.

  • The log cabin

    Toilets and cloakroom

    Where children change, go to the toilet and keep their things.

  • Mr McGregor’s garden

    Across the path

    A big space for growing vegetables, building dens and making natural art.

  • The wild corner

    Left to nature

    Our bug hotel, owl boxes and hedgehog homes. We visit carefully, so we don’t disturb our guests.

  • The polytunnel

    Where we eat

    Children eat lunch here together, with staff at the table.

  • The second site

    With a yurt

    More room to roam next door, with a yurt for shelter and indoor play, and its own toilets.
