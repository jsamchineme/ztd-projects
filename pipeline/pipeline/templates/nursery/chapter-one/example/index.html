The first chapter

From hello to home time

Leaving your child for the first time is a big step. Here’s how starting at Cosy Cottage goes, one page at a time.

  1. Chapter 1

    Hello

    Phone or email us to arrange a visit.

    Come and see our three rooms and two gardens, and ask us anything.

  2. Chapter 2

    Saying yes

    Spaces are very limited, so ask early. A £100 registration fee secures a place.

    Children receiving the 1,140 funded hours don’t pay it.

  3. Chapter 3

    Settling in

    Every child is different and settles at their own pace.

    Ask us how settling-in sessions work here, and how long they usually take.

  4. Chapter 4

    The first full day

    Meals, snacks and milk are part of the day, and included in your fees.

    Most days there’s time in one of our two gardens, whatever the weather.

  5. Chapter 5

    Home time

    At home time, the team tells you about your child’s day.

    Tired, fed and full of things to tell you.

Thinking about starting?

Every family’s first chapter is different. Phone us and we’ll talk it through.

Our rooms

Three rooms, three chapters

Children move through three rooms as they grow. The Baby Room is separate, and everyone meets in the garden.

Babies to about 2 years

The Baby Room

A warm, calm room for our youngest children, set apart from the older ones. Babies explore through sensory play, at their own pace.

They still meet the older children in the garden and on outings nearby.

More about the Baby Room

About 2 to 3 years

The Butterflies Room

A lively room where toddlers learn to share, make friends and do more for themselves.

Imaginative, sensory and creative play, outdoor adventures and everyday discoveries.

More about the Butterflies Room

3 to 5 years

The Pre-School Room

Child-led play and planned learning, guided by Scotland’s Curriculum for Excellence.

Children build friendships, make choices and get ready for the step up to primary school.

More about the Pre-School Room

A week at Cosy Cottage

What fills the days

No two days are the same. These are the things that happen week in, week out.

  1. Most days

    Out in the garden

    We have two garden areas, and children play outdoors in all weathers.

  2. Every week

    Music and movement

    A music and movement class every week, for children of all ages.

  3. Regularly

    Local walks

    Regular walks around the local area.

  4. Regularly

    Trips to the library

    Regular visits to the local library to choose stories.

  5. Every day

    Meals and snacks

    Healthy meals, snacks and milk, all included in your fees.

  6. Home time

    A proper catch-up

    Staff tell you how the day went. One parent called the feedback “very thorough”.

Your first visit

The best way to know if a nursery is right for your child is to see it for yourself.
