Our rooms

Three rooms, three chapters

Every child is unique and grows at their own pace, so each room is set up for a different stage.

Babies to about 2 years

The Baby Room

A warm, calm room for our youngest children, set apart from the older ones. Babies explore through sensory play, at their own pace.

They still meet the older children in the garden and on outings nearby.

About 2 to 3 years

The Butterflies Room

A lively room where toddlers learn to share, make friends and do more for themselves.

Imaginative, sensory and creative play, outdoor adventures and everyday discoveries.

3 to 5 years

The Pre-School Room

Child-led play and planned learning, guided by Scotland’s Curriculum for Excellence.

Children build friendships, make choices and get ready for the step up to primary school.

How children learn

Learning through play

We follow Scotland’s Curriculum for Excellence. It aims to help every child become a successful learner, a confident individual, an effective contributor and a responsible citizen.

It focuses on three core areas: literacy, numeracy, and health and wellbeing. Our staff plan play and activities around these, with support from the City of Edinburgh Council.

What are schemas?

Schemas are patterns young children repeat as they work out how the world works. They might move things from place to place, cover things up, fill containers, go round in circles or throw things.

Spotting a schema helps us give a child the right things to play with. We encourage children to use lots of different resources in the way that interests them.

What does play-based mean?

Children learn by doing: exploring, trying things and asking questions. Staff plan activities and set up spaces that let children follow their interests, and help them take the next step.
