# Weatherproof: photo shot list

For the nursery, or for whoever takes photos at handover. Weatherproof needs **3 to 8 photos**, all of places and kit. It is complete with none: the drawn route map, the weather tiles and the kit pegs carry the page.

## The rules, before anything else

- **No identifiable children.** No faces, no names, no name pegs or labels, no nappies, changing or sleeping. Children from behind and far away only if no child can be recognised by hair, clothing or context. If in doubt, leave it out.
- **No hi-vis close-ups.** Colour-coded waterproofs are lovely, but a child in hi-vis close up is identifiable. Shoot the waterproofs on their pegs instead.
- **Staff only with their own written consent.** Keep a copy. Record it in `img/CREDITS.txt`.
- **No stock and no AI images.** Parents are judging the real woods.
- **Strip the metadata.** Phones save GPS in every photo. Our build re-saves every image without it.

## The list, in priority order

| # | Shot | Where it's used | How to take it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The base, empty** (yurt, shelter, cabin or gathering circle) | Route stop 1, Our nursery | Landscape, eye height, from the corner so you see depth. Morning light. Tidy, but not staged. |
| 2 | **The main woodland or beach spot, empty** | Route stop 2 or 3 | Landscape. Include a path or a log seat so it reads as "a place we go", not just trees. |
| 3 | **Wellies and waterproofs on pegs** | Kit section, Visit and join | Straight on, with the whole peg rail in frame. Colour-coded groups side by side if the nursery uses them. Cover or turn name labels away. |
| 4 | **The welcome sign or gate** | Hero on desktop, "Come and see us" | Portrait (4:5). Close enough to read. This is the nursery's "secret gate" moment. |
| 5 | **The mud kitchen or a play set-up, empty** | Our nursery, A day outside | Landscape, low angle. Pans, scoops, water. |
| 6 | **Lunch outdoors (the food only)** | Food and mealtimes | From above: bowls, soup pot, bread board on a log table. No hands of identifiable children. |
| 7 | **The fire circle** (only if the nursery uses one, with its safety wording) | Our nursery | Unlit or lit, with the seating ring and the fire bucket in shot. |
| 8 | **Weather in action** (rain on the shelter, frost on the canopy, snow on the path) | Weather tiles (optional) | Any weather the nursery is proud of. Landscape. Shoot it when it happens, and keep a folder. |

Optional extras:
- **The manager** in the woods, in kit, half-length, smiling (with consent).
- **The whole team** outdoors in their kit (with everyone's consent).
- **Children from behind at a distance**, walking a path in the landscape, only where no child can be recognised.

## Crops and sizes our build makes

- Route stops and inner pages: 4:3, saved at 720px and 1200px.
- Hero and "Come and see us": 4:5, saved at 480px and 800px. The hero photo is desktop only, so phones never download it.
- Staff portraits: 1:1, saved at 200px and 400px.
- Every photo as WebP (quality about 45 to 60) plus JPEG (about 62 to 72).

## If the nursery has only a few photos

- **None:** remove `.hero__photo`, the `.stop-photo` figures and the "Come and see us" photo. The page still works: the gorse hero, the horizon, the map and the pegs are drawn.
- **One:** use it for route stop 1 (the base).
- **Two or three:** base, main woodland spot, kit on pegs.
