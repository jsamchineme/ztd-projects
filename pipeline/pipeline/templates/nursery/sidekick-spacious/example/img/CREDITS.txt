Jaybees Childcare (Lochmaben) worked example: image sources
=============================================================
Every photo below comes from the nursery's own website, jaybeeschildcare.co.uk,
crawled on 13 October 2026. Each was cropped so that no child's face is shown,
re-encoded as WebP (400px, and up to 800px where the source allowed), and saved
without any metadata (no EXIF, no location). Confirm with the nursery before
go-live that it is happy for these to be reused, and that staff consent covers
the two staff photos.
