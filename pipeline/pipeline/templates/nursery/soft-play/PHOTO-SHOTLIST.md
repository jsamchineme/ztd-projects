# Soft Play: photo shot list

Soft Play works with **no photos at all**: the foam scene, the foam-shape doors and the page bands carry it. Photos make it real. Ask the nursery for these, in this order. Every photo must be of an **empty** room or space, or of hands and backs only. **Never a child's face**, name label, peg name or uniform logo with a face.

| # | Shot | Where it goes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The play room, empty**: the soft-play room, sensory room, hall or the most playful room, wide, from a corner at child height | Homepage "Climb in and look around" (and the living photo) | The hero candidate. Landscape, at least 1600px wide. Toys out, lights on, tidy floor. |
| 2 | A 10 to 20 second clip of the same room, the camera moving slowly, nobody in it | Living photo | Phone video is fine. No sound needed. We re-encode it under 1.5 MB. |
| 3 | Each room by age (baby, toddler, preschool), wide | Our nursery, Gallery (Rooms) | One per room. Take down or angle away from wall displays with children's photos. |
| 4 | Close-ups of toys and set-ups: blocks, sand, water, paint pots, books, dressing up | Gallery (Play) | Square or portrait is fine. Hands at work are fine. |
| 5 | The garden or outdoor space, wide, plus one close-up (mud kitchen, planters, bikes) | Gallery (Garden), Our nursery | Daylight. Landscape. |
| 6 | Food as served on a plate or table | Gallery (add a Food group), Our nursery | Only if the nursery cooks or serves meals. |
| 7 | The front door or entrance from the street | Visit and join | Helps parents find it. No passers-by. |
| 8 | Staff (optional, with each person's written consent) | Our team | Head and shoulders, plain background. Without consent, Our team uses the foam shapes. |

**Before using any photo:**
- Check every wall in the background: pinned family photos and learning-journey displays often show children's faces. Crop, blur (feathered, so it doesn't look like a patch) or exclude.
- Strip metadata (EXIF, GPS). Resize to 400, 800 and 1200px WebP plus an 800px JPEG.
- Record the source of each file in `example/img/CREDITS.txt`, and what you excluded and why.
