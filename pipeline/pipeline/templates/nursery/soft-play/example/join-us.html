Is it good?

The Care Inspectorate inspected us on 1 April 2021 and published its report on 10 May 2021. It graded us Very good in all four areas. Grades run from 1 (Unsatisfactory) to 6 (Excellent).

  • Care, play and learning5, Very good
  • Setting5, Very good
  • Leadership5, Very good
  • Staff team5, Very good

Places and the waiting list

We have 96 places: 27 for babies up to 2, 25 for 2 to 3 year olds and 44 for 3 to 5 year olds.

Phone us to check if we have a place, and how to join the waiting list.

Starting and settling in

Before your child starts, we give them a key worker. Their key worker helps them settle and builds a close bond with them.

Ask us how settling-in visits work and what to bring on the first day.

Funded hours usually start the term after your child turns 3. Renfrewshire Council sets the dates.
