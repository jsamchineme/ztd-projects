How visits work

Three simple steps.

  1. Get in touch

    Phone 01224 741175 or email admin@kingswelliesnursery.com. Tell us your child's age and when you'd like to start.

  2. Pick a time

    We'll find a time that suits you. Phone to check our visit times.

  3. Look round and ask

    See the rooms and garden, and meet the team. You only pay a deposit if you decide to take a place.

How places are offered

Our rules, from our fees and terms (April 2026).

Ages
From birth until your child starts school.
Sessions
Full day 7:30am to 6pm. Morning 7:30am to 1pm. Afternoon 1pm to 6pm.
Fewest days
2 full days or 4 half days a week.
Weeks
Places are for 50 weeks a year. We don't offer term-time-only places.

£100 secures a place. We refund it when your child leaves, if you give one month's notice in writing. We keep it if your child doesn't start.

Settling in, at your child's pace

Some children settle in days. Some take longer. We go at their speed.

  1. Come and look round

    See the rooms, ask anything, and take our prospectus pack home.

  2. Meet your key person

    You'll be introduced to the team and given a key person. They plan your child's settling-in time with you.

  3. Tell us about your child

    We write a personal plan together. We review it at least every 6 months.

  4. Stay a while on day one

    You're welcome to stay for part of the first session. Bring a comforter or a favourite toy.

Moving up a room

Your child moves up by age and stage, not on a set date. They visit the new room first, and staff pass on a report.

What to bring

Meals, milk, nappies and art materials are in the fee. You bring the outdoor kit.

  • A waterproof suit or jacket
  • Wellies
  • A hat, a scarf and gloves
  • A comforter or favourite toy, if it helps
  • Your child's name on everything
